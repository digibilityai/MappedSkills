### 4. Section 1: What Is E-commerce Conversion Rate? ✅
- Definition: Percentage of website visitors who complete a purchase
- Mathematical formula block: (Number of Purchases / Total Website Visitors) × 100
- Real example: 10,000 visitors → 200 purchases = 2% conversion rate
- Note: Conversion rate should be reviewed with average order value, repeat purchases, customer acquisition cost, return rate, and profit margin

### 5. Section 2: Why Conversion Rate Matters for E-commerce ✅
- Conversion rate directly affects revenue and ad profitability
- Revenue impact example block:
  - Current: 10,000 visitors, 2% conversion, 200 orders, ₹1,500 AOV = ₹3,00,000/month
  - Improved: 10,000 visitors, 3% conversion, 300 orders, ₹1,500 AOV = ₹4,50,000/month
  - Result: ₹1,50,000 extra revenue from same traffic
- Explains why CRO is powerful

### 10. Section 6: Tactic 4 - Improve Mobile Buying Experience ✅
- 8 common mobile issues (large images, small text, CTA far down, difficult filters, hard checkout, slow load, intrusive popups, hidden proof)

### 11. Section 7: Tactic 5 - Reduce Checkout Friction ✅
- 8 common checkout problems (mandatory account, many fields, hidden shipping, limited payments, coupon distraction, no estimate, no trust, slow/unsafe)
- 8 fixes: Guest checkout, early shipping, clear payments, few fields, order summary, delivery timeline, security, clear errors

### 12. Section 8: Tactic 6 - Recover Abandoned Carts ✅
- 9 cart recovery methods: Email, WhatsApp, SMS, retargeting, limited-time offers, free shipping, benefits, social proof, stock alerts
- 6 implementation fixes: Track events, segment abandoners, reminder sequences, retargeting, avoid over-discounting, test timing

### 14. Section 10: Common E-commerce CRO Mistakes ✅

3. Using Weak Product Images - Reduces trust and buying intent
4. Ignoring Mobile UX - Mobile visitors drop off
5. Not Tracking Funnel Events - Can't improve what can't see
6. Discounting Too Quickly - Hurts margins and trains buyers to wait
