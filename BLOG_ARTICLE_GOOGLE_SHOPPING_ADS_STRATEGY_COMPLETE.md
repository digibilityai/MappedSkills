7. By Campaign Objective - Sales, remarketing, acquisition, seasonal

- Conversion Value: Total revenue
- ROAS: Revenue per rupee spent
- Cost Per Purchase: Cost to get one purchase

- Product-Level Performance: Product-by-product analysis
- Note: Product-level analysis is critical

- Ad spend up but sales flat
- Product feed has errors

- Cost per purchase rising

- Product pages have low conversion
- Add-to-cart rate is low
- Checkout drop-off is high
- Revenue tracking missing
- Can't identify profitable products
