## C. Formulas & Examples - All Implemented

### Formulas (LaTeX Rendering)
1. $$\text{Google Ads ROI} = \frac{\text{Revenue} - \text{Ad Cost}}{\text{Ad Cost}} \times 100\%$$
2. $$\text{ROI} = \frac{\text{Profit from Ads} - \text{Ad Cost}}{\text{Ad Cost}} \times 100\%$$
3. $$\text{ROAS} = \frac{\text{Revenue from Ads}}{\text{Ad Spend}}$$

### Worked Examples
1. **ROI Calculation Example:**
   - Ad Spend: ₹50,000
   - Revenue: ₹2,00,000
   - Gross Profit: ₹1,00,000
   - Net Profit: ₹50,000
   - ROI: 100%

2. **ROAS Calculation Example:**
   - Revenue: ₹2,00,000
   - Ad Spend: ₹50,000
   - ROAS: 4x

3. **Lead Gen Scenario:**
   - Solar company: 100 leads at ₹500 CPL
   - 2% conversion: Wasted spend
   - 20% conversion: 20 customers × ₹50,000 = 2000% ROI

### Comparison Table
| Metric | Meaning | Best For |
|--------|---------|----------|
| ROAS | Revenue generated from ad spend | Measuring campaign efficiency |
| ROI | Profit after all costs | Understanding real profitability |
