- Definition: More than filled form; must match criteria + show intent

  - Relevant requirement
  - Right location
  - Suitable budget

  - Engagement with ad, website, or offer
- Note: Criteria varies by industry

### 8. Section 5: A Simple Lead Scoring Framework ✅
5-category framework totaling 100 points:

**Category 1: Fit Score (25 points)**
- Perfect fit: 25 | Partial fit: 15 | Poor fit: 5
- Signals: Industry, location, company size, customer type, service need

**Category 2: Intent Score (25 points)**
- High intent: 25 | Medium intent: 15 | Low intent: 5
- Signals: Specific questions, requested pricing, booked call, downloaded brochure, visited pages

**Category 3: Budget Score (20 points)**
- Budget matches: 20 | Budget unclear: 10 | Budget mismatch: 0
- Signals: Selected budget range, asked proposal, shared investment, mentioned concerns

**Category 4: Timeline Score (15 points)**
- Immediate: 15 | Within 30-60 days: 10 | Not sure: 5
- Signals: Wants urgent solution, has launch, needs callback, exploring

**Category 5: Engagement Score (15 points)**
- High engagement: 15 | Medium: 10 | Low: 5
- Signals: Opened emails, replied WhatsApp, watched video, visited again, attended webinar, detailed form

**Lead Classification:**
- 80-100: Hot Lead (priority follow-up)
- 50-79: Warm Lead (nurture and qualify)
- 25-49: Nurture Lead (long-term nurturing)
- Below 25: Low Priority Lead (not ready now)

### 10. Section 7: How to Use Lead Scoring in Marketing Campaigns ✅
5-step practical implementation guide:

**Step 1: Add Better Form Questions**
- What service interested in?
- What is budget range?
- When do you want to start?
- What is biggest challenge?
- Which city located in?

**Step 2: Track Lead Source**
- Campaign name, ad set, keyword, landing page, platform, form source

**Step 3: Mark Lead Status**
- New lead, Contacted, Qualified, Not qualified, Follow-up later, Converted, Lost

**Step 4: Review Lead Quality Weekly**
- Which campaigns produced qualified leads?
- Which ads produced poor leads?
- Which forms captured useful info?
- Which sources wasted sales time?

**Step 5: Feed Sales Insights Back Into Campaigns**
- Improve targeting, keywords, ad copy, qualification questions, landing page messaging, retargeting
