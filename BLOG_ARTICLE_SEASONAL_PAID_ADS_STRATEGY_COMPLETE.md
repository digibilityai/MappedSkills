### 12. Section 7: Step 5 - Plan Creatives for Different Funnel Stages ✅
- Concept: One creative cannot do everything
- **Top of Funnel (Awareness):**
  - Goal: Build awareness and interest
  - Ideas: Educational posts, problem-based ads, festival prep guides, short videos, founder tips, product discovery
- **Middle of Funnel (Trust):**
  - Goal: Build trust and consideration
  - Ideas: Testimonials, case studies, product benefits, comparisons, FAQs, behind-the-scenes, reviews
- **Bottom of Funnel (Action):**
  - Goal: Drive action
  - Ideas: Offer ads, urgency ads, retargeting, cart recovery, call booking, limited-time, WhatsApp enquiries

### 13. Section 8: Step 6 - Allocate Budget Properly ✅
- Concept: If all budget spent in final days, campaign lacks learning/optimization time
- **Budget Allocation Block (3 phases):**
  - Pre-Season Phase: 20-30% of budget for awareness, traffic, audience building, testing
  - Main Season Phase: 50-60% of budget for conversions, leads, purchases, enquiries
  - Final Push Phase: 20-30% of budget for retargeting, urgency, cart recovery, warm audience

### 14. Section 9: Step 7 - Use Retargeting Aggressively ✅
- Concept: Seasonal buyers compare multiple options
- 11 retargeting audiences: Website visitors, product page viewers, add-to-cart users, checkout starters, lead form openers, video viewers, Instagram engagers, Facebook engagers, email lists, WhatsApp clickers, previous customers
- 8 retargeting messages: Reminders, offer deadlines, testimonials, product benefits, cart recovery, limited stock, consultation bookings, festive bundles
- Key: Retargeting works better when audience understands offer

### 15. Section 10: Step 8 - Track Campaign Performance ✅
- Concept: Seasonal campaigns move fast; weak tracking = unknown results
- **For Lead Generation, track:**
  - Leads, qualified leads, cost per lead, cost per qualified lead, call bookings, WhatsApp clicks, lead source, lead-to-sale ratio
- **For E-commerce, track:**
  - Add to cart, checkout started, purchases, ROAS, revenue, average order value, cart abandonment, cost per purchase
- **For Awareness, track:**
  - Reach, frequency, video views, engagement, website visitors, retargeting audience size
- Important: Don't judge only by impressions/likes

### 16. Section 11: Common Seasonal Campaign Mistakes ✅
- 7 mistake cards with explanations:
  1. Starting Too Late - Less time for testing, optimization, audience building
  2. Using Generic Festive Creatives - Colors aren't strategy; message/offer must be relevant
  3. No Retargeting - Many buyers need reminders
  4. Weak Landing Page - Strong ads fail with poor page experience
  5. No Budget Split - Spending everything in final days reduces learning
  6. Not Tracking Sales/Qualified Leads - Can't know if profitable without tracking
  7. Over-Discounting - Can increase sales but may hurt margins

### 17. Section 12: When Should You Audit Your Seasonal Campaign? ✅
- 11-point audit checklist:
  - Planning ads less than 2 weeks before season
  - Offer not clear
  - No retargeting audiences
  - Landing page not ready
  - Tracking incomplete
  - Don't know budget split
  - Haven't tested creatives
  - Previous festive campaigns didn't convert
  - Ad costs rise every season
  - Don't know which campaign generated sales/leads
  - Sales team cannot handle lead follow-up properly

## G. FAQ Section - Complete ✅

All 6 FAQs implemented with full answers:

**Q1:** When should I start planning seasonal paid ads?  
A: Ideally 4-6 weeks before buying period for offer planning, creative testing, audience building, and tracking setup.

**Q2:** Which platforms are best for seasonal campaigns?  
A: Google Ads for high-intent searches; Meta Ads for awareness, retargeting, product discovery, offer-led campaigns.

**Q3:** Should I increase ad budget during festivals?  
A: Yes if offer, landing page, tracking, and audience strategy ready. Without preparation, budget increase wastes money.

**Q4:** What should I track in a seasonal campaign?  
A: Leads, qualified leads, purchases, ROAS, cost per lead, cost per purchase, add-to-cart, checkout, revenue, retargeting performance based on goal.

**Q5:** Do seasonal ads work for service businesses?  
A: Yes, if season creates relevant buyer intent (New Year planning, tax season, wedding season, school admissions, year-end business planning).

**Q6:** What is the biggest mistake in holiday advertising?  
A: Starting too late without clear offer, tested creatives, retargeting audience, landing page, or conversion tracking.
