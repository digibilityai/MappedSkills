### 3. The ROI Formula Explained
**Purpose:** Teach the exact calculation method

Includes:
- Basic formula: ROI = (Revenue - Ad Cost) / Ad Cost × 100%
- Accurate formula using profit: ROI = (Profit from Ads - Ad Cost) / Ad Cost × 100%
- **Worked example** with real numbers:
  - Ad Spend: ₹50,000
  - Revenue: ₹2,00,000
  - Gross Profit: ₹1,00,000
  - Final ROI: 100%
  - Interpretation: ₹1 spent = ₹1 extra profit

**Key Takeaway:** Use profit, not just revenue, for accurate ROI

---

### 4. What Numbers You Need to Track
**Purpose:** List the 4 essential data points

Required tracking:
1. Ad Spend (total investment)
2. Conversions (leads/sales/signups)
3. Revenue per Conversion (average value)
4. Profit Margin (what you keep after costs)

**Key Takeaway:** Missing any one metric makes ROI calculation incomplete

### 5. How to Calculate ROAS
**Purpose:** Distinguish ROAS from ROI

Explains:
- ROAS = Revenue ÷ Ad Spend
- Example: ₹2,00,000 revenue ÷ ₹50,000 spend = 4x ROAS
- Comparison table: ROAS vs ROI
  - ROAS: Revenue efficiency
  - ROI: Profitability after costs
- Key difference: ROAS can be high with low profit due to product costs

**Key Takeaway:** ROAS looks good but ROI reveals reality

---

### 6. Cost Per Lead (CPL) vs ROI
**Purpose:** Explain when each metric matters

Content:
- When CPL matters: B2B high-ticket sales
- When ROI matters: E-commerce, low margins
- Real scenario: Same CPL can be great or terrible depending on conversion rate
- Example: 100 leads at ₹500 CPL
  - 2% conversion = wasted spend
  - 20% conversion = 2000% ROI

**Key Takeaway:** CPL alone is incomplete without conversion context

## FAQ Section - 6 Questions

### Q1: What is a good Google Ads ROI?
**Answer:** Healthy ROI typically 300%+, meaning $3-4 for every $1 spent. Varies by industry (e-commerce 400%+, B2B 200-300%).

### Q2: How do I calculate Google Ads ROAS?
**Answer:** ROAS = Revenue ÷ Ad Spend. Example: $4,000 revenue ÷ $1,000 spend = 4:1 or 400%.

### Q3: Why are my Google Ads getting clicks but no leads?
**Answer:** Common causes: landing pages don't match ad copy, forms too long, keywords too broad, wrong targeting. Check Quality Score.

### Q4: How often should I review Google Ads performance?
**Answer:** Weekly for active campaigns, monthly for detailed analysis, quarterly for strategy. New campaigns need daily monitoring first 2-4 weeks.

### Q5: When should I hire a Google Ads agency?
**Answer:** Consider hiring when monthly spend exceeds $5,000, you need expertise, or campaigns aren't hitting ROI targets after optimization.

### Q6: (Additional) Is ROI better than ROAS?
**Answer:** Both useful. ROAS shows revenue efficiency, ROI shows profitability after costs. Use together for complete picture.
