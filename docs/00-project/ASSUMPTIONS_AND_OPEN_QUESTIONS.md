# Assumptions and Open Questions

## Known Project Facts
- MappedSkills has an existing production website.
- Existing technology includes Next.js and Node.
- The owner wants a world-class redesign and marketing engine.
- SEO-led customer acquisition is a core objective.
- Initial launch must include content required to begin building topical authority.
- AI visibility across relevant answer/search systems is a core program requirement.
- Claude will execute many specialist roles under orchestration.
- Existing functionality and SEO equity must be protected.

## Working Business Hypotheses
- AI + Search Visibility should be the primary spearhead service.
- CRO should be a second service/value multiplier.
- Marketing Automation should be a third service/value multiplier.
- “GET FOUND → CONVERT → AUTOMATE → GROW” may provide a simple commercial story.
- Search Visibility may offer better SEO-scalability and white space than broader digital marketing positioning.

## Research Questions
- What is current Indian and international search demand for AI search optimization, GEO, AEO, AI SEO, CRO, and marketing automation?
- How competitive are the highest-intent SERPs?
- What commercial pages are actually justified by demand?
- Which content clusters can MappedSkills realistically own?
- What terminology do buyers use versus terminology agencies use?
- Which service has the best intersection of demand, margin, recurrence, scalability, and SEO opportunity?
- How visible is MappedSkills today across Google and major AI discovery systems?
- Which competitors dominate relevant AI recommendations?
- What source patterns drive citations in major LLM/search experiences?
