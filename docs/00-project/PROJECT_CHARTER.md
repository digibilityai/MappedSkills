# Project Charter — MappedSkills Website Transformation

## Project Mission
Build a world-class MappedSkills marketing website that acts simultaneously as:
1. a customer-acquisition engine;
2. a proof-of-capability asset;
3. a content and authority platform;
4. a conversion engine;
5. a measurable demonstration of MappedSkills' own services.

## OWNER REQUIREMENTS
The website must:
- acquire customers organically through Google over time;
- include the content/blog/research architecture required to build topical authority;
- be optimized for visibility and citation potential across relevant AI search and answer systems;
- support AI visibility monitoring across systems such as ChatGPT, Google AI experiences/Gemini, Claude, Perplexity, Grok, and future relevant systems;
- follow current SEO best practices;
- target excellent Core Web Vitals;
- target high page-speed performance;
- be designed for conversion;
- include high-converting landing pages for each service;
- include complete website funnel architecture;
- implement complete measurement and event tracking;
- implement Open Graph and social sharing metadata;
- implement appropriate schema and structured data;
- follow web development best practices;
- maintain strong accessibility, security, responsive behaviour, and maintainability;
- protect existing SEO equity during redesign and migration;
- include initial SEO content at launch rather than treating content as a post-launch afterthought.

## Existing Site Constraint
**OWNER REQUIREMENT:** MappedSkills already has an existing Next.js + Node website with live pages and functionality.

The redesign must not casually alter:
- URLs;
- backend functionality;
- forms;
- APIs;
- integrations;
- SEO hierarchy;
- existing valuable content;
- tracking;
- production behaviour.

Any necessary structural change must be justified, documented, migration-safe, and approved.

## Emerging Business Hypothesis
**WORKING HYPOTHESIS:**
MappedSkills may focus on three commercial pillars:
1. AI + Search Visibility
2. Conversion Rate Optimisation
3. Marketing Automation

Potential customer journey:
**GET FOUND → CONVERT → AUTOMATE → GROW**

Potential headline territory:
**Get Found. Convert More. Automate Growth.**

Potential brand idea:
MappedSkills helps businesses become discoverable across modern search, convert more of that demand, and automate the journey after conversion.

These are not final until validated.

## Website Business Jobs
The website must eventually perform five jobs:

### 1. Generate qualified inbound demand
Through commercial landing pages, service pages, problem-led pages, topic clusters, research, tools, and authoritative content.

### 2. Establish expertise
Through clear thinking, original evidence, useful frameworks, research, experiments, methodology, and proof.

### 3. Convert visitors
Through intent-aligned journeys, persuasive page structures, trust architecture, CTAs, forms, tools, and experimentation.

### 4. Demonstrate the service
MappedSkills should demonstrate its own Search Visibility, CRO, and Automation capabilities on mapskills.com.

### 5. Create compounding authority
Research, content, tools, digital PR, backlinks, entity authority, customer proof, and AI/search citations should reinforce one another.

## Success Definition
The project is successful only when:
- the final positioning is evidence-backed;
- the information architecture supports both people and search engines;
- target service pages are persuasive and technically sound;
- the content system can scale without quality collapse;
- the website performs strongly on real devices;
- analytics can trace acquisition and conversion;
- existing SEO equity is protected;
- the website is maintainable;
- a clear post-launch SEO, AI-visibility, and CRO operating cadence exists.

## Out of Scope at Foundation Stage
At this stage, do not assume:
- final keyword targets;
- final page counts;
- final service naming;
- final ICPs;
- final pricing;
- final industry verticals;
- final content clusters;
- final AI search opportunity;
- final technology changes.

These require validation.
