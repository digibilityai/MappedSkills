## 1. Owner-approved direction

**APPROVED DECISION — recorded as `DEC-005`:**

> **MappedSkills exists to help businesses capture buyer demand and turn it into measurable qualified enquiries.**

This is a **strategic foundation, not final homepage copy or a tagline.** Later messaging work may find a sharper buyer-facing articulation; **the underlying meaning must be preserved.**

### Approved strategic principles

| Area | Approved position |
|---|---|
| **Primary outcome** | **Measurable qualified enquiries.** Revenue is measured, attributed where possible and reported — **never promised** |
| **Primary buyer** | Considered-purchase businesses where each qualified opportunity has meaningful economic value, and which already have or can reasonably access market demand. **Not for businesses that need demand invented from zero** |
| **Core spearhead** | Capture existing buyer/search demand and turn it into enquiries |
| **Search / SEO** | **Primary acquisition capability.** SEO is an implementation discipline beneath the proposition. **Not** an SEO/GEO/AEO/LLMO agency at company level; the terminology remains usable on relevant service, search-intent and educational pages |
| **AI Search** | **Important, highly visible supporting capability and differentiator** — delivery capability, methodology, diagnostic component, content-authority surface, proof mechanism. **Not the company category.** **No universal "AI Visibility Score" as the core proposition** |
| **Conversion** | **Integrated into the core delivery system**, in buyer/business language rather than the acronym "CRO". No statistically valid experimentation promised where traffic is insufficient |
| **Measurement** | **Mandatory foundation of the operating model.** discovery/source → landing experience → interaction → enquiry → qualified enquiry → opportunity → client-supplied revenue data |
| **Paid media** | **Retained as an acquisition capability; removed from company-level positioning.** Existing paid-media URLs are not removed for positioning reasons; final URL decisions belong to the IA phase |
| **Automation** | **Limited downstream expansion capability** — enquiry routing, notifications, response-time workflows, email/WhatsApp follow-up, basic qualification, simple handoff. **Not** RevOps, CRM implementation or lifecycle consulting |
| **Vertical** | **Horizontal proposition with vertical beachheads.** Manufacturing / industrial B2B is **candidate beachhead #1 — not approved exclusive specialisation** |
| **Geography** | **India-first.** International strategically possible; **no international SEO or location strategy yet** |
| **Proof** | Significant proof deficit acknowledged. Trust built through real methodology, diagnostics, audits, research, experiments, benchmarks, transparent measurement, founder expertise, process transparency, third-party authority, and permissioned client evidence. **Never fabricate proof** |
| **Rejected positioning** | Full-service digital marketing agency · generic performance marketing agency · SEO agency · GEO/AEO/LLMO agency · marketing automation agency · RevOps consultancy · AI visibility dashboard company |

## 7. Approved business architecture

**Core outcome system:** capture demand → convert it into enquiries → measure the whole chain. **One engagement, not three services.**

| Group | Capabilities |
|---|---|
| **Acquisition** | organic search · technical search · content/search demand capture · AI-search readiness · paid search/media · third-party authority and entity footprint |
| **Conversion** | landing experiences · enquiry paths · forms · calls · booking/quote/RFQ journeys · conversion research · experimentation **where volume supports significance** |
| **Measurement** | analytics · event instrumentation · attribution · qualified-enquiry tracking · CRM handoff measurement · honest reporting including what cannot be attributed |
| **Expansion** | enquiry routing · response workflows · follow-up automation · additional acquisition channels · AI-search/source-authority programme · export/international demand for Indian clients · vertical content and authority |

**Commercial ladder:** **Entry** (low-friction diagnostic of whether and where demand is being lost — **free/paid/hybrid model NOT yet chosen**) → **Core** (recurring or structured engagement improving capture, conversion and measurement against a published baseline) → **Expansion** (sold when the client feels the pain) → **Retention** (the enquiry trend, the baseline history, the compounding authority asset, the measurement layer).

**Not services, deliberately:** marketing automation as a pillar · social media management · brand identity · standalone web development · ecommerce · an AI-visibility score product · scaled location SEO · content-volume production · CRM/RevOps/lifecycle implementation.
