## 0. Orchestrator correction pass — 2026-09-01

A narrow orchestrator correction pass was applied within this same session. **The messaging architecture was not restarted, no research was performed, no scope was expanded, no page copy was written, and no gate status was changed.** Three corrections:

**1. The approved buyer definition is no longer narrowed.** The internal positioning statement opened *"For considered-purchase businesses in India whose buyers are already searching…"*, which made **being findable in search an eligibility condition for the buyer**. Corrected to the approved definition — **considered-purchase businesses where each qualified opportunity has meaningful economic value, and where demand for what they sell already exists or can reasonably be reached.** India-first orientation is retained, existing buyer demand remains central to the acquisition story, and **search remains the primary acquisition capability without being a test of whether a business belongs in the target market.** Paid media is preserved as a legitimate route to that demand. **MappedSkills is not broadened into generic demand generation** — demand capture, not demand creation, is unchanged. The same narrowing was corrected in Candidate B and in the expanded "what does MappedSkills do?" answer.

**2. Unproven engagement-model absolutes are removed.** Supporting proposition S1 read *"One supplier owns the seams where enquiries are actually lost"*, and structural differentiator 2 read *"Acquisition, conversion and measurement are one engagement"*. **The delivery process and engagement model are owner-blocked**, and the claim that the loss *is* in the seams is a hypothesis rather than a measured finding — it also contradicts the approved four-zone diagnostic, whose credibility rests on not pre-judging the cause. The simplest defensible formulation is now used throughout: **the parts are connected rather than handed off, and the joins between them are somebody's job here.** A new `POSITIONING_MESSAGE_SYSTEM.md` §3.5 records **five prohibited absolutes** — that every client buys all three, that engagements cannot be scoped, that MappedSkills replaces three suppliers, that paid media is included by default, and that any engagement structure has been approved — alongside what may be said, including that **the measurement is the part that is not optional.**

**3. The hero territory was stress-tested and Territory 1 was reframed.** All five territories were tested on what a buyer understands in about five seconds. The finding: **Territory 1's content survived and its expression did not** — "accountable" is a governance word before it is a commercial one, and "The Accountable Enquiry" sat one step from restating "measurable qualified enquiries" as a slogan. Territory 3 is settled against by existing Session 04 evidence; Territory 4 is the problem page's job and presumes traffic; **Territory 5 is now partly prohibited by correction 2, since its name asserts that MappedSkills replaces three suppliers.** **No strategic gap was revealed and no sixth territory was invented.** Territory 1 is reframed as **"Judged on the enquiries"**, with Territory 2's concrete opening as the supporting line. Territories 2–5 are placed rather than discarded.
