**Supersedes:** the three-pillar working hypothesis (`DEC-004`) and every prior "Current Working Direction" statement in this file.

## 1. The approved fundamental direction

**APPROVED DECISION:**

> **MappedSkills exists to help businesses capture buyer demand and turn it into measurable qualified enquiries.**

This is the strategic foundation. It is not a tagline, not a headline and not homepage copy. Later messaging work may find a sharper buyer-facing articulation; **the underlying meaning must be preserved, the wording need not be.**

---

## 2. What business are we in?

**APPROVED DECISION.** MappedSkills is a **demand-capture and enquiry-generation firm** for businesses whose customers make a considered, high-value purchase.

The company is defined by the **commercial outcome it produces**, not by the techniques it uses to produce it.

### The category distinction that must not be lost

There are two different questions, and conflating them is how the previous hypothesis went wrong:

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| **What is MappedSkills strategically?** | A firm that captures existing buyer demand and converts it into measurable qualified enquiries. |
| **Through which search categories will buyers discover MappedSkills?** | Conventional and emerging service categories — SEO, AI search, lead generation, local agency terms, vertical agency terms — because that is what buyers type. |

**APPROVED DECISION:** MappedSkills may compete for, rank for, and publish pages targeting searches such as "SEO agency", "AI SEO agency" or "SEO agency for manufacturing companies" **without defining itself company-wide as an SEO agency, GEO agency, AEO agency or LLMO agency.**

A page that targets a query is not a company-level positioning claim. This rule governs all subsequent information-architecture, content and copy work.

### Why the category is not forced to a conventional label

**VERIFIED FACT (Session 03).** The conventional labels available do not survive contact with the evidence:
- "Search Visibility", "search growth" and "organic growth" already belong to other industries (social-follower services, contact-app profile settings). `search growth agency` returns **(NONE)**.
- "CRO" as an acronym resolves to pharmaceutical Contract Research Organisations in India; `conversion rate optimization agency` returns **(NONE)**.
- GEO / AEO / LLMO resolve upstream to courses, jobs and certifications ahead of agency intent.
- "AI visibility" resolves to free tools, not agencies.
- "Performance marketing agency" and "full-service digital agency" are red-ocean and are MappedSkills' current, non-performing identity.

The category is therefore stated in **plain commercial language** and the acronym vocabulary is confined to the contexts where buyers actually use it.

## 3. What problem do we solve?

**APPROVED DECISION — the primary buyer problem, in the buyer's own words:**

> "Buyers who need what we sell are out there searching. Most of them never reach us — and of the ones who do, too few are worth quoting, and we cannot tell where any of them came from."

Three failures, one problem: **not found → not converted → not measured.** MappedSkills is hired to fix all three together, because fixing one alone changes nothing the client can observe.

### Secondary problems — real, but expansion rather than entry
- enquiries are not followed up quickly or consistently → automation expansion;
- no attribution across channels or campaigns → measurement expansion;
- paid acquisition cost per enquiry is rising → paid expansion, and a common entry symptom;
- export or new-market demand is untapped → international expansion;
- the brand is absent from AI answers → visibility expansion and a credibility hook, **not** an entry problem.

### Deliberately not the problem we sell against
Rankings, traffic, impressions, brand awareness, "AI visibility". Each is a means. None is the reason a business owner picks up the phone.

## 4. For whom?

**APPROVED DECISION.** Considered-purchase businesses where **each qualified opportunity has meaningful economic value**, and where buyer demand already exists or can reasonably be accessed.

**MappedSkills is not designed primarily for businesses that need demand invented from zero.** The proposition is demand *capture*, not demand *creation*.

Full qualification criteria, buyer roles, weak-fit signals and disqualifiers are in `ICP.md`, which is the operational document for fit assessment.

## 5. What outcome do we create?

**APPROVED DECISION — the primary commercial outcome:**

> **Measurable qualified enquiries.**

MappedSkills improves the client's ability to capture buyer demand and convert it into qualified business enquiries, and instruments the path so the claim is checkable in the client's own data.

### Revenue — the rule
**APPROVED DECISION.** Revenue may be **measured, attributed where possible, reported and analysed**. Revenue is **NOT the promised outcome**.

MappedSkills does not control pricing, product quality, sales capacity, follow-up speed or close rate. The honest formulation, which must be preserved in all future messaging:

> *We own the enquiry. We report through to revenue using your close data. We state plainly what cannot be attributed.*

**No future session may convert this into a revenue promise, a guaranteed-ROI claim, or a "revenue marketing" position.**

### The hard precondition
**APPROVED DECISION.** The enquiry outcome may not be sold to any client — **including MappedSkills itself on its own website** — before analytics and enquiry-source capture are working.

**VERIFIED FACT (Session 01B, live):** MappedSkills currently has no analytics of any kind in production, a contact form with no `action` and no `method` that transmits nothing, and an empty booking widget. Neither documented conversion path functions. This precondition therefore has immediate practical force on the transformation programme and cannot be deferred past it.

## 6. How do we create it?

The core outcome system has three inseparable parts. They are one engagement, not three services.

1. **Capture demand** — make the business findable and compelling at the moment a buyer is already looking. Search is the primary capability here.
2. **Convert demand into enquiries** — landing experiences, enquiry paths, forms, calls, quote and booking journeys, designed and researched rather than assumed.
3. **Measure the whole chain** — discovery/source → landing experience → interaction → enquiry → qualified enquiry → opportunity → customer/revenue data supplied by the client, connected wherever technically and operationally possible.

**Measurement is a mandatory foundation of the operating model, not a reporting add-on.** It is what makes the promise falsifiable and therefore credible. Capability detail is in `SERVICE_ARCHITECTURE.md`.

## 7. What do we deliberately not do?

**APPROVED DECISION — rejected company-level positioning.** MappedSkills must NOT become positioned as:

1. a full-service digital marketing agency;
2. a generic performance marketing agency;
3. an SEO agency;
4. a GEO / AEO / LLMO agency;
5. a marketing automation agency;
6. a RevOps consultancy;
7. an AI visibility dashboard or score company.

**Capabilities may exist without defining the company.** MappedSkills does SEO; it is not an SEO agency. It runs paid media; it is not a performance marketing agency. It builds follow-up automation; it is not an automation agency.

### Also excluded from scope
- CRM implementation, platform migrations, lifecycle-automation consulting and sales-ops consulting;
- a universal or blended "AI Visibility Score" as the core proposition — **rejected on methodological grounds** (Session 04: cross-system agreement collapses; result order was unstable in every repeated prompt), not merely commercial ones;
- city-page grids, doorway pages and scaled location SEO (Project Rule 10);
- content-volume production;
- competing on price against packaged low-cost SEO;
- statistically valid A/B testing promised where traffic volume cannot support significance.

## 8. How do we acquire customers?

**APPROVED DECISION — the core spearhead is capturing existing buyer/search demand and turning it into enquiries. Search is currently the strongest acquisition spearhead.**

**VERIFIED FACT (Sessions 02, 03, 04 — three independent evidence bases):** organic search **cannot be the launch acquisition channel**. It is a 12-month-plus compounding engine. Category SERPs are gated by listicles and directories; local demand is gated by Google Business Profile, reviews and tenure.

Acquisition therefore runs on more than one horizon:

| Horizon | Route | Status |
|---|---|---|
| **Immediate** | Third-party listings, directory profiles, reviews, Google Business Profile | Highest-leverage action available; needs no case study and no repositioning to begin. Directory sources appeared in **15 of 28** unbranded AI runs (Session 04) and gate both the category SERPs and the Local Pack |
| **Immediate** | Owner network, outbound, referral | OWNER REQUIREMENT territory; no Claude session can execute it |
| **Near-term** | Paid media as a demand accelerator and proof engine | See §10 and `SERVICE_ARCHITECTURE.md` |
| **Compounding (12 months+)** | Organic search: vertical service pages, buyer-symptom problem content, AI-search question content, technical foundations | The long engine. **No plan may assume the new website generates demand on publication** |
| **Compounding** | Published methodology, own-site diagnostics, research and benchmarks | Also the proof programme (§11) |

## 9. How does the engagement expand?

Entry → Core → Expansion → Retention, defined commercially in `OFFER_ARCHITECTURE.md`.

Expansion is sold **when the client feels the pain, not when MappedSkills argues it**. Enquiry volume itself creates the follow-up problem that sells the automation, and the multi-channel question that sells the additional demand sources.

**APPROVED DECISION — the boundary that keeps this coherent:** enquiry-source capture is **measurement** and stays in the core engagement; nurture, follow-up sequences and lifecycle work are **automation** and stay in expansion.

## 10. Channel and capability decisions

### Search / SEO
**APPROVED DECISION.** Search is a **primary acquisition capability**. SEO is an **implementation discipline beneath the proposition**, not the proposition. SEO/GEO/AEO terminology remains usable on relevant service, search-intent and educational pages.

### AI Search
**APPROVED DECISION.** AI Search is an **important, highly visible supporting capability and differentiator** — and is **subordinate to the enquiry-generation outcome**. It operates as delivery capability, methodology, diagnostic component, content-authority surface and proof mechanism. **It is not the company category.** See §10 of `POSITIONING.md` and `SERVICE_ARCHITECTURE.md` for the operating detail.

### Conversion
**APPROVED DECISION.** Conversion and enquiry-path optimisation is **integrated into the core delivery system** — the second half of one outcome, not a second service. It is expressed in buyer and business language rather than the acronym "CRO". Statistically valid experimentation must not be promised where traffic is insufficient; below that threshold the work is conversion research, diagnosis and design.

### Measurement and attribution
**APPROVED DECISION.** Mandatory foundation. See §5 and §6.

### Paid media
**APPROVED DECISION.** Retained as an acquisition capability; **removed from company-level positioning.**

It can accelerate demand capture, create near-term enquiry volume, support testing, generate data, and complement organic acquisition. It is also the fastest available route to the client outcome data the proof programme requires.

**Existing paid-media URLs must not be removed merely because paid media is no longer company positioning.** Final URL decisions belong to the later information-architecture phase.

### Automation
**APPROVED DECISION.** A **limited downstream expansion capability**. In scope: enquiry routing, lead notifications, response-time workflows, email/WhatsApp follow-up, basic qualification, simple handoff automation. Out of scope: RevOps, CRM implementation, lifecycle-automation consulting.

### Vertical strategy
**APPROVED DECISION.** **Horizontal business proposition with vertical beachheads.**

Manufacturing / industrial B2B is **candidate vertical beachhead #1** — the vertical where content, listings, outbound and case-study effort is concentrated — because research produced useful signals. It is **NOT approved as exclusive specialisation**, and MappedSkills does not become a manufacturing agency. Other verticals may later be evaluated on evidence.

### Geography
**APPROVED DECISION.** **India-first.** International work remains strategically possible. **No international SEO or location strategy is to be created yet.**

**VERIFIED FACT:** no international SERP and no international AI answer has ever been observed in this programme — egress was Pune-locked in both research sessions. No claim about international winnability is supportable today.

## 11. What must be proven?

**APPROVED DECISION — MappedSkills currently has a significant proof deficit, and the strategy treats proof as the binding constraint rather than positioning.**

**VERIFIED FACT.** MappedSkills would enter *any* category today with: zero published case studies, zero published articles, no analytics baseline, no third-party directory profile, no surfaced Google Business Profile, no reviews, no research asset, and zero appearances across 28 unbranded AI runs — while already displaying ₹50K–₹2L/month pricing in production. **The credibility gap exists today, at the point of sale. It is not a future transition risk.**

### The proof programme, in order of speed to obtain
1. **Third-party listings, profiles and reviews** — Clutch, Semrush Agency Partners, GoodFirms, Google Business Profile. Self-serve, needs no case study, gates AI answers and category SERPs simultaneously.
2. **Fix MappedSkills' own conversion and measurement layer, and publish it as case study one** — the audit trail is documented, dated and reproducible.
3. **A published methodology, including a plain statement of what cannot be attributed** — unclaimed by every competitor examined.
4. **MappedSkills' own AI-visibility baseline, published honestly with method and run counts** — publishing a measured zero with its trajectory is more credible than any score.
5. **Real diagnostics, audits, research, experiments and worked examples**, anonymised where permission is absent.
6. **Named authorship and visible founder expertise.**
7. **Client evidence where written permission exists** — OWNER REQUIREMENT; no Claude session can produce it.

### Existing claims — the strategic rule
**APPROVED DECISION.** The unsupported claims currently present in MappedSkills' own material — **"300%+ ROI", "₹100Cr+", "₹10Cr+"** — **must not be relied upon as strategic reasons-to-believe, and must not be used as evidence in any strategy, positioning, content or copy work until provenance and evidence are established.**

**VERIFIED FACT (Session 04):** Google AI Mode surfaced and Perplexity repeated the "300%+ ROI" claim as a description of the company. Whatever the site asserts is what AI systems redistribute. This is a live exposure requiring owner action.

**No production content was deleted in Session 06.** The rule is recorded here; the remediation is an owner decision and a later implementation task.

**Never fabricate proof.** Project Rule 15 stands unqualified.

## 12. How does the business become more defensible over time?

`POSITIONING.md` §8 and §9 carry the full treatment. The distinction is load-bearing and must be maintained by every future session:

- **Differentiation today** is real, useful and **copyable within weeks**. It is what makes a prospect choose MappedSkills next quarter.
- **Defensibility later** is what compounds and what a competitor cannot catch up on: client outcome evidence, longitudinal benchmarks, vertical pattern libraries, proprietary datasets, reusable instrumentation, authority/source footprint, distribution, real research, and client history.

**A named framework is not a moat.** Neither is a score, a tool subscription, service breadth, office locations, years in business, or being early to a piece of terminology.

## 13. Strategic principle for the website itself

The MappedSkills website must not merely claim the capability. It should be a working demonstration of demand capture, enquiry conversion and honest measurement — including publishing what MappedSkills found wrong with itself and fixed.

This is currently the **only proof asset available that requires no client permission**, which makes it strategically load-bearing rather than a nice-to-have.

## 15. Reversal conditions

Recorded in advance so that any change of direction is a decision rather than a drift. These do not reopen the approved direction; they define what evidence would justify revisiting a subordinate choice.

- **Change the beachhead if** keyword tooling shows the manufacturing family cannot sustain a pipeline; or a healthcare SERP test shows materially better winnability on the same basis; or twelve months of concentrated effort produces no industrial client.
- **Reconsider the weight of AI Search — upward — if** re-verified data shows AI referral traffic reaching a materially larger share of relevant categories; or Claude/Grok baselines, once measurable, show a substantially different picture; or AI answers begin recommending agencies ahead of measurement tools; or Google's published position changes from "this is still SEO".
- **Reconsider paid media — downward — if** organic and referral acquisition reach self-sustaining volume and paid delivery dilutes the specialisation, or if paid-acquired clients systematically fail the fit test in `ICP.md`.
- **Reconsider the automation exclusion — upward — only if** MappedSkills obtains a genuine platform credential, or narrow-scope expansion revenue becomes a material and sustained share of revenue.
- **Reconsider the enquiry outcome itself only if** clients consistently reject enquiry-level accountability, demand revenue-level accountability, **and** grant the close-rate data that would make it honest. Absent that data, the promise does not move.
- **Escalate the proof problem to a business-viability question if** no publishable case study exists twelve months from now.
