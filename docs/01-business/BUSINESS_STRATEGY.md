# MappedSkills Business Strategy — Current Working State

## Strategic Problem
MappedSkills historically operates in a highly competitive digital/performance marketing market where:
- service lists are easy to copy;
- price comparison is common;
- recognizable proof matters;
- AI is commoditizing parts of agency execution;
- generic “full-service” positioning is difficult to differentiate.

## Strategic Goal
Identify a focused service architecture that:
- can be productised;
- is easier to explain;
- has recurring revenue potential;
- can scale operationally;
- can acquire customers through organic search;
- has international potential;
- offers sufficient white space to build authority.

## Current Working Direction
**WORKING HYPOTHESIS:**
Use three interconnected capabilities:

### 1. AI + Search Visibility
Primary acquisition / authority service.

Outcome:
Help businesses become more discoverable across traditional search and emerging AI discovery/answer experiences.

Potential components:
- SEO
- technical SEO
- AI search optimisation
- GEO
- AEO
- entity/brand authority
- content authority
- AI visibility measurement

### 2. Conversion Rate Optimisation
Value multiplier.

Outcome:
Increase the proportion of existing traffic that becomes enquiries, qualified leads, demos, purchases, or other business outcomes.

Potential components:
- CRO
- landing-page optimization
- funnel optimization
- website conversion
- A/B testing
- UX optimization
- offer/message experimentation

### 3. Marketing Automation
Post-conversion value multiplier.

Outcome:
Improve what happens after conversion through structured automation.

Potential components:
- CRM workflows
- lead routing
- lead scoring
- email/WhatsApp nurture
- AI-assisted qualification
- sales alerts
- follow-up automation
- lifecycle automation

## Working Commercial Story
**GET FOUND → CONVERT → AUTOMATE → GROW**

The commercial logic:
1. Search Visibility attracts demand.
2. CRO increases the value of acquired traffic.
3. Automation increases the value and efficiency of captured demand.

## Why This Is Attractive
Potential strengths:
- simpler than a broad 360° agency;
- natural cross-sell path;
- recurring work across all three pillars;
- website can demonstrate all three services;
- content and tools can support inbound acquisition;
- Search Visibility offers a direct reason to invest in SEO/GEO for MappedSkills itself.

## Strategic Risks
- AI-search/GEO is rapidly becoming crowded.
- terminology may change faster than buyer demand.
- CRO requires proof and experimentation discipline.
- marketing automation can become implementation-heavy and platform-specific.
- “three pillars” can still become broad if delivery scope is not productised.
- search visibility cannot depend on unproven LLM ranking tactics.

## Validation Required
The project must compare this direction against alternatives using:
- search demand;
- commercial intent;
- competitive density;
- buyer awareness;
- pricing potential;
- recurring revenue;
- delivery complexity;
- proof requirements;
- international scalability;
- AI commoditisation risk;
- ability to rank organically.

## Strategic Principle
The website should not merely claim expertise. It should become a live demonstration of MappedSkills' own Search Visibility, CRO, and Automation capabilities.
