## 1. Executive recommendation

**Recommendation: MODEL C — a specialist enquiry-generation firm for considered-purchase B2B, taken to market through one vertical beachhead at a time, beginning with industrial/manufacturing B2B.**

MappedSkills should sell **qualified enquiries, honestly measured**, to businesses whose customers make a considered, high-value purchase and who already have some traffic or spend but too few good enquiries and no reliable way to tell what produced them. The spearhead is a **paid Enquiry Diagnostic**. Search is the compounding acquisition engine underneath it; conversion is the second half of the same outcome, not a second service; paid media is retained as a delivery capability and the near-term proof engine but removed from the company's positioning; AI search is a supporting capability that is deliberately kept visible in methodology, diagnostics, content and proof; marketing automation is removed from positioning and retained as expansion.

**The three modifications this makes to the existing hypothesis, stated plainly:**

1. **The outcome replaces the technique as the proposition.** "AI + Search Visibility" fails on three independent evidence bases. "Qualified enquiries" survives all three, is causally close enough to the work to measure honestly, and is the outcome the AI systems themselves used unprompted in the vertical prompts (RFQ).
2. **The buyer narrows before the service list does.** The one mechanism observed twice, independently — in the Google Local Pack (S03) and inside AI answers (S04) — by which an agency without tenure beats agencies with tenure is **specialisation**. That is the only lever MappedSkills has against Pune incumbents trading since 1998 and 2004.
3. **Proof is treated as the first deliverable, not a marketing afterthought.** All three sessions converge on this. The single cheapest, fastest, evidence-backed proof action available — third-party listings and reviews — requires no case study to begin, and S04 documented it as the mechanism that actually drives selection in both AI answers and category SERPs.

**What is genuinely new in this synthesis, beyond restating the three sessions:**

- **The price-point premise carried since Session 02 is factually wrong** (§2.6). MappedSkills already displays ₹50K–₹2L/month in production. The repositioning is a **proof and messaging change, not a business-model or ICP price change.** This materially reduces the risk of the recommended direction and removes one of the two owner blockers Session 02 raised.
- **Paid media is given a positive strategic role rather than being quietly dropped.** Sessions 02–04 were all organic-lens sessions; each independently concluded organic cannot be the launch channel and is a 12-month-plus engine. No session then asked what pays for those twelve months or generates the client data the proof architecture requires. Paid media answers both, and it is coherent inside an enquiry proposition in a way it is not inside a visibility proposition.
- **The S03/S04 one-notch gap on AI search is closed on two axes rather than one** (§11). Session 04's lower placement is correct for *weight in the proposition*; Session 03's higher placement is correct for *visibility in the evidence and content layer*. These are different questions and both answers can stand.

### 2.3 Evidence that constrains what may be promised

- **AI referral traffic is ~0.28% of total web traffic against Google's 28.12% (March 2026)** — roughly 98× smaller by volume. Vendor blog citing an unaudited third-party panel; **must be re-verified before any client-facing use.** This alone settles that AI search cannot carry a proposition.
- **AI brand recommendations are unstable.** Identical repeat lists under 1-in-100; order unstable in every repeated prompt in S04's own data. No rank claim is defensible.
- **Google's published position** is that optimising for generative AI "is thus still SEO", with no new files, markup, chunking or schema required. A prospect can read this and use it against a GEO pitch.
- **Absolute search volume is UNKNOWN everywhere in this programme.** No keyword tool has ever been available. Every demand judgement in Sessions 03 and 04 is qualitative.
- **No international SERP or AI answer has ever been observed.** Egress was Pune-locked in both sessions. Every international statement in this document is explicitly a hypothesis.
- **Two of six charter AI systems (Claude, Grok) have no baseline of any kind.**
- **MappedSkills' commercial baseline is zero.** No analytics in production, no working contact form, no working booking widget, zero published articles, zero case studies, zero unbranded AI appearances.

### 2.4 The uncomfortable finding that must not be lost

**MappedSkills' *current* category has strong Pune search demand that MappedSkills is not capturing** (S03 finding #10). The owner-supplied absence of enquiries therefore cannot be blamed on absent demand or on the wrong positioning. The documented causes are: a contact form with no `action` and no `method` that transmits nothing, a booking widget that renders empty, no analytics of any kind, no surfaced Google Business Profile, and no reviews or tenure.

**Strategic consequence:** a repositioning that does not first fix the conversion and measurement layer would change the words on a website that still cannot receive an enquiry. **Sequencing matters more than category choice**, and this outranks every model comparison below.

### 2.5 The live exposure

Google AI Mode surfaced, and Perplexity repeated, MappedSkills' own "300%+ ROI" claim as a description of the company. Only ChatGPT qualified it as self-reported. Because branded AI answers are built almost entirely from first-party sources, **whatever the site asserts is what AI systems will repeat.** The claim is of unknown provenance (S02) and violates Project Rule 15. This is an owner decision requiring action, not a copy edit deferred to a later phase.

Separately, `testing.mappedskills.com` — a staging subdomain with no `robots.txt` — was cited by Perplexity as a source about the company.

## 5. Recommended model

**MODEL C.**

### Why C wins

1. **It is the only model with both a real outcome and a real acquisition surface.** Model A has the outcome and no way in; Model B has a way in that is gated by tenure MappedSkills does not have. C takes A's outcome and routes it through the one query family S03 found uncontaminated and open.
2. **It uses the only lever that beats tenure.** Specialisation was observed substituting for tenure twice, on two independent surfaces. Nothing else in three sessions of evidence gives an unproven agency a route past a 1998-founded incumbent.
3. **It makes the proof burden finite.** One vertical needs one case study, one pattern library, one benchmark. Horizontal models need proof in every industry they touch, simultaneously — which is precisely how the three-pillar hypothesis failed.
4. **It promises something MappedSkills can actually measure.** Enquiries are causally close to the work and instrumentable. Rankings are contested and slow; AI visibility is unstable and ~98× smaller than organic; revenue depends on the client's pricing, product and close rate.
5. **It keeps AI search useful without betting the company on it.** The capability is delivered, measured honestly, and made visible through buyer-phrased content and diagnostics — while the proposition rests on something that does not evaporate if the terminology changes again.
6. **It survives the terminology churn already underway.** GEO/AEO/AIO are still fragmenting and Google calls the whole thing "still SEO". An enquiry outcome is unaffected by any of that.
7. **It matches what MappedSkills already charges.** ₹50K–₹2L/month is a considered-purchase B2B fee, not an SMB packaged-SEO fee. C is the only model whose buyer justifies the price already displayed (§2.6).

### Why the other two lose

**Model A loses on acquisition.** Everything about the outcome is right — it is retained wholesale inside C. But "enquiry" is not a category anyone searches, the buyer-symptom family is clean but only 2–3 variants deep, and without a narrowing the model has no defensible answer to "why you?" beyond promises. S02 classified undifferentiated horizontal positioning RED OCEAN, and A would drift there within a year. **A is not rejected so much as absorbed: C is A with a way in.**

**Model B loses on evidence, twice over.** Its acquisition path is the most tenure-gated in the market — 1998 and 2004 incumbents with hundreds to thousands of reviews — and its differentiator was tested and found worthless: the two Pune agencies selling exactly this position appeared **0 times in 28** unbranded AI runs, while the AI answers recommended measurement *tools* ahead of any agency. Its proof burden is also the highest and slowest of the three. Its one genuine asset, the variance-aware method, is retained inside C as methodology and proof — where S04 found its real value lies — rather than as a proposition buyers were never observed asking for.

### What we are deliberately giving up

- **The broad Pune generalist demand** (`digital marketing agency pune`, `performance marketing agency pune`) as a content and positioning target. It is real and MappedSkills is not capturing it — but the barrier is tenure, reviews and Google Business Profile, and no content programme fixes that. It is addressed through listings and reviews instead, not through the proposition.
- **GEO/AEO/LLMO trend traffic** and any claim to be an AI-search company.
- **Breadth of addressable market in year one.** C says no to buyers with low value per customer, no considered purchase, or no sales process.
- **"Performance Marketing Agency" as the company's identity**, including the "300%+ ROI" claim attached to it.

### What remains optional

- The second vertical beachhead, and which one it is.
- International market entry, sequencing and target geography.
- Whether the automation expansion is ever built out or stays a referral.
- Whether the AI-visibility diagnostic becomes a productised standalone paid item or stays a section inside the Enquiry Diagnostic.
- Whether a public benchmark is published at all, and at what cadence.

## 6. ICP

**Primary ICP — the qualifying conditions, in priority order.** These are the conditions that make MappedSkills economically valuable, not a description of "companies that need SEO".

| Condition | Requirement | Why it is load-bearing |
|---|---|---|
| **Considered, high-value purchase** | Multi-step evaluation with a quote, specification or proposal stage; a human close | Without it, an enquiry is not the unit of value and the whole proposition collapses |
| **Value per customer** | High enough that a handful of additional qualified enquiries per month materially exceeds the fee | This is the arithmetic that supports a ₹50K–₹2L/month fee. It is the single most important qualifier |
| **Existing demand signal** | Some traffic, some spend, or a demonstrable search demand family for what they sell | MappedSkills improves capture and discovery; it cannot manufacture a market |
| **A functioning sales process** | Someone follows up, and can tell a good enquiry from a bad one | Enquiry *quality* cannot be measured against a client who does not qualify or track |
| **Measurement access** | Willingness to grant analytics, site and enquiry-data access | Every outcome claim depends on it. Non-negotiable |
| **Business maturity** | Past founder-only survival mode; a marketing budget that already exists and is being spent somewhere | S02's competitor evidence shows the sub-₹50K band is served by $199/month packaged competitors MappedSkills cannot and should not undercut |
| **Internal capacity to act** | Someone who can approve site changes, supply product knowledge and respond to enquiries | Recommendations that are never implemented produce no result and no case study |

**Segment characteristics (recommended, pilot-scoped):**
- **B2B over B2C.** Considered purchases, traceable enquiries, high customer value, and long cycles that make retainers rational.
- **Services and manufacturing over ecommerce.** Ecommerce optimisation is a different discipline with a different measurement model.
- **Beachhead: Indian industrial/manufacturing B2B** — components, engineering services, industrial equipment, contract manufacturing, technical suppliers with dealer or export channels.
- **India-first, with export-oriented Indian manufacturers as the natural bridge to international work** (§16).
- **Internal marketing capability: low to moderate.** These buyers typically have no in-house SEO or CRO function, which is why the work is bought rather than hired.

**Explicitly de-prioritised, and why:** pre-revenue startups (no budget, no data); local B2C services (value per customer too low for the fee); ecommerce (different discipline); enterprises with in-house teams and procurement (MappedSkills has no credential to clear that gate); and **professional-services clients** — S02 recorded professional services as the weakest AI-citation category reported, and it is also where MappedSkills' own weak-proof problem is mirrored in the client.

**Deliberately NOT fixed here:** revenue bands, headcount and turnover thresholds. There is no evidence to set them, and S02's ICP guidance that problem sophistication segments better than turnover stands.

## 7. Primary problem

**"Buyers who need what we sell are out there searching. Most of them never reach us — and of the ones who do, too few are worth quoting, and we cannot tell where any of them came from."**

Three failures in one sentence, in the buyer's own words, with no marketing taxonomy: **not found → not converted → not measured.** MappedSkills is hired to fix all three as one problem, because fixing any one alone changes nothing the client can see.

**Secondary problems** — real, but expansion, not entry:
- enquiries are not followed up quickly or consistently (→ automation expansion);
- no attribution across channels or campaigns (→ measurement expansion);
- paid media cost per enquiry is rising (→ paid expansion, and a common entry symptom);
- export or new-market demand is untapped (→ international expansion);
- the brand is absent from AI answers (→ visibility expansion, and a credibility hook, **not** an entry problem: ~0.28% of web traffic).

**Not primary, deliberately:** rankings, traffic, AI visibility, brand awareness. Each is a means; none is the reason a buyer calls.

## 8. Primary outcome

**Qualified enquiries, measured from first touch to enquiry — reported against revenue where the client's own data allows it.**

**Why not revenue.** MappedSkills does not control pricing, product quality, sales capacity, follow-up speed or close rate. Promising revenue makes the claim unfalsifiable at the point of sale and undefendable at the point of review — S02 recorded that "revenue growth agency" language is RED OCEAN precisely because every agency claims it and attribution is exactly what buyers dispute. **Revenue is reported, not promised.** The honest formulation is: *we own the enquiry; we report through to revenue using your close data; we state plainly what cannot be attributed.*

**Why not visibility, rankings or AI citations.** All are proxies. Rankings are contested and unstable; AI citations are ~98× smaller than organic by volume and methodologically resistant to rank-style reporting.

**Why enquiries.** Commercially meaningful to a business owner without translation; causally close enough to the work to measure honestly; instrumentable today; and it forces conversion and measurement into the core engagement rather than leaving them as upsells.

**Precondition, stated as a hard rule:** this outcome may not be sold to any client — including MappedSkills itself — before analytics and enquiry-source capture are working. S04 §12 is unambiguous: nothing in the chain is measurable without them, and MappedSkills currently has none of it in production.

## 11. Role of AI Search

**The five subordinate roles AI Search occupies:**

2. **Methodology** — per-system, per-prompt, run-count-published measurement. Never blended, never ranked, never scored. Mandatory internally whether or not it is ever sold.

**What AI Search must never be:** the category, the headline, a blended visibility score, a guarantee of citation or AI ranking, or a standalone acquisition product competing with free tools from Ahrefs and Semrush.

**The forward-looking requirement in ruling §14 is met by roles 3, 4 and 5 rather than by the positioning line.** MappedSkills looks current because it demonstrably *does* this work and publishes its own numbers — which is more convincing than claiming the category.

## 14. Role of paid media

**Explicit decision: retained as a core delivery capability and the primary near-term revenue and proof engine — and removed from company-level positioning.**

This is a deliberate divergence from the drift of Sessions 02–04, and the reasoning is stated so it can be contested.

**Why it is retained, and given real weight:**
1. **All three sessions concluded organic cannot be the launch channel and is a 12-month-plus compounding engine.** None then asked what funds those twelve months. Paid media is the existing capability that does.
2. **It is the fastest generator of the thing that is actually blocking the business.** The binding constraint is proof. Paid media produces enquiry volume, conversion data and attributable results in weeks — which is what makes conversion work statistically meaningful, what feeds a vertical benchmark dataset, and what becomes a publishable case study. **Organic cannot produce the first case study fast enough for organic to matter.**
3. **It is coherent inside an enquiry proposition.** Paid is a demand source feeding the same enquiry system, on the same measurement layer. It does not fracture the promise the way a service menu would. Inside a *visibility* proposition it would have been incoherent — which is why Models A and C can hold it and Model B cannot.
4. **The existing service pages carry SEO equity** (`/google-ads`, `/social-media-ads`, `/lead-generation`) and are the target of 308 redirects from legacy URLs. Charter constraints require preserving them. They are repositioned as demand-source pages beneath the enquiry proposition, not deleted.

**Why it is removed from positioning:**
1. **"Performance Marketing Agency" is RED OCEAN** (S02 §2.2), and it is MappedSkills' current, non-performing identity.
2. **The identity carries the live exposure.** The homepage title claim "300%+ ROI" is unsubstantiated, of unknown provenance, and is being actively redistributed by Google AI Mode and Perplexity as a description of the company.
3. **`reduce cost per lead` was found to be a paid-media-shaped query in buyers' minds** and was REJECTED as a target. Paid is how buyers frame a cost problem; it is not how MappedSkills should frame its value.

**Consequence:** paid media becomes a *demand channel inside the engagement*, chosen on merit per client, and a fast-proof mechanism. It is not the category, not the headline, and not the reason a client calls.

## 15. Vertical strategy

**Recommendation: HORIZONTAL PROPOSITION WITH VERTICAL BEACHHEADS.**

The proposition — qualified enquiries for considered-purchase B2B — is horizontal and does not need rewriting to serve a second industry. **Go-to-market is vertical, one beachhead at a time**, because that is the only observed route past a tenure barrier and because it makes the proof burden finite.

**Manufacturing / industrial B2B — assessed explicitly, as required.**

*Supporting evidence:* the cleanest commercial autocomplete family observed anywhere in the programme (eight variants, six provider-seeking, **zero contamination**); an open SERP held by small local agencies; a 3-year-old, 17-review specialist holding a Local Pack slot beside 486- and 507-review generalists; the same specialist also surfacing in ChatGPT's Pune list; Pune's standing as a major Indian automotive and engineering centre; **all three AI systems using "RFQ" unprompted**; Pune agencies with street addresses surfacing in the Pune industrial prompt; an AI Overview served on this family (unlike the Pune local queries); and a natural export bridge to international work.

*Adverse evidence:* **absolute volume is UNKNOWN** and is the largest single risk to this direction; **no manufacturing case study exists**; manufacturing was the *least stable* AI prompt tested (2 of ~5 names recurring across runs); every observed entry route was a directory or a single listicle, **not vertical content**; and **healthcare has never been SERP-tested** despite an equally clean autocomplete profile, so the beachhead cannot be ranked against its nearest alternative.

**Ruling: APPROVED AS A PILOT BEACHHEAD CANDIDATE ONLY. NOT approved as the company's identity, and MappedSkills must not become "a manufacturing agency."** Per orchestrator ruling A, attractive SERPs are not sufficient grounds to define a company.

2. **A healthcare SERP test** on the same basis, so the choice is a comparison rather than a default.
3. **One completed industrial client engagement with a publishable result.** Requires owner action and client permission; cannot be produced by any Claude session.

**Until those gates clear:** industrial B2B is where content, listings, outbound and case-study effort is *concentrated*, while the proposition itself stays industry-neutral so a good non-industrial client is not turned away. ## 24. Reversal conditions

Written in advance so a change of direction is a decision rather than a drift.

**Change the beachhead if:**
- keyword tooling shows the manufacturing family is too small to sustain a pipeline; **or**
- a healthcare SERP test shows materially better winnability on the same basis; **or**
- twelve months of concentrated effort produces no industrial client.

**Abandon the vertical go-to-market entirely if** two beachhead attempts fail to produce a publishable result while horizontal enquiries convert at a materially better rate.

**Reconsider the weight of AI search — upward — if** any of: re-verified data shows AI referral traffic exceeding a materially larger share of relevant categories' traffic (the current figure is ~0.28%); Claude or Grok baselines, once measurable, show a substantially different picture; AI answers begin recommending agencies ahead of measurement tools; or Google's published position changes from "this is still SEO".

**Reconsider paid media — downward — if** organic and referral acquisition reach self-sustaining volume and paid delivery is diluting the specialisation, or if paid clients systematically fail the ICP test in §6.

**Reconsider the automation exclusion — upward — only if** MappedSkills obtains a genuine platform credential, or if expansion revenue from the narrow scope in §13 becomes a material and sustained share of revenue.

**Reconsider the enquiry outcome itself if** clients consistently reject enquiry-level accountability and demand revenue-level accountability *and* are willing to grant the close-rate data that would make it honest. **Absent that data, do not move the promise.**

**Escalate the proof problem to a business-viability question if** no publishable case study exists twelve months from now. At that point the premium price is not defensible on any positioning, and the entry offer would be carrying the entire commercial burden alone.
