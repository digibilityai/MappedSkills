# Ideal Customer Profiles — Working Hypotheses

## Status
These ICPs require validation through market, buyer, and search research.

## ICP A — Search Growth
### Typical Situation
A business already invests in SEO/content or depends on organic discovery and is concerned about changing search behaviour.

### Potential Buyers
- Founder
- CMO
- Head of Marketing
- SEO Lead
- Growth Lead

### Potential Problems
- organic visibility stagnating;
- uncertainty about AI search;
- brand absent from AI answers;
- competitors increasingly visible;
- content not earning authority;
- weak technical SEO foundation;
- difficulty measuring visibility beyond Google rankings.

### Likely Entry Service
AI + Search Visibility.

## ICP B — Conversion Growth
### Typical Situation
A company already receives meaningful traffic or spends materially on acquisition but conversion efficiency is weak.

### Potential Buyers
- Founder
- CMO
- Growth Head
- Ecommerce Head
- Performance Marketing Lead

### Potential Problems
- low enquiry rate;
- weak landing pages;
- high CAC;
- poor lead quality;
- drop-offs in key funnels;
- limited experimentation discipline.

### Likely Entry Service
CRO.

## ICP C — Marketing Automation
### Typical Situation
A company already generates a meaningful volume of leads/customers but follow-up and nurturing are manual or fragmented.

### Potential Buyers
- Founder
- Marketing Head
- Sales Head
- Growth Head
- Revenue/Operations Lead

### Potential Problems
- slow lead response;
- inconsistent follow-up;
- poor CRM hygiene;
- unqualified leads reaching sales;
- missing nurture;
- fragmented WhatsApp/email processes;
- poor lifecycle visibility.

### Likely Entry Service
Marketing Automation.

## Cross-ICP Qualification Signals
Potentially attractive clients have:
- meaningful traffic or acquisition spend;
- measurable lead/customer volume;
- sufficient customer value to justify specialist work;
- willingness to grant measurement access;
- decision-makers who care about business outcomes;
- internal capacity to act on recommendations.

## Avoid Over-Segmentation Too Early
Do not lock revenue bands, industries, geography, or company size until research supports them.

Problem sophistication may be a better segmentation variable than turnover alone.
