## Productisation Principle
Every offer should eventually define:
- target customer;
- problem solved;
- scope;
- exclusions;
- prerequisites;
- deliverables;
- timeline;
- measurement method;
- handoff;
- recurring component;
- proof required.

## Commercial Principle
Do not depend on selling vague strategy documents.

Where possible, connect diagnosis to measurable implementation and ongoing optimization.

## Status
All offer names, packages, and pricing remain subject to research and operational validation.
