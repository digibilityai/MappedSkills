## 1. Strategic positioning

**APPROVED DECISION — the strategic foundation:**

> **MappedSkills helps businesses capture buyer demand and turn it into measurable qualified enquiries.**

**This sentence is a strategic foundation, not a headline.** Later messaging work may discover a sharper buyer-facing articulation. **The underlying meaning must be preserved; the wording may change.** No session may treat this sentence as approved homepage copy, and no session may drift its meaning while rewording it.

**Concept-level value proposition (a description of the position, not copy):**
MappedSkills makes sure the buyers already looking for what a business sells can find it — that the enquiry actually reaches the business — and that the whole path can be counted.

## 2. Category relationship

**APPROVED DECISION.** MappedSkills is defined by the commercial outcome it produces, not by a technique category.

**What MappedSkills IS strategically:**
A demand-capture and enquiry-generation firm for businesses whose customers make a considered, high-value purchase.

**Search categories through which buyers may discover MappedSkills:**
SEO and AI-SEO agency terms, lead-generation terms, local agency terms, and vertical agency terms — because that is the vocabulary buyers actually type.

**The rule, stated so it cannot be misread:**

> **MappedSkills may compete for searches involving SEO agencies, AI SEO agencies, lead generation and similar categories WITHOUT defining itself as any of them.**
>
> A page that targets a query is not a company-level positioning claim.

This governs the information architecture, content and copy phases. A "/seo" page may exist and may rank; the company is still not an SEO agency.

### Why a conventional category label was not adopted
**VERIFIED FACT (Session 03, autocomplete and SERP evidence):**
- `search growth agency` → **(NONE)**; `search visibility` → getcontact / Twitter / Instagram profile settings; `organic growth agency` → TikTok and Twitter follower services. These terms are not unclaimed — they belong to other industries.
- `cro services` → pharmaceutical Contract Research Organisations; `b2b cro` → croissants and crown rings; `conversion rate optimization agency` → **(NONE)**.
- GEO / AEO / LLMO → course, meaning, jobs, certification ahead of agency intent.
- `ai visibility` → checker, score, tool, tracker, free — no agency term in the top nine.

**VERIFIED FACT (Session 02):** "performance marketing agency" and "full-service digital agency" are red-ocean, price-compared, and are MappedSkills' current, non-performing identity.

Buyers were **not** forced into a familiar-but-wrong category, and MappedSkills was **not** forced into an unfamiliar one. The company is described in plain commercial language; the category vocabulary is used where buyers use it.

## 3. Primary buyer problem

**APPROVED DECISION.** In the buyer's own words, with no marketing taxonomy:

> "Buyers who need what we sell are searching. Most never reach us, too few of those who do are worth quoting, and we cannot tell where any of them came from."

**Not found → not converted → not measured.** One problem with three faces. Solving one alone changes nothing the buyer can see, which is why the three are sold and delivered as one engagement.

---

## 4. Primary outcome

**APPROVED DECISION.** **Measurable qualified enquiries** — measured from first touch through to enquiry, and reported through to revenue **using the client's own close data** where that data exists and is reliable.

- **Revenue is reported, never promised.** MappedSkills does not control price, product, sales capacity, follow-up speed or close rate.
- **Rankings, traffic, impressions and AI visibility are proxies, not the outcome.** They may be reported as diagnostics; they are never the promise.
- **The outcome is only sellable once it is measurable.** No outcome-based offer may be sold — to a client or on MappedSkills' own site — before analytics and enquiry-source capture are working.

## 5. Buyer-facing language principles

These are **rules for later copy**, not copy.

1. **Lead with the buyer's commercial problem and outcome, not with technique.** The buyer does not wake up wanting SEO; they wake up wanting the phone to ring with the right people.
2. **Plain business language over acronyms.** "Getting enquiries" over "conversion rate optimisation". "Being found when buyers search" over "search visibility".
3. **Technique vocabulary is permitted where the query justifies it** — on service pages, search-intent pages and educational content. It must not become the company-level proposition.
4. **Every number carries its sample size, date, location and method.** No exceptions, including in marketing material.
5. **State what cannot be attributed.** This is a positioning asset, not a weakness to hide.
6. **No superlatives, no guarantees, no unfalsifiable claims.** Nothing that a prospect could not check.
7. **Name people.** Founder expertise and named authorship are currently among the few available trust signals.
8. **Never present an unproven claim as proof.** Project Rule 15.

## 6. Competitive alternatives

What a prospect actually chooses between. Positioning must be defensible against each.

| Alternative | Why a buyer picks it | Where it fails the buyer | MappedSkills' honest counter |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Doing nothing** | No cost, no disruption, no internal argument | The problem compounds silently; demand keeps going to competitors | A fixed-scope diagnostic makes the cost of inaction visible and specific rather than abstract |
| **Generic digital agencies** | Familiar, one supplier for everything, easy to buy | Breadth without accountability; the enquiry outcome is nobody's job | One outcome, instrumented, with a named owner |
| **SEO-only agencies** | Clear category, easy to compare, cheap entry tiers exist | Delivers rankings and traffic, stops before the enquiry and before measurement | Capture and conversion and measurement are one engagement |
| **Paid-media agencies** | Fast, visible, immediately measurable spend | Enquiry quality and the site's conversion path are outside their scope; cost per enquiry rises | Paid is retained as a demand source **inside** the enquiry system, on the same measurement layer |
| **Internal teams** | Cheaper at the margin, full context, full control | Rarely have SEO, conversion and instrumentation skills together; capacity-bound | Specialist system plus instrumentation the team keeps |
| **Fragmented specialists** (freelance SEO + a developer + an analytics contractor) | Cheap, flexible, low commitment | Nobody owns the outcome; the seams are exactly where enquiries are lost | Single accountable system across the seams |
| **AI-visibility tools and dashboards** | Cheap or free, instant, self-serve | Report the absence; do not diagnose the cause and do not fix it | The diagnostic chain rather than the score (§7 item 3) |

**Note carried from evidence, and not to be softened:** MappedSkills' current category has strong Pune search demand it is not capturing. The absence of enquiries cannot be attributed to absent demand or to the wrong positioning — the documented causes are a form that transmits nothing, an empty booking widget, no analytics, no surfaced Google Business Profile, no reviews and no tenure. **Sequencing outranks category choice.**

## 7. Differentiation today

Available now. **All of it is copyable within weeks by any competitor reading the same sources. None of it is a moat.** Do not overclaim it as one.

1. **One outcome instead of a service list**, in the buyer's own words, in a market of acronyms.
2. **Conversion and measurement built into the discovery promise.** Session 02 found no examined Indian competitor connects visibility to a working conversion and measurement layer; the closest stops at dashboards.
3. **The diagnostic chain rather than the score** — absent from AI answers → because the cited sources are directories and listings → which this business is not on. Session 04's finding is that the tools report the absence and nobody sells the chain.
4. **Agreement with Google's published position** — that optimising for generative AI is "thus still SEO" — while being specific about which non-Google surfaces genuinely behave differently. Competitors selling GEO would have to contradict their own marketing to take this position.
5. **Variance-aware measurement** — frequency across repeated runs, per system, run counts published, with a plain statement that "rank in AI" is not a real number.
6. **Honesty about attribution**, including a published list of what cannot be attributed.
7. **The own-site case study** — a firm that ran its own diagnostic, published the failures, and fixed them in public.
8. **Industrial domain fluency** — RFQ paths, specifications, dealer and export journeys — in a category where the incumbents are generalists. *(Beachhead-scoped; see §11.)*

## 9. Language to avoid

### Never as company-level positioning
- **GEO**, **AEO**, **LLMO**, "generative engine optimisation", "answer engine optimisation";
- "AI Visibility Score", "average AI rank", any blended cross-system visibility figure;
- "Search Visibility" as a category, "organic growth", "search growth", "growth agency";
- "revenue marketing", "RevOps";
- "performance marketing agency", "full-service digital marketing agency", "360° agency";
- "**CRO**" as an acronym in Indian-facing language;
- "marketing automation agency", "CRM partner", "lifecycle marketing".

### Usable in content, service and delivery contexts where the query justifies it
"AI search", "how to appear in ChatGPT", "how to get cited by AI", "SEO", "technical SEO", "conversion", "landing pages", "lead generation", "Google Ads". **Ruling: a page may target a term the company does not use as positioning.**

### Unsupported promises — never usable anywhere
- any guarantee of ranking, AI citation, "rank #1 in ChatGPT", traffic or revenue;
- **"300%+ ROI", "₹100Cr+", "₹10Cr+"** — unsubstantiated claims of unknown provenance currently present in MappedSkills' own material and **actively being redistributed by AI systems as fact about the company**. These must not be used as reasons-to-believe in any strategy, content or copy work until provenance and evidence are established;
- any client result, logo or testimonial without written permission;
- A/B testing or experimentation language where traffic volume cannot support statistical significance;
- any number without its sample size, date, location and method;
- vendor-blog benchmarks quoted as MappedSkills' own — specifically the Ahrefs 23× and Buffer 185% AI-visitor conversion multipliers, which are single-company figures with self-selecting audiences.

## 10. Role of AI Search in the positioning

**APPROVED DECISION.** AI Search is an **important, highly visible supporting capability and differentiator** — **highly visible in capability, content and proof, and subordinate to the enquiry-generation outcome in the proposition.**

Both halves are load-bearing. Future teams must not collapse either one.

**Visible.** The website must clearly demonstrate genuine expertise in Google Search, AI-assisted search and discovery, citations and source authority, entity visibility, and evolving discovery behaviour. This is real work, it is technically genuine, and it is one of the few places where MappedSkills' documented capability is already a credential.

**Subordinate.** The homepage and the company category must not reduce MappedSkills to GEO/AEO. Evidence:
- AI chatbots sent ~0.28% of total web traffic in March 2026 against Google's 28.12% — roughly 98× smaller by volume *(vendor blog citing an unaudited third-party panel; re-verify before any client-facing use)*;
- the two Pune agencies selling GEO positioning appeared **0 times in 28** unbranded AI runs;
- AI answers recommend measurement tools (Semrush, SE Ranking, Ahrefs, Profound) **ahead of any agency**;
- the highest-intent local query, `best SEO agencies in Pune`, served a **Local Pack and no AI Overview at all**;
- Google's own published position is that optimising for generative AI "is thus still SEO".

**The five subordinate roles AI Search occupies:** delivery capability · methodology · diagnostic component · content-authority surface · proof mechanism. Operating detail in `SERVICE_ARCHITECTURE.md` §5.

**What AI Search must never be:** the category, the headline, a blended visibility score, a guarantee of citation or AI ranking, or a standalone product competing with free tools.

## 11. Vertical and geographic positioning

**Vertical.** Horizontal proposition, vertical beachheads. **Manufacturing / industrial B2B is candidate vertical beachhead #1 — not approved exclusive specialisation.**

**MappedSkills must NOT be described as "a marketing agency for manufacturers" or any equivalent.** The proposition stays industry-neutral so that a good non-industrial client is not turned away; the *effort* — content, listings, outbound, case-study pursuit — is concentrated on the beachhead.

**Geography.** India-first. International remains strategically possible and entirely unvalidated. **No international SEO or location strategy is to be created yet.** Explicitly rejected as tactics: doorway pages, city-page grids and scaled location SEO. The observed lever on local demand is **one strong local entity** — a claimed and complete Google Business Profile, genuine reviews, consistent listings and a real address.
