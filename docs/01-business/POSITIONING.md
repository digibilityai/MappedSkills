# Positioning — Working Hypotheses

## Status
This document contains **WORKING HYPOTHESES**, not final approved market positioning.

## Core Positioning Territory
MappedSkills may evolve from “digital/performance marketing agency” toward a focused growth partner built around:

**Search Visibility → Conversion → Automation**

## Candidate Positioning Line
**Get Found. Convert More. Automate Growth.**

## Candidate Explanation
MappedSkills helps growth-focused businesses become more discoverable across Google and AI search, convert more of that visibility into customers, and automate the journey after conversion.

## Candidate Service Story
### GET FOUND
AI + Search Visibility

### CONVERT
Conversion Rate Optimisation

### AUTOMATE
Marketing Automation

### GROW
The cumulative business outcome.

## Potential Differentiation
Rather than presenting dozens of unrelated marketing services, MappedSkills could own a simple customer-growth journey.

The differentiation must eventually come from:
- methodology;
- proof;
- original data;
- measurement;
- tools;
- research;
- repeatable delivery;
- visible expertise.

The words “AI Search” or “GEO” alone are not a defensible moat.

## Brand Perception Target
Potential desired perception:
- intelligent;
- commercially aware;
- modern;
- analytical;
- technologically capable;
- clear;
- performance-oriented;
- premium without being corporate;
- bold without being noisy;
- human and transparent.

## Messaging Constraint
Avoid unexplained jargon as the primary value proposition.

Terms such as:
- GEO;
- AEO;
- RevOps;
- entity SEO;
may be useful in search/content contexts but should not make the core proposition difficult for a business buyer to understand.

## Research Required
- Which category term has the strongest buyer recognition?
- Does “Search Visibility” have enough commercial clarity?
- How should SEO and AI Search be balanced in headline language?
- Which markets respond best to this positioning?
- Which competitor positions are already saturated?
