# Service Architecture — Working Hypothesis

## Principle
MappedSkills should avoid becoming a long menu of disconnected services.

The current model is three pillars connected into one growth journey.

# 1. AI + Search Visibility

## Outcome
Help the business get discovered wherever relevant customers search.

## Potential Scope
- Search strategy
- Technical SEO
- Organic search optimisation
- AI Search optimisation
- GEO
- AEO
- Entity/brand authority
- Content authority
- Structured content
- Digital authority / citation development
- AI visibility monitoring
- Search visibility reporting

## Important Boundary
This is one service category, not a promise to guarantee placement inside any LLM.

# 2. Conversion Rate Optimisation

## Outcome
Turn more existing traffic into meaningful business actions.

## Potential Scope
- Conversion research
- Landing-page optimization
- Funnel diagnostics
- UX conversion improvements
- Message/offer testing
- Form optimisation
- A/B testing
- Experimentation programs
- Conversion analytics

# 3. Marketing Automation

## Outcome
Improve the journey after a lead/customer action.

## Potential Scope
- Lead routing
- CRM workflows
- Lead scoring
- Lead qualification
- Email nurture
- WhatsApp automation
- Lifecycle workflows
- Sales alerts
- Follow-up automation
- AI-supported marketing workflows

## Working Journey
**GET FOUND → CONVERT → AUTOMATE → GROW**

## Service Hierarchy Hypothesis
AI + Search Visibility should likely be the acquisition spearhead.

CRO and Marketing Automation should likely function both as:
- standalone entry services where demand exists; and
- natural expansion services for existing Search Visibility clients.

## Validation Required
For every proposed component determine:
- buyer demand;
- search demand;
- buyer understanding;
- price potential;
- required proof;
- delivery complexity;
- recurring potential;
- margin;
- competition;
- whether MappedSkills can credibly deliver it.
