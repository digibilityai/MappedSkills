- **URL:** https://searchengineland.com/ai-recommendation-lists-rarely-repeat-study-468076

## E. Method Limitations of Session 02

**VERIFIED FACT** statements about this session's own method:

1. No paid research tool was used. No search volume, keyword difficulty, ranking, traffic, backlink, or AI-citation-frequency figure was gathered for MappedSkills or any competitor, and none was estimated.
2. Competitor analysis is based on **public website claims retrieved on a single date**. No competitor client, revenue, retention, headcount or delivery-quality figure was independently verified.
3. Competitor coverage is **purposive, not exhaustive**: twelve companies were examined directly (four Pune, three other-India, five international). Pune and India coverage is sufficient to characterise dominant and emerging positioning but is not a census.
4. Category saturation classifications in `MARKET_RESEARCH.md` are **INFERENCES** from the observed evidence, not measurements. They carry no numeric precision and should not be reported as scores.
5. SERP composition observations are recorded qualitatively (what kinds of pages rank) and deliberately exclude volume, difficulty and position data, which belong to Session 03.
6. No AI-visibility test of MappedSkills or any competitor was run. Any statement about who AI systems currently recommend in this category is **UNKNOWN** and is Session 04 work.
7. No India-specific equivalent of the Pew study (S-07) was found. The click-loss evidence base is US-centric.
