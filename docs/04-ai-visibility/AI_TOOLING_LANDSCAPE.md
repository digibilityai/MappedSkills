Assessed against the framework in `AI_MEASUREMENT_FRAMEWORK.md`.

## 3. Could MappedSkills realistically differentiate?

**Not on measurement capability. It has already lost that.**

- The commodity floor is **free** (Ahrefs, Semrush checkers).
- The professional floor is **$99/month**, from two vendors, one of which is Semrush.
- Multi-system coverage — the one thing this session proved is genuinely hard to obtain manually — is precisely what the paid tools sell.
- Session 03 established that tool queries are owned absolutely by tool companies, and Session 04 found that **Google AI Mode itself answers "companies that improve visibility in AI search" with Semrush, SE Ranking and Ahrefs, and answers "who can help my company get found in Google and AI search" by naming Profound**. The AI answer layer is now recommending the tools over the agencies.

**Where a narrow gap does exist** — and it is narrow, and it is a *method* gap, not a *capability* gap:

1. **Variance-aware reporting.** Neither vendor publishes it; the market's own research (SparkToro/Gumshoe) says the underlying data demands it.
2. **Per-system rather than blended reporting.** This session's cross-system agreement result makes blending indefensible.
3. **Interpretation and remediation.** Both tools tell a client *that* they are absent. Neither tells them that the reason is an absent Clutch profile, an empty `sameAs` array, a malformed `postalCode`, no Google Business Profile, and a staging subdomain being cited as a source. **That diagnostic chain is agency work, and it is the work this session actually did.**

**Judgement:** the differentiator is not the measurement. It is the **diagnosis and the fix**, with the measurement bought in as an input.

## 4. Cost implication for any MappedSkills service

Recorded because it is a commercial input the orchestrator needs, not a recommendation.

Any offer promising coverage of the six systems in `PROJECT_CHARTER.md` (ChatGPT, Google AI/Gemini, Claude, Perplexity, Grok, future systems) requires either a paid platform at the multi-engine tier or paid accounts on several systems. This session demonstrated that a signed-out manual method **cannot reach Claude or Grok at all**, and reaches Gemini and Perplexity only at reduced tiers with Gemini exposing **no citations whatsoever**.

**Consequence:** "AI visibility monitoring" carries a recurring cost of goods that a technical-SEO audit does not. Whether the Indian price point Session 02 and Session 03 both documented (packaged SEO from $199/month; Pune pricing content quoting from ₹8,xxx) supports that cost of goods is **UNKNOWN** and is a pricing question for the owner.

## 5. Limitations

1. Only **two** platforms were verified at source in this session. Otterly, Ahrefs Brand Radar, SE Ranking and others were not re-verified and are carried at Session 02/03 confidence.
2. **No tool was trialled.** Every capability claim is a vendor claim read off a vendor page. Accuracy, coverage and citation fidelity are **UNKNOWN**.
3. **Whether these tools' figures agree with manual observation is untested.** That comparison would be genuinely informative and was not run.
4. The comparison listicles that dominate this category's search results are written by competing vendors and were **not** used as evidence.
