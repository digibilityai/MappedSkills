## 1. What this asset is
MappedSkills' own AI-discovery baseline, published with its full method, run counts, dates, location, access tier and stated non-coverage — **including a measured zero.**

## 2. Why it can exist
**It requires no client, no permission, no budget and no case study.** It is the only substantial proof asset MappedSkills can publish today, and it is genuinely unusual: **no competitor selling an AI-visibility dashboard can copy it without devaluing their own product.**

## 3. The asset's job
Proof (5) · source and citation value (4) · authority (3) · conversion support (6). **It is not funded for AI-citation acquisition** — Session 04 found no research asset cited in 28 unbranded agency runs.

## 4. Audience
Evaluating buyers, practitioners and potential citers. **Many will never be buyers, and the asset must still be worth their time.**

## 5. What may be published

Everything below is a **VERIFIED first-party observation** from the Session 04 measurement.

| Finding | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Branded retrieval works** | Identified accurately in **7 of 8** branded runs across the systems that could search the live web |
| **Unbranded discovery is zero** | **0 appearances in 28 unbranded runs**, across four systems and six prompt classes — **including a 16-entry ChatGPT list of Pune agencies and a Pune-specific industrial prompt** |
| **Recommendation and citation are zero** | `mappedskills.com` was never used as a source in an unbranded answer |
| **The four layers are separated, never combined** | Retrieval, discovery, recommendation, citation. **The whole finding would be invisible under any single score** |
| **Presence is retrieval-dependent** | ChatGPT with web search **disabled** did not know the company and read the name compositionally. **No system tested carries it in parametric knowledge** |
| **Access is not the constraint** | Full server-rendered HTML served to `OAI-SearchBot/1.4` and `ClaudeBot/1.0`; `robots.txt` blocks no AI crawler. **Complete access, zero appearances** |
| **Source-domain frequency** | Across the 28 unbranded runs: `agencies.semrush.com` in **10**, `clutch.co` in **5**, LinkedIn in 4, GoodFirms in 2. **MappedSkills has a profile on none of the directories** |
| **Variance** | Membership often stable within a system, **order never was**; cross-system agreement collapsed — **no agency appeared in three or more of the four systems tested** |
| **Category positioning did not produce visibility** | Three agencies selling GEO/AI-visibility positioning appeared **0 times each in 28 runs**, including on GEO-specific prompts |

## 6. What may NOT be published

| Excluded | Reason |
|---|---|
| **Any rank, position or score** | Order was unstable in every repeated prompt. **No blended cross-system figure, ever** |
| **Any trend, change or percentage improvement** | **There is one measurement.** A second round is required before change can be described |
| **Anything about Claude or Grok** | **NOT TESTABLE.** No baseline of any kind exists |
| **Anything about markets outside India, or languages other than English** | Egress was Pune-locked; English only |
| **"Competitor C has no AI visibility"** | Zero appearances in a 28-run signed-out sample is not absence. **Competitor results are reported as observations, never as verdicts** |
| **Linked-citation frequency** | Google surfaces exposed domains but not anchor hrefs. **Unmeasurable in this environment and not reported** |
| **Any causal claim** | The entity defects observed are *consistent with* the absence. That is all the evidence supports, and the entry must say so |
| **Internal or confidential material** | Client names, internal commercial reasoning, and the programme's internal strategy documents are not published |
| **Any exhaustive off-site mention count** | A search-operator query triggered a bot check that was **not bypassed**; operator queries were abandoned |

## 7. Method disclosure — mandatory
Systems tested and the surface used (including `udm=50` for AI Mode) · **signed-out state and disclosed tier** — Gemini ran on a reduced "3.5 Flash-Lite" tier and **exposed no citations at all**; Perplexity's answers were **markedly abbreviated** · the frozen prompt universe, **including prompts the brand was expected to fail** · run counts per prompt · date · egress location, verified · language · what a run recorded.

## 8. Limitations disclosure — mandatory
**39 runs, one day, one ~40-minute window, one location, English, signed out.** **No day-to-day variance data at all — the largest unmeasured quantity.** Two of six charter-named systems untestable. Domain-level citations only on Google surfaces. **Stability is a property of the prompt** — membership ranged from ~100% to ~40% within one system on one day, and a fragile prompt must not be reported with the confidence of a stable one.

## 9. Version and update policy
**A re-measurement creates a new version with its own date. It never edits this one.** Prior versions remain accessible. The prompt set is frozen; changing it starts a new series and says so. **Editing a published measurement would destroy the trend the asset exists to establish.**

## 10. Author and method owner
Named individual, with the method owner identified. **A measurement with no named owner cannot be defended when questioned.**

## 11. Dataset availability
Publish the per-run record in a plain machine-readable form where it contains no confidential material. **`Dataset` schema only if a genuine dataset with provenance is published** — otherwise `Article` alone.

## 13. CTA rules
**RESEARCH** primary; PROOF secondary; PRIMARY **well below the fold**. **No sticky CTA. No gate. No email wall. No mid-method sales interruption.**

## 14. Source requirements
First-party observations, fully specified. Any external source — including the AI-referral-traffic figure — cited with publisher, date, retrieval date **and its own limitations**, with vendor sources labelled as vendor-published. **The Ahrefs 23× and Buffer 185% multipliers must not appear as benchmarks.**

## 15. Visual opportunities
Per-system, per-prompt appearance frequency **with run counts visible on the figure** · source-domain frequency across the 28 runs · a variance illustration showing **membership stable, order unstable** · the four layers with MappedSkills' result on each. **No single score. No rank. No chart implying a trend from one measurement.**

## 16. What would make this asset fail
- Softening or omitting the measured zero.
- Any rank, score or blended figure.
- Implying a trend.
- A CTA that interrupts the method.
- Publishing a competitor's zero as a verdict.
- Any claim about Claude or Grok.
- **Reading as marketing.** If the asset is only valuable as a sales artefact, it is not research and it will be read as an advert.
