# Page Reason-to-Exist Matrix — LAUNCH PAGES

**Session:** 07 · **Date:** 2026-08-31 · **Branch:** `test_branch`
**Status:** **PROPOSED — NOT APPROVED.** Quality Gate 5 open.

**Rule:** no launch page exists without a defensible reason. All **22 unconditional indexable launch pages** appear in §1–§6, and the **2 conditional launch pages** in §6B. The 5 non-indexable launch routes are covered in §7.

**Column definitions.** *Intent* uses Session 03's taxonomy: **T** transactional · **CI** commercial investigation · **PA** problem-aware · **I** informational · **N** navigational/brand. *Funnel stage*: **TOFU** / **MOFU** / **BOFU**. *Proof needed* states what the page must be able to show honestly **at launch** — with the proof deficit assumed, not wished away.

**Standing constraints on every row.**
- The **primary conversion is qualified enquiry creation**, reachable through either surface — a qualified form submission or a qualified meeting booking. Where a row's conversion event reads "qualified enquiry created", **either mechanism satisfies it**; analytics distinguishes `lead_form_submitted` from `meeting_booked` while both roll into one business-level measure.
- The primary CTA is model-agnostic (the free/paid/hybrid entry decision is still open).
- **Proof is prominently available and integrated in context on every commercial page, and is a required step on none.** No page may interpose `/how-it-works` or `/research` between itself and the conversion.
- No page may promise revenue, guaranteed rankings, guaranteed AI citations or guaranteed enquiry counts.
- No page may reuse "300%+ ROI", "₹100Cr+" or "₹10Cr+" (`DEC-007`).
- Every conversion event below is **unmeasurable until analytics and enquiry-source capture are implemented** — a launch prerequisite, not a page requirement.
