## Cross-cutting rules the matrix enforces

1. **Two pages never carry the same primary message.** Where two rows look close — `/conversion-optimization` and the problem page, `/seo` and `/ai-seo`, `/lead-generation` and `/services` — the boundary is stated in both page briefs and is checked at gate item 16.
2. **Levels 1 and 2 of the message hierarchy are identical on every row.** Only the entry point and the emphasis differ.
3. **No row may carry a proof type stronger than what exists.** The whole proof column reduces to: the published method, two own diagnostics, named people, and primary-source citations.
4. **Every row's CTA role comes from the approved CRO system and is not changed here.**
5. **The prohibited-drift column is the operative one.** It is what a reviewer checks first, because drift is how a coherent system becomes an incoherent one page at a time.
6. **A page that cannot fill its row does not get written yet.** Missing evidence, a missing owner input or an unresolved boundary is a reason to defer, not to improvise.
