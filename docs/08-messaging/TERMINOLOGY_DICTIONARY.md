**This is the canonical term list. Future writers use it.** Where a writer needs a term it does not carry, the dictionary is extended by decision and recorded here — **not improvised on one page.**

---

## 1. How to read the columns

- **Preferred** — the term to use by default.
- **Allowed alternative** — acceptable in the stated context only.
- **Avoid** — do not use, or use only under the stated restriction.
- **Definition** — what MappedSkills means by it, in one sentence.
- **Notes** — the reason, and any context-specific ruling.

| **Lead** | *(not preferred)* | On `/lead-generation` — title, metadata and query-matching phrasing | "leads" as MappedSkills' deliverable · "lead scoring" · "lead quality score" · "lead nurturing" | The buyer's word for an enquiry, in one query family | Permitted under `DEC-008` to capture the live Indian phrasing. **No scoring model exists** and none may be implied |
| **Opportunity** | *(restricted)* | Describing the **client's** downstream stage in a measurement explanation | As something the website produces · in any CTA · in any count | A deal the client's own system recorded after an enquiry | Never a website metric. | **SEO** | **SEO** | "organic search" · "search" | "SEO agency" as MappedSkills' identity · "SEO services" as the company's category | The discipline of making a business findable in organic search results | **Say the word.** It is a capability, never the company |

## 3. PROHIBITED language

**Never, in any context, on any page.** Each entry states the specific reason, because a ban without a reason gets argued away.

| # | Prohibited | Why, specifically here |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **guaranteed · guarantee · promise** *(of an outcome)* | Outcome guarantees are prohibited by the frozen strategy, and demanding one is a documented ICP disqualifier |
| 2 | **best · leading · top · #1 · world-class · award-winning · premier** | Superiority claims require a verifiable, dated, third-party basis. **None exists** |
| 3 | **"300%+ ROI" · "₹100Cr+" · "₹10Cr+"** | `DEC-007`. Unsupported, unknown provenance, and verified as being redistributed by AI systems as fact about the company |
| 4 | **ROI · ROAS · payback period** *(as a claim or projection)* | Revenue is reported, never promised. No figure exists |
| 5 | **AI Visibility Score · average AI rank · any blended cross-system figure** | Rejected on the classic SERP, inside AI answers, **and methodologically** — order was unstable in every repeated prompt |
| 6 | **revolutionary · revolutionise · game-changing · cutting-edge · next-generation · state-of-the-art** | Unfalsifiable novelty claims. They fail the "how do you know that?" test on contact |
| 7 | **skyrocket · explode · dominate · crush · supercharge · turbocharge** | Inflated outcome verbs promising things MappedSkills does not control |
| 8 | **unlock · unleash** *(as verbs for outcomes)* | Magic register. "Unlock your growth potential" is the archetype of the generic-agency failure |
| 9 | **360-degree · full-service · one-stop shop · end-to-end agency · holistic · synergy · ecosystem** | The rejected breadth positioning, plus empty abstraction |
| 10 | **performance marketing agency · full-service digital marketing agency · growth agency · growth hacking · revenue marketing · RevOps** | Explicitly rejected company-level positioning (`DEC-005` principle 14) |
| 11 | **results-driven · ROI-driven · data-driven** *(as self-descriptions)* | Unfalsifiable self-labels. "Data" and "results" are fine as nouns; the **"-driven" self-label** is not |
| 12 | **trusted by · trusted partner · your growth partner** | Implies social proof and a relationship status that do not exist. **No client count, logo or rating exists** |
| 13 | **proven** *(process, results, methodology, track record)* | Nothing is proven. One measurement round, no baseline, no case studies |
| 14 | **seamless** | A claim about an experience the site cannot verify. *(Note the deliberate contrast: "the seams" is a load-bearing term here — the point is precisely that the seams exist and someone must own them)* |
| 15 | **CRO** *(in body copy)* · **GEO · AEO · LLMO** *(as positioning)* | Broken or education-dominated in this market; and never MappedSkills' framing |
| 16 | **search visibility · organic growth · search growth** *(as category nouns)* | Owned by other industries in this market. `search growth agency` returns **(NONE)** |
| 17 | **pipeline · funnel velocity · north-star metric · flywheel · GTM motion · book a demo** | US SaaS/RevOps register, and several name stages or artefacts MappedSkills does not own |
| 18 | **"in today's fast-changing digital landscape"** and every variant | The single most recognisable generic-agency opening |

## 4. USE ONLY WHEN SPECIFICALLY JUSTIFIED

**Not banned — these have a legitimate use. They are restricted because the illegitimate use is far more common.**

| Term | Permitted use | Prohibited use |
|---|---|---|
| **leverage** | As a **noun**, in genuine commercial analysis — "the leverage is off-site" | As a verb replacing "use" |
| **optimise / optimisation** | With a real object — "optimise the enquiry path", "conversion optimisation" in metadata | Objectless — "optimise your growth", "optimise your presence" |
| **bespoke / custom** | Where scope genuinely differs per client and the alternative is more words | As a quality claim. Prefer "scoped to your situation" |
| **AI-powered** | Naming a **specific system doing a specific named thing** | As an adjective on MappedSkills' own services. **MappedSkills does not sell AI-powered anything** |
| **data-driven** | Describing an **actual, identified dataset** — "driven by the 28-run dataset" | As a self-description of the firm's approach |
| **strategic / strategy** | Where a genuine strategy document or decision exists | As a modifier making an ordinary noun sound more expensive |
| **solution / solutions** | Where the thing being described genuinely is a solution to a stated problem | As a substitute for naming the actual thing |
| **partner** | The legal or relationship sense, accurately | As a euphemism for supplier, or in "your growth partner" |
| **journey** | A documented buyer path with observable stages | As a metaphor for a client relationship |
| **transform / transformation** | The internal website transformation programme | In buyer-facing copy about client outcomes |
| **free** | **Only once the entry-offer model is decided** (`OWNER_INPUT_REGISTER.md` §6) | Any "free audit" or "free strategy session" framing before that decision |
| **audit** | A specific, scoped, named examination | As a synonym for a sales call |
| **expert / expertise** | Attached to a named person with a checkable basis | As a company-level self-description |
| **insights** | Never on `/research`; acceptable in ordinary sentences where it means what it says | As a content-category name — it dissolves the research/editorial boundary |

## 5. Rules on the dictionary

1. **The dictionary is binding on every page of copy**, and is checked at `MESSAGE_QUALITY_GATE.md` items 9, 10, 12 and 18.
2. **A prohibited term is not made acceptable by qualification.** "Almost guaranteed", "arguably the best", "a kind of ROI" are all prohibited.
3. **A restricted term is justified in the brief, not in the draft.** If a writer cannot state why the restricted use applies, it does not apply.
4. **Metadata is not a loophole.** Where a query genuinely requires a term the body copy avoids — `conversion rate optimization`, `lead generation` — the use is confined to the title, meta description and matching phrasing, and is recorded in the page brief.
5. **New terms are added by decision.** A term that appears on a page and is not in this dictionary is a defect until it is added here.
