## 1. The primary value proposition

> **The buyers already looking for what you sell reach you, more of them enquire, and you can see the whole path.**

Three clauses, in this order, and the order is deliberate: **capture → convert → measure.** It is the approved mechanism (`BUSINESS_STRATEGY.md` §6) stated as value to the buyer rather than as a delivery model.

### Why this and not a sharper single clause

A shorter proposition — *"more of the enquiries you should already be getting"* — is more memorable and **loses the two things that make the position defensible**: that conversion is inside the promise rather than a separate service, and that the result is checkable. Both were the specific failures of the superseded three-pillar hypothesis, and both are what the approved strategy exists to fix.

### What the primary proposition must always carry with it

The **accountability boundary**, in the same breath or immediately after it:

> MappedSkills owns the chain up to the qualified enquiry. It reports through to revenue using the client's own close data. It states plainly what cannot be attributed.

**A value proposition without its boundary becomes a promise.** That is the single most likely way this messaging system fails.

## 2. Supporting value propositions — the minimum coherent set

**Four. No more.** Each earns its place by doing work the primary proposition cannot do alone, and by being defensible under the claims rules today.

### S1 — The joins between the parts are somebody's job

> **CORRECTED 2026-09-01 (orchestrator correction pass 2).** This proposition previously read *"One supplier owns the seams where enquiries are actually lost"*, and asserted that *"the loss is rarely inside one discipline"*. **Both went beyond the evidence** — the loss may be in demand, on the page, in the path, or in the measurement, and pre-judging it contradicts the approved diagnostic. The original wording is recorded here rather than erased.

**The value:** a break can fall *between* disciplines as easily as inside one — between the search work and the landing page, between the page and the form, between the form and where the enquiry is supposed to arrive, and between all of it and the measurement. Where responsibility is split, those joins can end up belonging to nobody. **MappedSkills takes responsibility for them.**

**Why it earns a place:** it is the strongest honest counter to every competitive alternative in `POSITIONING.md` §6 at once — generic agencies, SEO-only agencies, paid-media agencies, internal teams and fragmented specialists — **without claiming to replace any of them.**

**Its limits, both recorded:**
- **It is a statement about responsibility, not a result**, and it is only credible if the site itself reads as one connected system rather than a service list.
- **It must not become an engagement-model absolute.** It does not claim every client buys all three parts, that engagements cannot be scoped, that MappedSkills replaces three suppliers, or that paid media is included by default. See `POSITIONING_MESSAGE_SYSTEM.md` §3.5.

### S2 — The unit is a qualified enquiry, and what qualifies one is published

**The value:** the buyer is not sold volume. They are told exactly what counts, what does not, and who decides — and they get quality reported alongside volume rather than behind it.

**Why it earns a place:** enquiry *quality* is the language that lands with the actual buyer (`ICP.md` §5), especially in the beachhead segment. And publishing a machine-evaluable definition of the outcome is genuinely unusual in this category.

**Its limit:** the website can verify that an enquiry is real, reachable and in context. **It cannot verify value per opportunity — the single most important qualifier in `ICP.md`.** That is handled in conversation, and messaging says so rather than implying the form qualifies on economics.

### S3 — The result is checkable, including the parts that cannot be attributed

**The value:** the buyer can audit the claim instead of believing it. They are told where enquiries came from where that is knowable, and told plainly where it is not.

**Why it earns a place:** it is what makes the primary proposition falsifiable, and falsifiability is what replaces the guarantee this business will not give.

**Its limit, and it is important:** Session 04 tested measurement honesty as a *proposition* and concluded it is **not one** — a business owner looking for enquiries does not have a methodology problem. **S3 is a supporting proposition and a proof principle. It must never lead.**

### S4 — Modern discovery is covered, and sized honestly

**The value:** whether a business shows up when a buyer asks an AI assistant is handled as part of the search work — measured per system, with the limits stated — rather than sold as a separate discipline with a score.

**Why it earns a place:** it is the buyer's live question, MappedSkills holds genuine first-party evidence on it, and it is the one place where the honest answer is also the differentiated one.

**Its limit:** it is a supporting capability and never the company category. It is sized honestly — including that AI chatbots sent roughly 0.28% of total web traffic in March 2026 against Google's 28.12% *(vendor blog citing an unaudited third-party panel; re-verify before any client-facing use)* — and it carries no guarantee of appearance, mention or citation.

## 3. Candidate propositions deliberately excluded, and why

The session brief offered seven candidate supporting concepts. **Using all of them would produce a list, not a system.** Four were adopted above, in modified form. The rest are excluded as *company-level* propositions and reassigned.

| Candidate | Verdict | Where it goes instead |
|---|---|---|
| **Capture existing demand** | **Absorbed into the primary proposition.** It is the first clause, not a supporting item | Primary VP, clause 1 |
| **Convert more of that demand** | **Absorbed into the primary proposition.** Separating it would restore the acquisition/CRO split the strategy exists to close | Primary VP, clause 2 |
| **Know which enquiries came from where** | **Adopted as S3**, widened to include what cannot be known — the narrow version overclaims | S3 |
| **Become understandable and discoverable across search and AI-assisted discovery** | **Adopted as S4**, narrowed. The broad "discoverability" framing drifts toward the rejected "search visibility" territory | S4 |
| **Use paid media where additional demand is justified** | **Excluded at company level.** Paid media is a retained capability removed from company-level positioning (`DEC-005` principle 8). Elevating it to a value proposition would quietly recreate the performance-marketing identity | `CAPABILITY_LANGUAGE.md` · `/google-ads` · `/services` as one part of the chain |
| **Reduce leakage between visit and enquiry** | **Excluded as a separate proposition** — it is a restatement of the primary proposition's second clause and of S1, in more technical words | Problem page · `/conversion-optimization` · S1 |
| **Connect marketing activity to qualified business opportunities** | **Excluded.** "Opportunities" is a downstream client-side stage the website does not produce, and the phrasing imports US RevOps register (`PROBLEM_AND_OUTCOME_LANGUAGE.md` §5) | Reframed inside S3 as reporting through to the client's own close data |

## 4. How the propositions are used

| Context | What appears |
|---|---|
| **Homepage** | Primary proposition + the accountability boundary. **S1 and S2 as the immediate support. S3 and S4 later on the page, never in the first screen** |
| **`/services`** | Primary proposition as the page's argument; **all four supporting propositions**, each attached to the part of the chain it describes |
| **Capability pages** | The primary proposition once, as the bridge from the searched capability; **at most two supporting propositions**, chosen for that capability's buyer |
| **`/pricing`** | Primary proposition before any number; **S1 and S3**, because they are what justify a premium without case studies |
| **Problem page** | **No proposition above the fold.** The page's job is diagnosis; the primary proposition appears only in the commercial transition at the end |
| **`/how-it-works`** | The mechanism behind the primary proposition, plus **S3 at full strength** — this is the page where the limits are published |
| **Research and editorial** | **None, except as one contextual link.** A research asset that argues a value proposition is marketing wearing a lab coat |
| **`/about`, `/faq`, `/contact`** | Primary proposition, compressed. **No supporting propositions on `/contact`** — the visitor has decided |

## 5. Rules

1. **Never state more than the primary proposition plus two supporting propositions on any single screen.** Four supporting propositions stacked read as a feature list and dissolve the hierarchy.
2. **S3 never leads a page.** It supports; it does not sell.
3. **S4 never leads a page other than `/ai-seo`** — and even there it leads as the searched capability, bridged immediately to the primary proposition.
4. **No supporting proposition may be quantified.** None of the four has a number available, and inventing one is prohibited.
5. **The boundary travels with the proposition.** Any surface that states what MappedSkills produces also states where the accountability stops.
6. **A fifth supporting proposition is not added without an owner decision.** The set is deliberately minimal; growth here is how a proposition becomes a menu.
