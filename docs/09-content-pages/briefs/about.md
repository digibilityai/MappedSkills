# Content Brief — `/about`

**Session:** 09 · **Status:** **PROPOSED — NOT APPROVED.** · **Class:** LAUNCH — unconditional
**Page type:** Entity / trust · **IA reference:** `PAGE_REASON_TO_EXIST_MATRIX.md` §6, `NAVIGATION_ARCHITECTURE.md` §2
**No copy or design is written in this brief.**

---

## 1. Page job
Be the **entity anchor** — the page that establishes who MappedSkills is, who works there, and what they can genuinely claim. **Newly promoted from footer-only into the primary navigation.**

## 2. Audience
Late-stage evaluators; **and systems resolving the organisation.** Session 04 verified that branded AI answers about MappedSkills are assembled almost entirely from first-party sources — own site plus LinkedIn. **This page is what those systems read.**

## 3. Search intent
**N / I** — brand and people. Not a commercial keyword target.

## 4. Primary query family
Brand terms only.

## 5. Buyer problem
"Who actually does this work, and why should I believe they can?"

## 6. Buyer sophistication
Medium to high. Anyone reaching this page is checking.

## 7. Desired belief change
From *"an anonymous agency"* to *"named people with a real basis for doing this, who are specific about what they have and have not done."*

## 8. Core proposition
Real people, real expertise, real history, stated precisely — **and a plain statement of where the firm is today**, including that it is building its evidence base in public.

## 9. Required evidence
Named individuals with real roles and genuinely relevant expertise · founder expertise made visible · verified company facts · real, current social and professional profiles · links to the method and the research as evidence of how the firm works.

## 10. Proof limitations
**No client logos, no client count, no testimonials, no ratings, no awards, no invented milestones.** No "trusted by". No years-in-business claim unless the founding year is owner-verified — AI systems report **2016 from LinkedIn**, which is **NOT VERIFIED** in this programme.

## 11. Questions the page must answer
1. Who are you?
2. Who works here, and what have they actually done?
3. Where are you based? *(A real, complete address.)*
4. What do you do, and what do you not do?
5. Why should I believe you, given you cannot show me clients?

## 12. Objections
| Objection | Honest handling |
|---|---|
| "Small and unproven" | **Answered by specificity, not by inflation.** Named people, published method, published measurements including an unflattering one |
| "Where are your clients?" | Do not deflect and do not imply. State what can be shown today and what is coming |
| "Are you real?" | Complete NAP, real profiles, real people, consistent everywhere |

## 13. Differentiation to express
Named people rather than a brand voice; a firm that published its own failures and fixed them; **verifiability as the distinguishing quality.**

## 14. Entity fact set — must be identical everywhere
Legal entity name · complete postal address **with a real postal code** · phone · email · leadership and team · capabilities · founding year. **The same fact set appears on `/about`, `/contact`, the footer, schema, LinkedIn and every directory profile.** Inconsistency here is what produced two AI systems reporting two different service architectures from the same website.

## 15. Internal links in
Header navigation (**newly**) · homepage · footer · article bylines · `/how-it-works`.

## 16. Internal links out
`/how-it-works` · `/research` · `/services` · the enquiry path.

## 17. Primary CTA role
**PROOF.** Secondary: **PRIMARY.** Sticky not required.

## 18. Schema and content implications
`Organization` · `Person` for each named individual · `FAQPage` **only where genuine, with re-verified eligibility** · `BreadcrumbList`. **`sameAs` is currently an empty array on both the Organization and LocalBusiness blocks** — no declared link to LinkedIn, Facebook, Instagram or any directory, which are the exact profiles AI systems used to describe the company. **This page is where the real profile set is established.**

**Author pages (`/about/{person}`) are PHASE 2** — an author page with three articles behind it is thinner than no author page. Team profiles on this page are sufficient at launch.

## 19. What NOT to claim
- **No invented credentials, certifications, awards, partnerships or media features.**
- **No invented founding year, client count, team size or milestones.**
- No "leading", "best", "award-winning" or any superlative.
- **No "300%+ ROI", "₹100Cr+", "₹10Cr+"**, and no unverified on-site claim such as "over half of their clients continue beyond a single campaign", which Session 04 recorded as **NOT VERIFIED**.
- No industry specialisation claim — **MappedSkills is not a manufacturing agency.**
- No rejected company-level category: full-service, performance marketing, SEO agency, GEO/AEO/LLMO agency, marketing automation agency, RevOps consultancy, AI visibility dashboard company.

## 20. Owner input — BLOCKING
Verified company facts · current team members, roles and genuine expertise · founder biography · real company history · which profiles are current · any genuine partnerships, credentials, media features or awards. **`OWNER_INPUT_REGISTER.md` §2 and §3.**

## 21. Visual opportunities
Real photographs of real people — **and nothing that substitutes for them**: no stock imagery presented as the team, no invented office scenes, no logo wall. Where a timeline is used, only genuine, owner-verified milestones.
