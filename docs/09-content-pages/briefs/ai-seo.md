**Session:** 09 · **Status:** **PROPOSED — NOT APPROVED.** · **Class:** LAUNCH — unconditional · **NEW page**

---

## 1. Page job
Hold the **one** AI category term with genuine provider intent, and make AI-search capability visible **without letting it become the company's category**. This is the single most likely page on the site to be written wrongly.

## 2. Audience
A marketer or owner worried their business is absent from AI answers, actively looking for someone to help. Often already sceptical, having read vendor hype.

## 3. Search intent
**CI / T.**

## 4. Primary query family
**`ai seo agency`**, plus its city and neighbourhood variants — Google surfaces `near pune, maharashtra` and `near undri, pune`. **COMPETITIVE BUT POSSIBLE: listicle-gated, not authority-gated.**

**Explicitly NOT targeted:** `generative engine optimization`, `answer engine optimization`, `aeo`, `llm seo`, `ai visibility`, `ai visibility checker`, `chatgpt visibility`, `geo vs seo`. Every one is REJECT or DEFER on recorded evidence. **The question phrasings belong to the launch guide, not to this page.**

## 5. Buyer problem
"We do not appear when someone asks an AI assistant for a supplier like us, and the tools tell us we are absent without telling us why or what to do."

## 6. Buyer sophistication
Medium to high, and **frequently misinformed** — usually by vendors selling the acronyms this strategy rejects.

## 7. Desired belief change
From *"we need to be optimised for AI"* to *"this is mostly a source-authority problem, it is measurable per system, and the people telling us it is a new discipline with a score are selling us something."*

## 8. Core proposition
**The diagnostic chain, not the score:** absent from AI answers → because the sources those answers are built from are directories, listings and third-party lists → which this business is not on. Delivered as entity hygiene, citability, the crawler taxonomy, source-authority work and per-system measurement — **inside the enquiry outcome, not as a standalone product.**

## 9. Required evidence
- `/research/{ai-visibility-baseline}` — MappedSkills' own measurement, including its **measured zero**.
- The **training-crawler versus retrieval-crawler** distinction, which most of the market gets wrong.
- Google's own published position, cited: optimising for generative AI "is thus still SEO", with no new files, markup or chunking required.
- **The counter-example, stated openly:** full crawler access produced zero appearances. **Access is necessary and demonstrably not sufficient.**

## 10. Proof limitations
**No client AI-visibility results.** One measurement round, so **no trend**. **No baseline at all for Claude or Grok** — both must be named as untested. Every observation is signed-out, India-lens, English, one day.

## 11. Questions the page must answer
1. Why is my business not appearing?
2. Is this actually different from SEO? *(Answer honestly, citing Google's position and where non-Google surfaces genuinely differ.)*
3. What can be measured, and what cannot?
4. What would you actually do?
5. How much of this is on my website, and how much is not? *(Mostly not.)*
6. Can you guarantee I will appear? *(No.)*

## 12. Objections
| Objection | Honest handling |
|---|---|
| "Everyone sells GEO now" | Two Pune agencies selling exactly that appeared **0 times in 28 unbranded runs**. Positioning is not the mechanism |
| "Ahrefs and Semrush give me a free checker" | **True.** A tool reports the absence; it does not diagnose the chain or fix it. Say so without disparaging the tools |
| "Prove you can do this" | The published baseline, with method and run counts — including the unflattering result |
| "Is this even worth it?" | AI chatbots sent ~0.28% of web traffic against Google's 28.12% *(vendor blog, unaudited panel, re-verify)*. **Size it honestly rather than inflating it** |

## 13. Differentiation to express
The chain rather than the score · per-system, variance-aware measurement with run counts published · the retrieval-versus-training distinction · agreement with Google's published position while being specific about which surfaces genuinely differ · a published own baseline including a measured zero.

## 20. What NOT to claim
- **No AI Visibility Score, no average AI rank, no blended cross-system figure — ever.** Rejected on the classic SERP, inside AI answers, and methodologically.
- **No guarantee of appearance, mention, citation or ranking** in any AI system.
- **Nothing about Claude or Grok** — no baseline exists.
- No trend, improvement or percentage change — **there is one measurement.**
- **Never GEO, AEO, LLMO or "AI visibility" as category framing**, even where the words appear in a cited quotation.
- **Do not sell crawler configuration as the route to visibility.**
- No competitor named as having "no AI visibility" — zero in a 28-run signed-out sample is not absence.
- No prohibited figures; no superlatives.
