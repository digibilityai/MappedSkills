## 1. Page job
Hold the **residual category intent** for conversion work, under `DEC-008`, while the real acquisition route runs through `/problems/traffic-but-no-enquiries`.

## 2. Audience
A buyer who arrived through the category term rather than the symptom — usually someone with an in-house marketing function who already uses the vocabulary.

## 3. Search intent
**CI.**

## 4. Primary query family
**`cro agency india`, `conversion rate optimization services`** — residual only. COMPETITIVE BUT POSSIBLE, **LOW VALUE**: `conversion rate optimization agency` returns **(NONE)**, `cro services` returns pharmaceutical Contract Research Organisations, and a 2024 listicle and a "Top 1" listicle rank on the head term.

**Explicitly NOT this page's target: the buyer-symptom sentence.** That belongs to the problem page, and the boundary must appear in both briefs.

## 5. Buyer problem
Traffic arrives and does not become enquiries, and nobody can say which part of the path is losing them.

## 6. Buyer sophistication
Medium to high — but **the acronym does not survive contact with the Indian market**, so the page must use it sparingly in metadata where the query requires it, and **never in body copy**.

## 7. Desired belief change
From *"we need CRO"* to *"the loss could be upstream, on the page, in the path, or invisible — and it has to be diagnosed before it is optimised."*

## 8. Core proposition
Conversion and enquiry-path work as **the second half of one outcome**, integrated into the core engagement, expressed in buyer and business language.

## 9. Required evidence
`/research/{own-site-enquiry-diagnostic}` — MappedSkills diagnosed and fixed exactly this failure on its own site. **The explicit A/B-testing scope boundary must appear on this page.**

## 10. Proof limitations
**No client uplift figures, no conversion rates, no before/after.** No industry conversion benchmark exists in this programme's evidence base and **none may be invented**. MappedSkills has no analytics baseline of its own, so **a pre/post comparison is impossible and must not be presented.**

## 11. Questions the page must answer
1. Where exactly are enquiries being lost?
2. How would you find out on my site?
3. Will you run tests? *(Honestly: only where traffic supports significance. Below that, this is research, diagnosis and design.)*
4. What changes, and who implements it?
5. How will I know it worked?

## 12. Objections
| Objection | Honest handling |
|---|---|
| "We do not have the traffic for testing" | **Agree, plainly.** State the threshold reality and what the work is below it |
| "Show me an uplift figure" | None exists. The own-site diagnostic is what is offered instead, and it is real |
| "Is this just redesigning pages?" | The path includes the form, the booking surface, delivery reliability and the measurement — **which is where MappedSkills found its own failure** |

## 13. Differentiation to express
The **honest experimentation scope boundary**, published rather than glossed; diagnosis before optimisation; and the enquiry path treated as including delivery and measurement, not only page design.

## 20. What NOT to claim
- **"CRO" must not appear in body copy.** In India the acronym resolves to pharmaceutical Contract Research Organisations. Its use in metadata is confined to what the residual query genuinely requires.
- **No promise of statistically valid A/B testing** where traffic cannot support significance.
- **No uplift percentage, no conversion benchmark, no "average" figure** — none exists.
- **No fabricated "before"** for MappedSkills or anyone else.
- No client results; no prohibited figures; no guarantees.
- **Do not target the buyer-symptom sentence** — that is the problem page's, and taking it would cannibalise the most winnable surface in the programme.
