# Content Brief — `/faq`

**Session:** 09 · **Status:** **PROPOSED — NOT APPROVED.** · **Class:** LAUNCH — unconditional
**Page type:** Support / entity · **IA reference:** `PAGE_REASON_TO_EXIST_MATRIX.md` §6
**No copy or design is written in this brief.**

---

## 1. Page job
Answer late-stage questions honestly — **and serve as a first-party source surface.** Branded AI answers about MappedSkills are assembled from what the site asserts, so an honest, scoped FAQ is an entity asset, not just a support page.

## 2. Audience
Late-stage evaluators; and systems reading the site for facts about the company.

## 3. Search intent
**I** — long-tail question phrasings, **no single query family.** This page is not a ranking target.

## 4. Primary query family
None. Long-tail only.

## 5. Buyer problem
The specific questions that stop a decision: cost, commitment, timeline, access, what happens if it does not work.

## 6. Desired belief change
From *"there are things they are not telling me"* to *"the awkward questions are answered here, including the ones with unwelcome answers."*

## 7. Core proposition
Honest answers to real questions, **including the ones that lose a poor-fit enquiry.** Losing a poor-fit enquiry here is a success.

## 8. Questions the page should cover
Grouped, and each answered in a way that is accurate when read alone:

**Commercial** — what it costs and what changes that · minimum commitment · whether ad spend is separate · whether smaller scopes exist *(gated on the entry-offer decision)*.

**Outcome** — what MappedSkills is accountable for · what it will not promise · **why there is no guarantee** · how long before anything changes *(12 months-plus for organic, stated plainly)*.

**Measurement** — what is measured · what cannot be attributed · what access is needed · what the client keeps. **The client's measurement layer belongs to the client.**

**Fit** — who this is for and who it is not for · whether MappedSkills works with a given business model *(answered against `ICP.md` criteria, not by industry label)*.

**Process** — what happens after an enquiry · who does the work · what is needed from the client.

**AI search** — whether MappedSkills can get a business into ChatGPT *(**no guarantee**, and why)* · whether GEO is a separate discipline *(cite Google's published position, and be specific about which surfaces genuinely differ)*.

## 9. Required evidence
Links to `/how-it-works`, `/pricing` and `/research` for anything requiring depth. **The FAQ summarises; it does not become the methodology page.**

## 10. Proof limitations
**No client results in any answer.** No response-time number. No conversion or ROI figure. No AI ranking or citation claim.

## 11. Objections
This page **is** the objection-handling surface. The rule: **an honest answer that costs an enquiry is correct.** Every answer must survive a prospect asking "how do you know that?"

## 12. Internal links in
Footer · `/contact` · `/pricing` · `/services`.

## 13. Internal links out
`/how-it-works` · `/pricing` · `/services` · the enquiry path.

## 14. Primary CTA role
**LOW-COMMITMENT.** Secondary: PRIMARY.

## 15. Schema and content implications
`FAQPage` · `BreadcrumbList`. **Two defects to resolve:** the page currently emits schema for **one** Question against a larger on-page set — the markup must match the visible content — and **FAQ rich-result eligibility must be re-verified against current official guidance at implementation** (Project Rule 19). **Do not emit FAQ markup site-wide on the assumption of a rich result.**

## 16. What NOT to claim
- **No answer that promises an outcome** — rankings, citations, enquiries, revenue, ROI.
- **No response-time number** until measured.
- **No prohibited figures**; no client results; no superlatives.
- No answer that contradicts `/how-it-works` or `/pricing`. **Systems reading this site have already been observed reporting contradictory service descriptions from it — consistency here is a correctness requirement.**
- **Do not turn every page into an FAQ.** This page is where the format belongs; elsewhere it is used only where genuine recurring questions exist.

## 17. Owner input
Confirmed prices and scope · the real process · what MappedSkills will and will not take on · business hours.

## 18. Visual opportunities
Minimal and deliberately so. **A plain fit/not-fit summary** is the one element worth showing rather than describing, because it does qualification work faster than prose.
