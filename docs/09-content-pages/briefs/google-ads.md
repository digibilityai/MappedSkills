# Content Brief — `/google-ads`

**Session:** 09 · **Status:** **PROPOSED — NOT APPROVED.** · **Class:** LAUNCH — unconditional
**Page type:** Capability (demand source) · **IA reference:** `PAGE_REASON_TO_EXIST_MATRIX.md` §2, `SERVICE_ARCHITECTURE.md` §7
**No copy or design is written in this brief.**

---

## 1. Page job
Hold existing paid-search demand and equity, **repositioned as a demand source inside the enquiry system** rather than as a standalone service or a company identity.

## 2. Audience
A business with a paid-acquisition symptom — spending on Google Ads and not seeing enquiries it can trace, or wanting to start and not knowing what it will get.

## 3. Search intent
**T.**

## 4. Primary query family
Google Ads / paid-search provider intent. **Not** `reduce cost per lead` — that family is Meta/Facebook/Google-Ads-shaped in buyers' minds and was REJECTED as a target.

## 5. Buyer problem
Spend is going out; enquiries are not traceably coming back; cost per enquiry is rising and nobody can say why.

## 6. Buyer sophistication
Medium. Often already burned by a previous agency, and price-aware.

## 7. Desired belief change
From *"we need someone to manage the account"* to *"the account is only part of it — the landing experience, the enquiry path and the measurement decide what the spend produces."*

## 8. Core proposition
Paid search as a **demand accelerator on the same measurement layer as everything else**, chosen on merit for that client, with the enquiry path and instrumentation inside the scope rather than someone else's problem.

## 9. Required evidence
The method · the measurement chain · honest scope. **The existing page's own claim history must not be reused.**

## 10. Proof limitations
**No client account results, no ROAS figures, no cost-per-lead figures, no spend figures.** None exists in permissioned form.

## 11. Questions the page must answer
1. What is in scope, and what is not?
2. How is spend separated from fee?
3. What will I be shown, and how often?
4. What happens to a click after it lands?
5. What can you not promise?

## 12. Objections
| Objection | Honest handling |
|---|---|
| "Ads are expensive and did not work before" | Distinguish the account from the landing experience from the enquiry path from the measurement. Link to the problem page |
| "What ROI will I get?" | **No ROI figure. No ROAS promise.** Revenue is reported, never promised |
| "Are you a performance marketing agency?" | No. Paid is a capability inside an enquiry system. **This must be unambiguous** |

## 13. Differentiation to express
Paid demand sitting inside one accountable enquiry system, on one measurement layer, rather than being optimised in isolation from the page it lands on.

## 14. Supporting research and content
`/how-it-works` · `/problems/traffic-but-no-enquiries` · `/research/{own-site-enquiry-diagnostic}`.

## 15. Internal links in

## 16. Internal links out
Up to `/services` · across to `/lead-generation` and `/social-media-ads` · down to `/how-it-works` and `/problems/traffic-but-no-enquiries`.

## 17. Primary CTA role
**PRIMARY** — qualified enquiry, both surfaces. Sticky permitted.

## 18. Secondary CTA role
**PROOF.**

## 19. Schema and content implications
`Service` · `BreadcrumbList`. **This page currently emits no structured data at all.** Existing 308 target from `/google-ads-service`; **the URL does not move** — paid-media URLs are retained by the frozen strategy and must not be removed for positioning reasons.

## 20. What NOT to claim
- **No ROI, ROAS, cost-per-lead or spend-efficiency figure.**
- **No "300%+ ROI", "₹100Cr+", "₹10Cr+"** — this page's category is where those claims originated.
- **No "performance marketing agency" framing anywhere**, including in the title and meta description. That is MappedSkills' current, non-performing identity and is a rejected company-level positioning.
- No guaranteed leads, no guaranteed cost per lead.
- No client account screenshots without written permission.

## 21. Dependencies and owner input
Sequenced after `/services` and `/how-it-works`. **Owner input needed on how spend is separated from fee** before that can be stated on the page.

## 22. Visual opportunities
Where paid sits in the enquiry chain, and **what the fee covers versus what the spend covers** — a boundary diagram removes the most common source of dispute in this category.
