# Content Brief — `/` (Homepage)

**Session:** 09 · **Status:** **PROPOSED — NOT APPROVED.** · **Class:** LAUNCH — unconditional
**Page type:** Homepage · **IA reference:** `WEBSITE_INFORMATION_ARCHITECTURE.md` §4, `PAGE_REASON_TO_EXIST_MATRIX.md` §1
**No copy, headline, tagline or design is written in this brief.** Information hierarchy only.

---

## 1. Page job
Make the approved commercial proposition legible in seconds, make a reason to believe it visible, then route or convert. **It is the entity anchor** — the page search engines and AI systems resolve the organisation against.

## 2. Audience
Mixed and mostly mid-funnel: a considered-purchase business owner, or a marketing/commercial lead, arriving from a brand search, a referral, a directory listing or a deeper page. **Not primarily a cold organic entry point.**

## 3. Search intent
**N — brand/navigational only.** `mappedskills`, `mappedskills agency`, `mappedskills pune`.

## 4. Primary query family
Brand terms and the entity itself. **The homepage must not attempt to rank for service or category terms** — every capability page carries its own family, and a ranking homepage would cannibalise all of them.

## 5. Buyer problem
"Buyers who need what we sell are searching. Most never reach us, too few of those who do are worth quoting, and we cannot tell where any of them came from." **Not found → not converted → not measured.**

## 6. Buyer sophistication
Low to medium on discipline, **high on their own commercial arithmetic**. They know what an enquiry is worth. They do not know, or care, what GEO means.

## 7. Desired belief change
From *"another agency that will show me traffic charts"* to *"this firm is accountable for the enquiry itself, and it can show me how it counts."*

## 8. Core proposition
Capturing buyer demand that already exists and turning it into measurable qualified enquiries — **capture, convert and measure as one engagement, not three services.** Industry-neutral. No technique category, no acronym.

## 9. Required evidence
- The published method (`/how-it-works`), including what cannot be attributed.
- MappedSkills' own diagnostics — the AI-visibility baseline and the own-site enquiry diagnostic.
- **Named people.**
- **And nothing else, because nothing else exists.**

## 10. Proof limitations
**No logo wall. No client count. No testimonial. No rating. No "trusted by". No metric of any kind.** Zero permissioned client results exist. **No case-study CTA** — `/work` is `noindex` and out of navigation.

## 11. Questions the page must answer
1. What does this business do, in commercial terms a business owner uses?
2. Who is it for, and who is it not for?
3. What outcome is it accountable for, and where does that accountability stop?
4. How does it know it worked?
5. Why should I believe any of this today?
6. What happens if I make contact?

## 12. Objections
| Objection | Honest handling |
|---|---|
| "Every agency says this" | The accountability boundary and the published method are the differentiator, not the promise |
| "Where are your results?" | The method and the own diagnostics, stated plainly. **Do not deflect and do not imply results exist** |
| "Will you promise revenue or rankings?" | No. State it as a position, not an apology |
| "Are you an SEO agency?" | Capabilities are named without the company being defined by any of them |

## 13. Differentiation to express
One outcome instead of a service list · conversion and measurement inside the discovery promise · the diagnostic chain rather than a score · honesty about attribution · a firm that ran its own diagnostic and published it. **None of this may be described as a moat — all of it is copyable within weeks.**

## 14. Supporting research and content
`/how-it-works` · both `/research` entries · `/services`.

## 15. Internal links in
Every page in the site (header and footer). Brand search, referrals, directory profiles.

## 16. Internal links out
`/services` (primary) · `/problems/traffic-but-no-enquiries` · `/how-it-works` · `/research` · `/pricing` · both conversion surfaces · **`/industries/manufacturing` only if the conditional gate clears, via a removable link.**

## 17. Primary CTA role
**PRIMARY** — create a qualified enquiry, by form **or** by booking. Mechanism-independent and model-agnostic. Sticky CTA permitted.

## 18. Secondary CTA role
**PROOF** — read the method, see the evidence. **An offer, never a gate.**

## 19. Schema and content implications
`Organization` · `WebSite` **without `SearchAction`** unless a real site search exists · `ProfessionalService`/`LocalBusiness` consolidated to **one** entity declaration. **The `postalCode: "India"` defect must be fixed here and on `/contact` together.** `openGraph.locale` is `en_US` for an India-only business and must be corrected.

## 20. What NOT to claim
- Not an SEO, GEO, AEO, LLMO or AI-search agency.
- Not a manufacturing agency, and **no industry framing at company level**.
- **No "300%+ ROI", "₹100Cr+" or "₹10Cr+"** — the current title carries the first of these and **must change**; it is verified as being redistributed by Google AI Mode and Perplexity as fact about the company.
- No guarantee of rankings, AI citations, enquiries or revenue.
- No AI Visibility Score, no AI rank, no blended figure.
- No superlative — "best", "leading", "top", "award-winning".
- No number without sample size, date, location and method.

## 21. Dependencies and owner input
**BLOCKING:** verified company facts · named people · the decision on the production claims. **Sequenced:** `/how-it-works` and both research entries must exist first, because the homepage links to them as proof.

## 22. Visual opportunities *(what is better shown than told — not a design)*
The enquiry chain as one continuous object — discovery → landing → interaction → enquiry → qualified enquiry → opportunity → client revenue data — **with the accountability boundary marked on it.** This is the proposition, and it is a diagram.
