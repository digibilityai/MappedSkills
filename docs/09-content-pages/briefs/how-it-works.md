## 0. Why this is the most important page at launch

**It is the highest-value proof asset MappedSkills can publish today, and the only one that requires no permission from anyone.**

No competitor examined in this programme publishes a methodology. Neither of the two verified AI-measurement vendors publishes run-to-run variance, while both use ranking language. **A published method, including an explicit statement of what cannot be attributed, is genuinely unclaimed** — and it is what has to carry the credibility that a premium price with zero case studies otherwise cannot.

It is also the **root dependency** in the production sequence: every commercial page's proof link, the attribution-limits statement, and both research entries point here.

## 1. Page job
Publish how the work is actually done, what is measured, what is not, and where judgement enters — in enough detail that a stranger can check the claims.

## 2. Audience
Evaluating buyers; **and also journalists, AI systems and anyone verifying whether the claims are real.** Written for the first, and written so the others can quote it accurately.

## 3. Search intent
**CI / I.** **Not a keyword target.** Its value is credibility and citability, not ranking.

## 4. Primary query family
None. It is published for credibility, not for traffic.

## 5. Buyer problem
"Every agency describes a process. None of them tells me what they cannot do, or how they will know whether it worked."

## 6. Buyer sophistication
Medium to high. Anyone who reaches this page is evaluating seriously.

## 7. Desired belief change
From *"this is a black box I have to take on trust"* to *"I can see the method, I can see its limits, and I can check the claims."*

## 8. Methodology stages to publish

Each stage states: what happens · what MappedSkills needs from the client · what is produced · what is measured · what cannot be concluded from it.

| Stage | Content |
|---|---|
| **1 — Diagnose** | Demand and visibility assessment · enquiry-path teardown · instrumentation and measurement audit · listings, reviews and entity check · AI-visibility baseline. **Ends in a prioritised, costed plan** |
| **2 — Instrument** | Analytics, event instrumentation, enquiry-source capture, attribution capture. **Stated as a precondition, not a deliverable — nothing outcome-based is reported before it works** |
| **3 — Capture demand** | Organic search, and paid where it is the right demand source for that client. Technical foundations, structure, content aimed at commercial and problem-led queries |
| **4 — Convert demand** | Landing experiences, enquiry paths, forms, calls, booking and quote journeys — **built and improved, not merely recommended** |
| **5 — Measure and report** | Reporting against a published baseline, with what cannot be attributed stated in the report itself |
| **6 — Extend** | Only when the client feels the next pain, and only within the approved expansion scope |

## 9. What MappedSkills measures
Discovery and source · landing experience · interaction · enquiry · **qualified enquiry — the accountable outcome** · and, using client-supplied data, opportunity and revenue where the client's data allows.

## 10. What it does not claim to measure
Stated plainly, on the page:
- **Revenue MappedSkills does not control** — it does not control price, product, sales capacity, follow-up speed or close rate.
- **A "rank" in any AI system** — order was unstable in every repeated prompt.
- **Anything about systems it cannot test** — Claude and Grok have no baseline.
- **Statistical significance below a real traffic threshold** — below it this is conversion research, diagnosis and design, **not A/B testing.**
- **Enquiries with no usable acquisition context** — direct entry, missing referrer, consent declined, and manually received enquiries carry **no first-touch attribution at all**.
- **A pre/post comparison where no baseline was captured before the work started.**

## 11. The attribution-honesty section
**The highest-value single section on the site, and it must not be softened into a disclaimer.** It publishes the named limitations of the attribution model — cross-device breakage, consent-declined sessions, dark social landing as direct, manual enquiries with no attribution, and revenue attribution depending entirely on client-supplied data — and states the governing rule:

> **A measurement gap never disqualifies a legitimate enquiry.** Qualification and attribution completeness are independent, and the gap is reported rather than hidden.

## 12. Diagnostic logic
The chain that connects the disciplines: *absent from answers → because the sources those answers are built from are directories and listings → which this business is not on.* And in the conversion half: *traffic without enquiries is one of four failures, and measurement is what tells you which.* **The page's argument is that the disciplines are one chain, and that this is why they are sold together.**

## 13. Acquisition → conversion → measurement relationship
One engagement, three inseparable parts. Described separately because they are different skills, **not because they are sold separately** — and the page must not read as three service descriptions stacked.

## 14. Role of paid media
A **demand source inside the enquiry system**, chosen on merit per client, on the same measurement layer as everything else. **Never a positioning claim, never a headline, never a category.**

## 15. Role of AI Search
A supporting capability occupying five roles: delivery capability · methodology · diagnostic component · content-authority surface · proof mechanism. **Highly visible in capability, content and proof; subordinate to the enquiry outcome in the proposition.** Both halves must survive onto the page. **No score, no rank, no guarantee.**

## 16. Role of automation
**A limited downstream expansion capability only** — enquiry routing, notifications, response-time workflows, follow-up, basic qualification. Explicitly excluded: CRM implementation, platform migrations, RevOps, lifecycle architecture, sales-ops consulting. **It gets a sentence, not a stage.**

## 17. Where human judgement enters
Named explicitly rather than hidden behind process language: which demand is worth capturing · which of four failures a symptom indicates · whether an enquiry is a real fit *(the website cannot verify value per opportunity — it is handled in conversation)* · what a measurement means when the data is partial · when to say a channel is wrong for a business.

## 18. Uncertainty and limitations
A visible limitations block, not a footnote: **absolute search volume is UNKNOWN everywhere in this programme** · **there is no analytics baseline yet** · organic is a 12-month-plus engine · AI measurement is one round, one location, English, signed out, with two of six named systems untestable · variance is a property of the prompt.

## 19. Evidence that supports the methodology
Both `/research` entries · the documented own-site diagnostic · primary-source citations for every platform claim, including Google's own published position on generative-AI optimisation.

## 22. Primary CTA role
**PROOF.** Secondary: PRIMARY, **at the end**. **Sticky CTA not permitted** — a persistent sales prompt undercuts the credibility this page exists to build.

## 23. Schema and content implications
`FAQPage` **only where genuine recurring questions exist, and only with re-verified eligibility** (Project Rule 19) · `BreadcrumbList` · **`HowTo` only if the content is genuinely procedural — do not force it.** Existing URL and existing nav slot; neither moves.

## 24. What NOT to claim
- **No branded framework name.** A named methodology is not a moat, `POSITIONING.md` §8 says so explicitly, and **no branded name is invented or approved by this session.**
- No guaranteed outcome of any kind.
- No client results; no prohibited figures.
- **No response-time SLA** until one has been measured.
- No process step that does not actually happen. **A methodology page describing an invented process is fabricated proof.**

## 25. Owner input — BLOCKING
**The real delivery process**, stage by stage: what is delivered, in what order, by whom, what the client must supply, what is measured, and what MappedSkills will not promise. **No Claude session can supply this.**

## 26. Visual opportunities
The method as a sequence with inputs and outputs per stage; and — separately and prominently — **a "what cannot be attributed" panel**. That panel is the single item on the site most improved by being visual: it turns a list of caveats into a deliberate, legible position.
