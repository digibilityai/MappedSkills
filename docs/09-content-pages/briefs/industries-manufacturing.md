**This page ships at launch only if the manufacturing validation gate clears before content freeze:**
1. absolute search-volume verification with a real keyword tool;
2. a healthcare SERP test on the same basis, so the beachhead is a comparison rather than a default;
3. one completed industrial engagement with a publishable result — **owner action; no Claude session can produce it.**

**If the gate does not clear, this page and its supporting article move to PHASE 2 together**, and no other part of the launch content architecture changes.

> **Manufacturing / industrial B2B is candidate vertical beachhead #1. It is NOT an approved specialisation. MappedSkills does not become a manufacturing agency, and no company-level page may be written as though the beachhead were settled.**

## 1. Page job
Capture the cleanest commercial query family observed anywhere in the programme, and speak to an industrial buyer in that buyer's own vocabulary — **without turning the company into a vertical agency.**

## 2. Audience
An Indian industrial or manufacturing B2B decision-maker. **Frequently the owner and the commercial lead are the same person or sit one desk apart, and enquiry quality is the language that lands** — not traffic, not rankings.

## 3. Search intent
**T.**

## 4. Primary query family
**`seo agency for manufacturing companies`, `seo for manufacturing industry`, `digital marketing agency for b2b manufacturing companies`** — 8 variants, 6 explicitly provider-seeking, **zero contamination**. The only family in Session 03 with that property.

**Explicitly not targeted:** sub-vertical phrasings (engineering components, industrial equipment, contract manufacturing) — sub-vertical pages are on the DO NOT BUILD list. Unqualified `seo` and `lead generation` terms belong to the capability pages.

## 5. Buyer problem
"Buyers and distributors are searching for what we manufacture. The RFQs are going elsewhere, and the ones we get are not from serious buyers."

## 6. Buyer sophistication
Low to medium on marketing, **very high on their own product, their specifications and their buying process.** Content that gets the RFQ path wrong will be dismissed immediately.

## 7. Desired belief change
From *"agencies do not understand how our buyers buy"* to *"this firm understands the specification-to-RFQ-to-quote path, and is accountable for the enquiry at the end of it."*

## 8. Core proposition
Demand capture and enquiry conversion for industrial B2B, expressed in the vertical's own outcome word. **All three AI systems tested used "RFQ" unprompted**, and a ranking Pune specialist promises "Get RFQs Fast" — the buyer's outcome is conversion-shaped, not ranking-shaped.

## 9. Required evidence
RFQ-path fluency · specification, dealer and export journey competence · the method · the own-site diagnostic. **Domain fluency is the differentiator available before any case study exists.**

## 10. Proof limitations — the hardest constraint on this page
**No manufacturing case study exists. The page must not imply one.** No industrial client name, no industrial result, no "manufacturers we work with", no sector logo wall, no RFQ volume figure. **Absolute search volume for this family is UNKNOWN.**

**And the honest weakness, recorded:** the observed entry mechanism into this vertical was **a directory or a single listicle — not vertical content.** This page is necessary and not sufficient; the listings and reviews programme is the part of the same job that owned-site publishing cannot do.

## 11. Questions the page must answer
1. Do you understand how industrial buyers actually search and buy?
2. What is an RFQ worth to us, and how do we get more of the right ones?
3. Where do our enquiries currently get lost?
4. Can you help with export and international enquiries? *(Honestly: **no international SERP or AI answer has ever been observed in this programme**, so no winnability claim is supportable.)*
5. Have you done this for a manufacturer? *(**No. Say so.**)*

## 12. Objections
| Objection | Honest handling |
|---|---|
| "You have not worked with manufacturers" | **Answer directly.** Domain fluency and method are what is offered, and pretending otherwise would be discovered in the first call |
| "Our buyers do not use Google" | Engage with how they actually search — including marketplaces such as IndiaMART and TradeIndia, which surfaced in an AI answer as channels |
| "Marketing agencies do not get technical products" | Demonstrated through specification and RFQ-path fluency, not asserted |
| "Can you get us export enquiries?" | **No claim is supportable.** State the limit plainly |

## 13. Differentiation to express
Industrial domain fluency — RFQ paths, specifications, dealer and export journeys — in a category where the observed incumbents are generalists. **Beachhead-scoped, and never described as a moat.**
