## 0. Pricing philosophy

Four things make this page strategically important, and one makes it dangerous.

1. `/pricing` **already exists in production** and is in the current navigation.
2. Session 03 found genuine, under-served demand: PAA asks SEO cost **twice**, a ranking snippet already quotes Pune pricing, and autocomplete surfaces cost phrasings. **HIGH ranking feasibility.**
3. **Transparency reduces poor-fit enquiries** — which matters more than usual, because the target query families attract price-shoppers, students and job-seekers by design.
4. **The proof deficit makes pricing credibility the largest single credibility gap on the site.** MappedSkills displays ₹50K–₹2L/month against zero case studies, no reviews and no directory profiles. **No CRO work fixes this; it is not a funnel problem.**

**The philosophy that follows:** publish enough for a buyer to disqualify themselves honestly, explain what changes the price, and **let the method carry the credibility the price cannot supply on its own.**

---

## 1. Page job
Let a fit buyer self-select in, and a poor-fit buyer self-select out, **before** the enquiry — while giving the price a defensible context.

## 2. Audience
Price-stage buyers, frequently late-stage; and a meaningful share of price-shoppers arriving from cost queries.

## 3. Search intent
**CI**, shading into T.

## 4. Primary query family
`seo services cost` · `how much does SEO cost in Pune` · `how much does AI SEO cost`.

**A PHASE 2 cost article is planned for the informational version of this intent.** The split is: **this page owns the commercial price-stage intent; the article owns the informational question.** If the split cannot be sustained, **ship only the page.**

## 5. Buyer problem
"I do not know what this should cost, I have been quoted wildly different numbers, and I cannot tell what changes the price."

## 6. Buyer sophistication
Mixed, and **price-aware by market**. Packaged SEO sells from $199/month in this market and a ranking snippet quotes Pune pricing from ₹8,xxx.

## 7. Desired belief change
From *"how cheap can I get this?"* to *"I understand what drives the number, and whether my business is in the band this is built for."*

## 8. Core proposition
Transparent bands with the **factors that move them stated openly**, plus an explicit statement of who this is not for.

## 9. What buyers need before they see a number
1. What is actually being bought — one system, not a service list.
2. What MappedSkills is accountable for, and where that stops.
3. **That organic is a 12-month-plus engine** — a price with no horizon reads as a fee, not an investment.
4. That measurement is included rather than sold separately.
5. What the engagement requires **from them** — access, decision-making, content input, follow-up capacity.

## 10. What must be communicated
| Element | Requirement |
|---|---|
| **Bands** | As currently and verifiably offered. **Owner-confirmed only** |
| **What is included / excluded** | Explicitly, including whether ad spend is separate |
| **What changes the price** | Scope, number of demand sources, site complexity, existing measurement maturity, content requirements, competitiveness of the market. **This is the most useful section on the page** |
| **Minimum engagement** | If one exists, stated |
| **Who this is not for** | The disqualifiers, in plain language |
| **What is not promised** | Rankings, citations, enquiry counts, revenue, ROI |

## 11. Qualification and disqualification role
**High.** The `ICP.md` fit criteria are the mitigation for price-shopper traffic, **not the page** — a rising unqualified rate from this page is **expected and is not a defect**. Disqualifiers to make visible in buyer language: low value per opportunity · impulse or price-only purchases · demand that must be created rather than captured · unwillingness to grant measurement access · an expectation of guaranteed outcomes.

## 12. Required evidence
`/how-it-works` — **the method is what makes a premium price defensible when case studies do not exist.** Also: the accountability boundary, and the scope honesty on experimentation.

## 13. Proof limitations
**No client results, no ROI figures, no "clients typically see" statement, no value calculator producing an implied return.** The gap between the price and the proof is real and cannot be written around.

## 14. Questions the page must answer
1. What does it cost?
2. What changes that number?
3. What is included, and what is extra?
4. Is ad spend separate?
5. How long is the commitment, and how long before anything changes?
6. What if it does not work?
7. Am I too small or too large for this?

## 15. Objections
| Objection | Honest handling |
|---|---|
| "Others charge a fraction of this" | **True, and named.** Explain what is different rather than disparaging cheaper suppliers |
| "You have no case studies at this price" | **The hardest objection on the site, and it must not be dodged.** The method, the own diagnostics and the accountability boundary are what is offered. Do not imply proof that does not exist |
| "Can I start smaller?" | Answer honestly once the entry-offer model is decided |
| "What is the ROI?" | **No ROI figure.** Revenue is reported, never promised |

## 16. Internal links in
Header navigation · `/services` · every capability page · `/faq` · footer.

## 17. Internal links out
`/how-it-works` (primary) · `/services` · `/faq` · the enquiry path.

## 18. Primary CTA role
**PRIMARY, with booking emphasised** — price-stage buyers want a person. Sticky permitted.

## 19. Secondary CTA role
**PROOF** — the method.

## 20. Schema and content implications
`Offer` / `PriceSpecification` **only for what is genuinely and publicly offered — no invented tiers** · `FAQPage` **subject to re-verified eligibility at implementation** (Project Rule 19) · `BreadcrumbList`.

## 21. What NOT to claim
- **No price may be invented, changed, rounded or inferred by any Claude session.** Every figure requires explicit owner confirmation.
- No ROI, revenue or payback claim; **no "300%+ ROI", "₹100Cr+", "₹10Cr+"**.
- No guaranteed rankings, citations or enquiry counts.
- No competitor price comparison that names and disparages.
- No fake scarcity, countdown, limited-time framing or manufactured urgency.
- No "starting from" figure that is not genuinely available.

## 22. Owner input — BLOCKING
Confirmation that the displayed bands are current · inclusions and exclusions · **what changes the price** · whether ad spend is separate · minimum engagement length · whether any figure currently on the page is out of date.

**Verified starting point, for reconciliation only:** production `/pricing` currently displays **STARTER ₹50K–₹75K/month**, **GROWTH ₹1L–₹2L/month** and Custom Pricing, with "Starting From ₹50K" in the hero and an FAQ recommending a management fee of at least ₹50K/month excluding ad spend.

## 23. Visual opportunities
**A factor matrix — what moves the price and in which direction.** More useful than a paragraph of caveats, and it is the section that does the qualification work. Also a plain fit/not-fit indication, so a poor-fit buyer can leave without an enquiry.
