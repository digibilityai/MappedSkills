## 0. The binding constraint

> **This page must be materially useful on its own terms. It must contain a real diagnostic sequence a reader can follow without hiring anyone.**

A service pitch behind a question-shaped headline is a doorway page, Project Rule 10 forbids it, **and it will not convert either** — the visitor is problem-aware, not vendor-selecting. This is simultaneously an SEO constraint and a CRO constraint, and it is the reason the page exists rather than being a section of `/conversion-optimization`.

## 1. Page job
Capture buyer-symptom search demand on **the most winnable SERP observed anywhere in this programme**, give the reader a diagnosis they can act on, and let those who want help ask for it.

## 2. Audience
A business owner or marketing lead who has traffic and no enquiries, **has not named the discipline**, and may not know an agency is the answer.

## 3. Search intent
**PA — problem-aware.** TOFU shading into MOFU.

## 4. Primary query family
Three families with one intent, **consolidated into one page** to prevent self-cannibalisation:
- `why your website gets traffic but no leads` — Google recognises the full problem sentence;
- `why is my website not generating leads`;
- `how to get more leads from website`.

**Why the SERP is winnable:** no HubSpot, no Semrush, no Forbes, no CXL. Incumbents are peer-level small agencies. **Results dated "4 days ago" and "1 week ago" rank — freshness is a live lever here.**

**Explicitly NOT targeted:** `conversion rate optimization` and every category phrasing. Those belong to `/conversion-optimization`, and taking them would collapse the boundary that makes both pages viable.

## 5. Problem definition
Traffic arrives. Enquiries do not. **The cause is one of four things, and they need different fixes:**

| Zone | The failure | Typical symptom |
|---|---|---|
| **Demand** | The traffic is the wrong traffic | High volume, wrong queries, wrong audience, wrong stage, wrong country |
| **Page** | The page does not answer the visit | Message, offer, evidence or objection handling missing |
| **Path** | The enquiry cannot complete | Form friction, broken delivery, an empty booking widget, mobile failure |
| **Measurement** | Enquiries may be arriving unseen | No analytics, no source capture, no thank-you endpoint, no baseline |

## 6. Diagnostic framework
The page's core value, and it must be genuinely runnable by the reader:

1. **Is there demand, and is it the right demand?** Which queries and sources actually produced the sessions; whether the intent behind them is commercial at all.
2. **Does the landing experience match the intent it received?** Whether the page answers the question the visitor arrived with.
3. **Can an enquiry physically complete?** **Submit a real test enquiry and confirm it arrives.** A success screen is not proof of delivery.
4. **Would you know if one arrived?** Whether analytics exists, whether the conversion is recorded server-side, whether the source survives.
5. **Are the enquiries you do get the right ones?** Whether the problem is volume or quality — a different diagnosis with a different fix.

**Each step ends with an observable outcome**, so the reader finishes with a specific answer rather than a general sense that something is wrong.

## 7. Likely causes to cover
Wrong-intent traffic · a page that does not answer the query it ranks for · **a form that transmits nothing while showing success** · a booking widget that does not render · mobile failure · no visible or credible reason to act · unanswered objections · price mismatch · enquiries arriving in an unmonitored inbox · **no measurement at all, so the loss is invisible**.

## 8. How the visitor self-identifies
Named symptom patterns they can recognise without instrumentation — sessions up and enquiries flat, enquiries stopped after a site change, enquiries arriving from one channel only, enquiries arriving but consistently poor quality, no idea whether anything arrives at all.

## 9. How search, conversion, measurement and demand quality interact
The page's distinctive argument: **these are one chain, and a break anywhere shows up as the same symptom.** More traffic on a broken path produces nothing. A perfect path fed the wrong traffic produces nothing worth quoting. And **without measurement you cannot tell which failure you have** — which is why measurement is a precondition, not a report.

**The honest correction the page must make:** it must **not** pretend every traffic-without-enquiries problem is a conversion-optimisation problem. In several of the four zones, conversion work is the wrong intervention, and the page says so plainly. That honesty is the page's credibility and the reason it can rank against agency posts that all sell the same fix.

## 10. Required evidence
`/research/{own-site-enquiry-diagnostic}` **as the worked example** — MappedSkills ran this diagnostic on itself and found a form with no `action` and no `method` transmitting nothing while showing success, an empty booking widget, an orphaned `/thank-you`, and no analytics of any kind. **Dated, verified, first-party and unflattering**, which is exactly what makes it usable here.

## 11. Proof limitations
**No client examples. No uplift figures. No conversion benchmarks — none exist in this programme's evidence base and none may be invented. No "average conversion rate" of any kind.** MappedSkills has no baseline of its own, so **no before/after may be shown.**

## 12. Questions the page must answer
1. Why does this happen?
2. Which of the four causes is mine?
3. How do I check each one myself?
4. What do I do about each?
5. When is it a traffic problem rather than a website problem?
6. How would I know if enquiries were arriving and I was not seeing them?

## 13. Objections
| Objection | Honest handling |
|---|---|
| "This is just an agency pitch" | The diagnostic must be completable without contact. **That is the answer, and it has to be true** |

## 14. Commercial transition

The transition earns itself: a reader who has run the sequence and found a break has a specific reason to ask for help. **It must never dead-end in a service pitch.**
