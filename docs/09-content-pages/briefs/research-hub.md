# Content Brief — `/research` (Research Hub)

**Session:** 09 · **Status:** **PROPOSED — NOT APPROVED.** · **Class:** LAUNCH — unconditional · **NEW page**
**Page type:** Research index · **IA reference:** `WEBSITE_INFORMATION_ARCHITECTURE.md` §9.3, `RESEARCH_CONTENT_STRATEGY.md`
**No copy or design is written in this brief.**

---

## 1. Page job
Be the place where MappedSkills' own evidence accumulates — and, at launch, prove it is not an empty hub by opening with **two genuine entries**.

## 2. Audience
Evaluating buyers; practitioners; potential linkers and citers. **Many will never be buyers, and that is the point.**

## 3. Search intent
**I.** **Not a keyword target.** It is an index.

## 4. Primary query family
None.

## 5. Buyer problem
"Everyone claims expertise. Nobody shows their work."

## 6. Buyer sophistication
High. This audience will check the method before it reads the conclusion.

## 7. Desired belief change
From *"another agency insights page"* to *"this firm publishes measurements with their limits attached, including the ones that make it look bad."*

## 8. Core proposition
Original, dated, method-published, versioned first-party evidence — **useful whether or not the reader ever hires MappedSkills.**

## 9. What the index itself must communicate
The evidence standard, **on the index and not only inside entries**: sample size, date, location, method, run counts and stated limitations on everything published here; per-system reporting, never blended; frequency, never rank; adverse findings published; re-measurement creates a new version rather than an edit.

**And the canonical rule, stated on the index:** an original first-party dataset or measured study **has exactly one canonical URL — here.** Editorial elsewhere on the site links to it and adds application; it does not republish the dataset (`CONTENT_STRATEGY.md` §6A).

Plus: **what qualifies as MappedSkills Research and what does not**, briefly, so the boundary with `/blog` is legible.

## 10. Required evidence
The two launch entries. **The index earns its navigation slot with genuine entries, and would not earn it with zero.**

## 11. Proof limitations
Two entries. **One measurement round.** No trend. No client data. Two of six named AI systems untestable. **The index must not imply a body of work larger than it is.**

## 12. Questions the page must answer
1. What is published here, and what is it for?
2. What standard does it meet?
3. Who did it, and can I check it?
4. How often is it updated, and what happens to old versions?
5. How do I cite it?

## 13. Objections
| Objection | Honest handling |
|---|---|
| "Two entries is not a research programme" | **Agree.** Say what exists and what is planned, without promising a cadence that has not been resourced |
| "This is marketing dressed as research" | The evidence standard on the index, and the presence of a **measured zero** among the launch entries, are the answer |

## 14. Supporting content
Both research entries · `/how-it-works` · launch article 1, which links to the baseline as its canonical source.

## 15. Internal links in
Header navigation ("Evidence") · homepage · `/how-it-works` · commercial pages · footer.

## 16. Internal links out
Both entries · `/how-it-works`. **One or two contextual commercial links maximum.**

## 17. Primary CTA role
**RESEARCH** — open an entry, or subscribe. Secondary: PROOF, then PRIMARY **well below the fold**. **Sticky CTA not permitted. No gate, no email wall, no download form.**

## 18. Schema and content implications
`CollectionPage` · `ItemList` · `BreadcrumbList`. **No `Dataset` on the index** — datasets belong to entries that genuinely publish one.

## 19. What NOT to claim
- **No "insights" or thought-leadership framing** — that is agency vocabulary and it dissolves the boundary with `/blog`.
- **No borrowed statistics** anywhere on the index.
- No implied cadence that has not been resourced.
- **No AI Visibility Score, no rank, no league table of anyone.**
- **No "top agencies" list** — MappedSkills has no standing to arbitrate a category it appears in zero times out of 28.
- No claim that the research earns AI citations. **Session 04 found no research asset cited in 28 unbranded runs**, and this hub is funded for vertical authority, sales credibility and client trust.

## 20. Dependencies and owner input
**Written last**, because an index describes what exists. **If the own-site fixes slip, `/research` launches with one entry** — preferable to publishing an unremediated defect list as though it were proof.

## 21. Visual opportunities
Entry cards that surface **method metadata at a glance** — date, systems or scope, sample size, version. Making the standard visible on the index is what distinguishes this from a blog listing, and it does that work faster than a paragraph explaining the standard.
