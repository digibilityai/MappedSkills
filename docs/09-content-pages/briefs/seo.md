# Content Brief — `/seo`

**Session:** 09 · **Status:** **PROPOSED — NOT APPROVED.** · **Class:** LAUNCH — unconditional
**Page type:** Capability · **IA reference:** `PAGE_REASON_TO_EXIST_MATRIX.md` §2, `SEARCH_ARCHITECTURE.md` §2
**No copy or design is written in this brief.**

---

## 1. Page job
Capture provider-seeking organic-search demand from buyers who have already decided the problem is search — and connect it to the enquiry outcome rather than stopping at rankings.

## 2. Audience
A B2B or considered-purchase business that believes it should be found more often. Frequently a marketing lead rather than the owner.

## 3. Search intent
**CI / T.**

## 4. Primary query family
**`b2b seo`, `enterprise seo`** — the cleanest generic-SEO sub-family observed: no course, jobs or acronym contamination, high buyer sophistication, provider-seeking.

**Explicitly NOT this page's target: `seo company in pune` and `digital marketing agency pune`.** Those SERPs are gated by tenure, reviews and Google Business Profile — incumbents trading since 1998 and 2004 with 75–4,543 displayed reviews. **A page cannot win them and must not be written as though it could.**

## 5. Buyer problem
Buyers are searching for what they sell and finding competitors instead — and the traffic they do get is not turning into enquiries they can trace.

## 6. Buyer sophistication
**High.** This family is the most sophisticated in the SEO space. Generic explanation will lose them.

## 7. Desired belief change
From *"we need SEO"* to *"we need the search work and the enquiry path and the measurement, from one accountable supplier."*

## 8. Core proposition
Organic search as **the primary acquisition capability beneath the enquiry outcome** — strategy, structure, internal linking, indexation, technical foundations and content aimed at commercial and problem-led queries, instrumented end to end.

## 9. Required evidence
Technical competence, demonstrable from the own-site diagnostic — crawlability, indexation, structured data, canonicalisation, sitemap correctness, migration safety. **This is the one capability where MappedSkills' own documented audit work is a genuine credential.**

## 10. Proof limitations
**No client rankings, no traffic figures, no case studies.** No search volume or difficulty figure may appear — none has ever been obtained in this programme. **Organic is a 12-month-plus engine and the page must say so** rather than implying speed.

## 11. Questions the page must answer
1. What does this actually involve, month to month?
2. How long before anything changes? *(Answer honestly: 12 months-plus.)*
3. How will I know it worked, and what will I be shown?
4. What happens to the traffic once it arrives?
5. What can you not promise?
6. Is my existing site and its equity at risk?

## 12. Objections
| Objection | Honest handling |
|---|---|
| "SEO agencies show traffic and never leads" | The chain reaches the enquiry, and the measurement is part of the engagement |
| "How long?" | 12 months-plus, stated up front. **Do not soften it** |
| "Can you guarantee rankings?" | No, and the page says why guarantees in this category are not credible |
| "We tried SEO and it did nothing" | The diagnostic distinguishes a demand problem from a page problem from an enquiry-path problem from a measurement problem — link to the problem page |

## 13. Differentiation to express
Search work that does not stop at the ranking; technical depth that is demonstrable; migration and equity protection treated as part of the discipline; measurement included rather than sold as reporting.

## 17. Primary CTA role
**PRIMARY** — qualified enquiry, both surfaces. Sticky permitted. High friction tolerance: the visitor has declared a need.

## 18. Secondary CTA role
**PROOF**, and CONTEXTUAL to the problem page.

## 19. Schema and content implications
`Service` · `BreadcrumbList`. **This page currently emits no structured data at all.** Existing 308 target from `/seo-services`; the URL does not move.

## 20. What NOT to claim
- **The company is not an SEO agency.** The page targets the query; it does not redefine the company (`DEC-008`).
- No guaranteed rankings, traffic or timelines.
- No volume, difficulty or CPC figure — **none exists**.
- No local/Pune framing that turns this into a location page.
- No prohibited figures; no superlatives.
- **Do not sell crawler configuration or technical fixes as a route to AI visibility** — that belongs to `/ai-seo`, and even there it is stated as necessary and not sufficient.
