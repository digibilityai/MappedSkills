# Content Brief — `/services`

**Session:** 09 · **Status:** **PROPOSED — NOT APPROVED.** · **Class:** LAUNCH — unconditional
**Page type:** Core proposition hub · **IA reference:** `WEBSITE_INFORMATION_ARCHITECTURE.md` §5, `PAGE_REASON_TO_EXIST_MATRIX.md` §2
**No copy or design is written in this brief.**

---

## 1. Page job
**Repositioned from an agency catalogue into the page that *is* the proposition.** It explains the whole system that produces measurable qualified enquiries, and it is the only true capability hub in the architecture.

## 2. Audience
A buyer evaluating whether MappedSkills solves their **whole** problem, not one symptom of it. Frequently arriving from the homepage or the header.

## 3. Search intent
**CI.** Not a keyword target. It exists to be understood and to route, not to rank.

## 4. Primary query family
None. **This page targets no query family** — that is deliberate and must not drift. Capability pages beneath it carry the families.

## 5. Buyer problem
The three-faced problem: not found → not converted → not measured. Fixing one alone changes nothing the buyer can observe.

## 6. Buyer sophistication
Medium to high. This visitor is comparing suppliers and will notice a service menu dressed as a system.

## 7. Desired belief change
From *"which of these services do I need?"* to *"these are not separable, and one supplier is accountable for the whole path."*

## 8. Core proposition
One outcome, three inseparable parts — **capture demand · convert it into enquiries · measure the whole chain** — sold and delivered as one engagement. Capabilities sit beneath it as demand sources and delivery disciplines, **not as co-equal pillars.**

## 9. Required evidence
The method (`/how-it-works`) · the accountability boundary, stated explicitly · the own-site diagnostic as evidence the firm applies this to itself · scope boundaries and exclusions.

## 10. Proof limitations
No client evidence. No outcome figures. **The honest scope boundary on experimentation must appear here:** below a real traffic threshold this work is conversion research, diagnosis and design — **not statistically valid A/B testing.**

## 11. Questions the page must answer
1. What is the system, end to end?
2. What is MappedSkills accountable for, and where does that stop?
3. Which capabilities are involved, and are they optional?
4. What does measurement actually mean here, and what will I be shown?
5. What do you not do?
6. Who is this for, and who is it not for?

## 12. Objections
| Objection | Honest handling |
|---|---|
| "This is just SEO with extra words" | The chain reaches the enquiry and the measurement; SEO stops before both |
| "I only want one part" | State honestly that parts can be scoped, and why fixing one alone rarely changes the number |
| "Can you guarantee results?" | No. The measurement is what replaces the guarantee |
| "Do you do X?" | Exclusions are named plainly — CRM implementation, RevOps, lifecycle consulting, standalone development, ecommerce merchandising |

## 13. Differentiation to express
Conversion and measurement built into the discovery promise; the accountability boundary published rather than blurred; **the diagnostic chain rather than a score.** Not a moat, and not to be described as one.

## 14. Supporting research and content
`/how-it-works` · both research entries · every capability page.

## 15. Internal links in
Homepage · header ("What we do" must be a **link**, not only a dropdown trigger — it is currently unreachable from the primary navigation) · every capability page linking back up · footer.

## 16. Internal links out
All six capability pages · `/pricing` · `/how-it-works` · `/research` · `/problems/traffic-but-no-enquiries` · **`/industries/manufacturing` conditionally, via a removable link.**

## 17. Primary CTA role
**PRIMARY** — qualified enquiry, both surfaces offered. Sticky CTA permitted. Highest friction tolerance on the site alongside the capability pages.

## 18. Secondary CTA role
**PROOF**, plus CONTEXTUAL links to the matching problem page.

## 19. Schema and content implications
`Service` · `BreadcrumbList` · `Organization`. Already the richest existing schema on the site — **preserve it.** Existing 308 target from `/digital-marketing-services`; **the URL does not move.**

## 20. What NOT to claim
- **This page must not read as an SEO, AI-search, GEO/AEO or performance-marketing agency page.** If it does, the architecture has been violated regardless of what `/ai-seo` says.
- No service-menu framing that restores the three-pillar structure.
- No guarantees; no revenue promise; no client results; no prohibited figures.
- **No automation or RevOps framing** — automation is a limited downstream expansion capability and gets no page.
- No "AI Visibility Score" and no blended AI figure.

## 21. Dependencies and owner input
**BLOCKING:** the real delivery process (`OWNER_INPUT_REGISTER.md` §9). `/how-it-works` must be written first — this page links to it as its primary proof.

## 22. Visual opportunities
The same enquiry chain as the homepage, **annotated**: which parts MappedSkills owns, which it reports on using client data, and which it explicitly does not promise. A capability-to-stage mapping is clearer as a matrix than as prose.
