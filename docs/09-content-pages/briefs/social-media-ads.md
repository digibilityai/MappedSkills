# Content Brief — `/social-media-ads`

**Session:** 09 · **Status:** **PROPOSED — NOT APPROVED.** · **Class:** LAUNCH — unconditional
**Page type:** Capability (demand source) · **IA reference:** `PAGE_REASON_TO_EXIST_MATRIX.md` §2
**No copy or design is written in this brief.**

---

## 1. Page job
Preserve an existing URL and its equity, repositioned as a demand source inside the enquiry system. **The most modest page in the launch set, and it should read that way.**

## 2. Audience
A business with a paid-social symptom, usually already running something on Meta.

## 3. Search intent
**T.**

## 4. Primary query family
Paid-social provider intent. **This page has the weakest independent search evidence of the three paid pages** and is the strongest future merge candidate — but **backlink data is UNKNOWN for every URL on this site**, so it is retained rather than merged on speculation.

## 5. Buyer problem
Paid social produces volume and clicks, and few enquiries worth quoting.

## 6. Buyer sophistication
Medium, and often conflating reach with demand.

## 7. Desired belief change
From *"we need more social ads"* to *"paid social is one demand source, and it is judged on qualified enquiries like every other."*

## 8. Core proposition
Paid social as an optional demand source on the same measurement layer, **selected on merit for that client and not by default.** For considered-purchase B2B it frequently is not the right channel, and the page may say so.

## 9. Required evidence
The method · the measurement chain · honest channel-fit reasoning.

## 10. Proof limitations
**No client results, no cost figures, no audience figures.** No claim about which channel performs better — none has been measured.

## 11. Questions the page must answer
1. Is paid social right for my business at all?
2. What is in scope?
3. How is it measured against the enquiry outcome?
4. What can you not promise?

## 12. Objections
| Objection | Honest handling |
|---|---|
| "Social does not work for B2B" | Often true for considered purchases. **Say so** — declining a poorly-fitting channel is a trust signal |
| "What results will we get?" | No figures. Measurement replaces the promise |

## 13. Differentiation to express
Willingness to say a channel is wrong for a business — which is only credible because the outcome, not the channel, is what is being sold.

## 14. Supporting research and content
`/how-it-works` · `/services`.

## 15. Internal links in
`/services` · `/google-ads` · footer. **No header navigation slot** — a slot would overweight it against company positioning.

## 16. Internal links out
Up to `/services` · across to `/google-ads`, `/lead-generation`.

## 17. Primary CTA role
**PRIMARY** — qualified enquiry, both surfaces.

## 18. Secondary CTA role
**PROOF.**

## 19. Schema and content implications
`Service` · `BreadcrumbList`. Currently emits **no structured data at all**. Existing 308 target from `/social-media-advertising`; **the URL does not move.**

**Recorded engineering note:** whether Meta Pixel is retained at all is an open owner decision, and if activated it must be consent-gated first. **That decision has no bearing on this page's content and must not be pre-empted by it.**

## 20. What NOT to claim
- **No "social media management" framing** — it is explicitly not an acquisition capability.
- No follower, reach, impression or engagement metric as an outcome.
- No client results; no prohibited figures; no guarantees.
- **No brand-building framing** — the company sells demand capture, not demand creation.

## 21. Dependencies and owner input
Sequenced after `/services`. No blocking owner input.

## 22. Visual opportunities
Minimal, deliberately. At most, where paid social sits in the enquiry chain relative to the other demand sources. **This page does not need a diagram to justify itself.**
