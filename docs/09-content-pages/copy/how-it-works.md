## Section 1 — The diagnostic logic. This is the page's argument

### Heading
Why these disciplines are one chain

### Copy
Two observations sit underneath everything below.

The first: when a business is absent from AI answers, the reason is usually that those answers are assembled from directories, listings and third-party pages the business is not on. We measured that on ourselves — across 28 unbranded runs on four systems in one day, we appeared zero times, while directory sources appeared in 15 of them. The fix is mostly not on the website.

The second: traffic that does not become enquiries is one of four failures — the demand, the page, the enquiry path, or the measurement — and the same symptom appears in all four. Measurement is what tells you which one you have.

Both point the same way. The work at the top of the chain and the work at the bottom of it are connected, and the joins between them are where a result quietly disappears. That is an argument for the joins belonging to somebody. **It is not an argument that every engagement contains all of them** — what a given piece of work covers is decided per client.

## Section 3 — What is measured

### Heading
What we measure

### Copy
Six points on one chain.

**Discovery and source** — how someone first found the business, where that is knowable.

**Landing** — what they arrived on, and from what.

**Interaction** — what they did before enquiring, at the level of pages and actions rather than surveillance.

**The enquiry** — recorded at the moment it is made, carrying its own source, rather than reconstructed later.

**The qualified enquiry** — whether it is real, reachable, from a business context, with a stated need, and not a duplicate. This is the accountable number.

**Opportunity and revenue** — only where the client supplies their own close data, reported as the client's numbers from the client's system.

Every one of those is reported against a starting point captured before the work begins. Where no starting point was captured, there is no before, and we say so rather than manufacturing one.

## Section 4 — What we do not measure, and cannot

### Heading
What we do not measure, and cannot

### Copy
**Revenue we do not control.** We do not set the price, build the product, staff the sales team, follow up the enquiry or close the deal. Revenue is reported from client data; it is never promised.

**A rank in any AI system.** There is no such number. Order changed in every repeated prompt we measured, so a position would be a reading of noise.

**Anything about systems we cannot test.** Claude and Grok returned no testable result in our measurement. We hold no baseline for either and make no claim about either.

**Statistical significance below a real traffic threshold.** Below it, the work is research, diagnosis and design, and we describe it that way rather than calling an observation a test.

**Enquiries with no usable acquisition context.** Direct entry, missing referrer, consent declined, and manually received enquiries — a phone call or an email — carry no first-touch attribution at all.

**A before-and-after where no baseline was captured first.** This applies to our own site as much as to a client's.

## Section 5 — What cannot be attributed. The highest-value section on the site

### Heading
What we cannot tell you about where an enquiry came from

### Copy
Attribution means connecting an enquiry back to how the person first found the business and what they were looking at when they enquired. It is useful, it is incomplete, and the incompleteness is not evenly distributed.

**Where it breaks, specifically:**

A person who researches on their phone and enquires from a laptop appears as two different people. We cannot join them.

A visitor who declines tracking consent is not tracked. That is correct, and it means their enquiry arrives with no source.

A link shared in WhatsApp, an email or a private message arrives with no referrer and is recorded as direct. In this market that is a large category, not an edge case.

Someone who was recommended by a customer, saw the business somewhere offline, or simply typed the domain in, arrives with nothing to attribute.

Phone calls and emails carry no first-touch attribution at all. They are entered by a person, with the source recorded as unknown, and they are never quietly assigned to a channel.

Revenue attribution depends entirely on data the client supplies from their own system. Where it is not supplied, that part of the chain is empty and shown as empty.

**And the rule that governs all of it:**

> **A measurement gap never disqualifies a legitimate enquiry.** Whether an enquiry counts and whether we can tell where it came from are two separate questions. An enquiry we could not trace is still a real enquiry, it stays in the count, and the gap is reported as a gap — because moving it out would make the measurement look tidier and the business smaller.

## Section 7 — Where paid media, AI search and automation sit

### Heading
Where the other capabilities sit in this

### Copy
**Paid media** is a demand source chosen on merit for a particular business, on the same measurement layer as everything else. It is not part of every engagement and it is not a positioning claim.

**AI search** occupies five subordinate roles: a delivery capability, part of the method, a diagnostic that explains absence, a reason to publish properly, and — because we measured ourselves and published the result — a piece of evidence. It is a supporting capability and not what the firm is.

**Automation, after the enquiry exists**, is limited to routing it to the right person, acknowledging it, and reminders around a booked call. It gets this sentence and no more, because that is genuinely all of it. We do not implement CRMs, build lifecycle programmes or run sales operations.

## Section 8 — What the client provides

### Heading
What we need from you

### Copy
Stated as a requirement rather than a request, because the work does not function without it.

Access — analytics, search console, ad accounts, the site itself, and the systems where enquiries land.

Capacity to follow up. An enquiry that is not answered is not a result, and it is the one part of the chain we do not control.
