## Section 1 — What is actually being bought, before any number

### Heading
What you are buying

### Copy
Not a list of services. One piece of work with an outcome attached: reaching buyers who are already looking, making the route from that first visit to a sent enquiry work, and measuring the whole thing so the result can be checked rather than believed.

We are accountable up to the qualified enquiry. Where you supply close data, we report through to the revenue your own system recorded. We do not promise rankings, positions in AI answers, enquiry volumes, revenue or a return on spend.

The measurement is included. It is not a reporting add-on, and it is not optional — nothing we tell you afterwards is checkable without it.

## Section 2 — The horizon, before the price

### Heading
How long before anything changes

### Copy
Organic search is a 12-month-plus engine. That is stated here, before the price, because a monthly figure with no horizon attached reads as a fee rather than as an investment — and because the answer changes whether this is right for you at all.

Some things move sooner. Paid search can produce enquiries in weeks where the demand exists. A broken enquiry path can often be fixed in days, and where that turns out to be the problem it is usually the least expensive thing we ever do for a client.

What does not move quickly is organic search against competitors who started years earlier.

## Section 4 — What moves the number. The most useful section on the page

### Heading
What moves the number

### Copy
This matters more than the bands, and it is the part a quote will not tell you.

**Scope.** Which parts of the chain the work covers, and whether the enquiry path is being fixed as well as fed.

**How many demand sources.** One channel costs less than three, and three is not automatically better.

**Site complexity.** A ten-page site and a two-thousand-page site are different jobs. So are a clean rebuild and a site carrying fifteen years of redirects.

**Existing measurement maturity.** Where analytics, conversion tracking and source capture already work properly, the work starts sooner and costs less. Where none of it exists, that comes first.

**Content requirements.** Whether the pages that need to exist already do, and who writes them.

**How competitive your market is.** Not the industry in general — the specific searches your buyers use, and who currently holds them.

### Supporting Elements
- **Visual explanation useful here.** A factor matrix stating the direction each factor pushes, so a reader can place themselves in a band before contacting anyone. It does the qualification work that a paragraph of caveats does not.
- **No calculator producing an implied return.** No payback period. No "clients typically see".

## Section 6 — Who this is not for

### Heading
Who this is not for

### Copy
Businesses where a single new customer is worth a small amount. The arithmetic does not work, and it is not close.

Purchases made on impulse or on price alone.

Markets where the demand does not exist yet and would have to be created. That is a different discipline and we are not it.

Businesses that cannot or will not grant measurement access. Without it, nothing we reported to you afterwards could be checked, and we would rather not take the work.

Anyone who needs the outcome guaranteed. That is a legitimate requirement and this is not where to get it.

There is no revenue or headcount threshold here, deliberately. Size is not the test; what one customer is worth is.

## Section 7 — The hardest objection, answered directly

### Heading
A premium price with no case studies

### Copy
It is a fair objection and it is the one we get most.

Cheaper suppliers in this market are real, and many of them do exactly what they say: manage an ad account, or produce an agreed number of pages or posts each month. If that is what a business needs, paying more here would be a poor decision.

What is different here is what the fee covers and what it is measured against. The enquiry path and the measurement are inside the work rather than somebody else's problem, and the number we answer for is enquiries rather than deliverables.

What we cannot offer you is a portfolio of client results, because we do not have permission to publish any. Writing around that would be the first thing you should not trust us on.

What is available instead is checkable before you commit: the method is published in full, including what cannot be attributed. We measured our own visibility in AI answers and published the result, which was zero. We ran our own enquiry diagnostic on ourselves and published what it found, which was worse. And the accountability boundary is written down rather than negotiated later.

Whether a smaller entry engagement exists, and what it costs, is undecided and must not be implied here.]`

## Section 8 — Action

### Heading
Get an actual number

### Copy
The bands are a starting point. A real number comes from scope, and scope comes from a conversation about what is currently happening — which is not an evasion, it is the reason two businesses of the same size get different quotes from anyone honest.

Booking a time is usually faster than writing it out.
