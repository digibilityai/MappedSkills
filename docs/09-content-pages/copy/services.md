## Block 2 — The three connected parts

### Heading
Three parts, and the joins between them

### Copy
**Capture the demand.** Reach buyers who are already looking for what you sell. Organic search is the primary route because it compounds and because the intent is already there. Paid search buys access to the same demand immediately. Paid social reaches demand that search cannot yet reach, when the channel genuinely fits — and often it does not.

**Turn it into enquiries.** A visit becomes an enquiry only if the page answers the question the visitor arrived with, the enquiry path completes on the device they are holding, and the enquiry actually arrives somewhere a person reads. Each of those is a separate failure point, and each has a different fix.

**Measure the whole chain.** Where an enquiry came from, what it landed on, what it was about, whether it counts — recorded at the moment it is made, not reconstructed afterwards.

They are described separately because they are different skills. What decides the outcome is frequently the joins between them: between the search work and the page it feeds, between the page and the form, between the form and wherever the enquiry is supposed to land, and between all of that and the measurement. Split across suppliers, those joins can belong to nobody.

### Supporting Elements
- **Scope, stated plainly rather than in a caveat:** parts can be scoped. If you want the search work alone, say so and we will tell you honestly whether it is likely to change the number you care about — because fixing one part while another is broken usually does not.
- **Visual explanation useful here.** The chain with the joins marked, and the accountability boundary drawn on it. It is the page's argument, and prose makes the reader assemble it.
- **Prohibited:** any statement that all three must be bought · any tier or package · any claim to replace three suppliers.

## Block 3 — The accountability boundary

### Heading
What we are accountable for, and where that stops

### Copy
We are accountable up to the qualified enquiry. That is the number we work to and the number we report.

Where you supply your close data, we report through to the opportunities and revenue your own system recorded — as your numbers, with what we controlled and what we did not stated beside them. We do not control your price, your product, your capacity, how quickly you follow up, or whether you win the work.

Some enquiries arrive with no usable trail: direct visits, phone calls, a recommendation from a customer. We record those as unattributed rather than assigning them to a channel, and an enquiry we could not trace still counts as an enquiry.

We do not promise rankings, positions in AI answers, enquiry volumes, revenue or a return on spend.

### Supporting Elements
- **PROOF CTA:** *See what can and cannot be attributed* → `/how-it-works`.
- No contractions in this block.

## Block 4 — Where each capability enters the chain

### Heading
Where each discipline comes in

### Copy
Named by what each contributes, in the order the chain runs.

### Supporting Elements
**Capture**
- **Organic search (SEO)** — the primary acquisition capability, and the slowest. Technical foundations, structure, and content aimed at commercial and problem-led searches. A 12-month-plus horizon, stated before you commit rather than after. → `/seo`
- **Google Ads** — access to demand that is active now, chosen on merit for your business, on the same measurement layer as everything else. Spend and fee are separate things. → `/google-ads`
- **Paid social** — an optional additional source, judged on qualified enquiries like every other. For a considered purchase it is frequently the wrong channel. → `/social-media-ads`
- **AI search** — whether your business appears when a buyer asks an assistant for a supplier like you. On the evidence we have, that is mostly a question of which sources those answers are assembled from, much of which sits off your own site. Measured per system, with the run counts published, and guaranteed by nobody. *(One paragraph. No section, no score, no dedicated block.)* → `/ai-seo`

**Convert**
- **The enquiry path** — the route from arrival to a received enquiry: the page, the form, the booking, the delivery. Diagnosed before it is optimised, because in at least two of the four places demand gets lost, page-level conversion work is the wrong fix. → `/conversion-optimization`
- **Enquiry generation** — the outcome named: more of the right enquiries, with a published definition of what "right" means. → `/lead-generation`

**Measure**
- Analytics, event instrumentation, enquiry-source capture and attribution. It has no page and it is not sold separately, because it is the precondition for everything above being checkable. → `/how-it-works`

## Block 7 — Scope honesty on testing

### Heading
What we can and cannot test

### Copy
Below a real threshold of traffic and enquiries — which is most businesses in this market — there is not enough volume for a test to reach statistical significance. Anyone promising you A/B testing at that volume is promising a result the arithmetic cannot produce.

Below the threshold, the work is research, diagnosis and design: we find what is wrong, we change it, and we observe what happens afterwards, saying plainly that observation is what it is. Above the threshold, we test properly and report the significance.

### Supporting Elements
- **Prohibited:** any uplift figure, benchmark or average · "we tested" language below the threshold · any before/after.
