## Move 5 — The boundary, and the honest channel answer

### Heading
When we would tell you not to do this

### Copy
When what you sell is a considered purchase with a long decision, a small addressable audience, and buyers who search for it by name. Our judgement in that situation is that the same budget works harder in search — social is reaching people at the moment they were thinking about something else, and cheap clicks are not the same as enquiries.

When there is no reliable way to measure what the spend produced. Paid social is the easiest channel to be flattered by, because reach and engagement figures look like progress and are not.

We do not do social media management, content calendars, community management, or brand-building campaigns. Those are different jobs with a different unit of value.

We do not claim one channel outperforms another for your business. Nothing has been measured on your account yet, and a claim made before that would be invented.

### Supporting Elements
- **Prohibited:** follower, reach, impression or engagement figures as outcomes · any cost or audience-size figure · any client result · brand-building framing.
