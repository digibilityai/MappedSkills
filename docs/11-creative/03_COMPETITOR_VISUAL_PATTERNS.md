## 0. Method and limits

**VERIFIED FACT.** Six sites were loaded in a real browser and observed directly on **2026-09-01**. All six are companies already examined in Session 02, so no new competitor was introduced.

**Limits:**
- **Entry screens only.** Scroll was blocked or unreliable on several pages; no consent banner was accepted. These are first-impression findings, which is the right unit for a convention check and is nevertheless a real limit.
- **Desktop only**, 1440×900, one location, one date. No mobile observation.
- **Six sites is a purposive sample.** It supports statements of the form "this convention is present across otherwise dissimilar firms". It supports **no** claim about how common a pattern is in the market, and none is made.
- **No competitor claim observed on any page is treated as true.** Figures quoted below are recorded as **COMPETITOR CLAIM** — the label already used in Session 02 — because the *presence and visual treatment* of a claim is the finding, not its truth.

## 1. What was actually observed

### 1.1 upGrowth — `upgrowth.in` *(Pune)*
Dark navy hero split diagonally against white. Orange as the single brand accent. **A flat vector illustration of a businessman in a suit, briefcase in hand, striding up a staircase formed by an ascending arrow.** Headline "Growth at the Intersection of Digital and AI". A promotional bar above the navigation offering a free AI diagnosis in four minutes. A security notice pinned to the bottom of the viewport warning about scammers impersonating the company.

**Devices present:** growth arrow · aspirational business-person illustration · dark/light diagonal split · single hot accent · free-diagnostic bar · "AI" in the headline.

### 1.2 Rise at Seven — `riseatseven.com` *(UK/US)*
Full-bleed hero built on a **blurred video of a client product** (a Red Bull can), with very large white display type set over it: "We Create Category Leaders". Above the headline, a centred row of **award laurels** under the superlative "#1 MOST RECOMMENDED CONTENT MARKETING AGENCY". Below it, a **row of platform logos** — Google, ChatGPT, Gemini, TikTok, YouTube, Pinterest, GIPHY, Reddit, Amazon — under the line "on every searchable platform". Deep navy-blue gradient wash. A promotional strip at the top: "The Category Leaderboard - Live Now".

**Devices present:** superlative headline · award laurels · dark gradient · giant type over video · **platform logo wall** · leaderboard asset as an acquisition device.

*(The platform logo row is a variant worth naming: it is a logo wall of surfaces rather than of clients. It borrows the credibility grammar of a client wall without requiring clients.)*

### 1.3 iPullRank — `ipullrank.com` *(US enterprise)*
**Notably outside the dark-gradient convention.** Warm off-white ground, hard black hairline rules forming an explicit grid, condensed uppercase display type at two weights: "DON'T OPTIMIZE FOR AN ALGORITHM." in grey, then "ENGINEER FOR AN EMPIRE." in heavy black. Orange primary button with a hard offset shadow. Beneath the hero, a rule-separated row of **monospace-styled service labels**: `GEO`, `+CONTENT STRATEGY & MARKETING`, `++TECHNICAL SEO`. The right half of the hero is a circular-masked **AI-generated retro-futurist collage** — a figure viewed from behind, facing a stylised city of screens and interface fragments.

**Devices present:** editorial/technical grid · mono service labels · high-contrast condensed display type · **AI-generated collage imagery** · empire/conquest metaphor.

### 1.4 Conversion — `conversion.com` *(UK/NA)*
Pure white ground, pure black type, no imagery at all above the fold. An eyebrow line in small caps: "THE WORLD'S LEADING DEDICATED CRO AGENCY". H1 "Better results with evidence". Two short paragraphs, the second ending in **"...helped our clients generate over $2 billion in additional revenue" (COMPETITOR CLAIM)**. A single black rectangular button. **The entire right half of the viewport is empty.** Immediately below, a black band beginning "Trusted by" — a client logo wall.

**Devices present:** monochrome minimalism · half-empty hero · superlative eyebrow · **very large aggregate revenue figure in body copy** · client logo wall on black.

### 1.5 Profound — `tryprofound.com` *(AI-visibility SaaS)*
Pure black ground. A thin dashed grid drawn as visible layout guides down the page. Centred hero: "Marketing agents to win in" followed by an animated, **motion-blurred rotating platform name** ("ChatGPT" at the moment of capture). Two adjacent pill buttons, "Get a Demo" and "Get Started". A promotional strip: "The Profound Index — Understand where you stand in AI Search in your industry".

**Devices present:** near-black ground · dashed technical grid overlay · centred hero · **animated rotating platform names** · demo-led CTA pair · a proprietary named "Index".

### 1.6 DigiMark — `digimarkagency.com` *(Bengaluru)*
A **desaturated stock photograph of a smiling office team around a laptop**, filling the hero, darkened for legibility. Gold/amber accent. Headline "Digital Marketing Agency in Bangalore for SEO, CRO and AI Search Growth". Supporting copy with inline highlighted claims: **"11+ years", "100+ clients", "3x leads" (all COMPETITOR CLAIM)**. **Devices present:** stock office-team photography · city-in-headline SEO framing · inline highlighted metric claims · **third-party rating badge row** · free-strategy CTA · persistent phone number · floating enquiry pill.

## 2. What is now generic

Stated only where the pattern appeared across firms with little else in common.

| Generic pattern | Observed on | Why it no longer signals anything |
|---|---|---|
| **Dark ground / dark gradient as the credibility device** | Rise at Seven, Profound; and on the non-competitor references Linear, Perplexity, Kagi | Six companies in three different businesses arrived at the same ground colour. It has stopped carrying information |
| **The superlative eyebrow** — "#1", "World's leading", "India's #1" | Rise at Seven, Conversion; Session 02 records the same shape at SEOTonic and Brainmine | Every firm claims it, so it reads as a category password rather than a differentiator. **Prohibited for MappedSkills anyway** (`POSITIONING.md` §5 item 6) |
| **The credibility wall** — clients, awards, platforms or rating badges as a horizontal strip near the fold | Rise at Seven (awards + platforms), Conversion (clients), DigiMark (rating badges) | The *slot* is a convention independent of what fills it. Rise at Seven filling it with platform logos rather than clients shows the slot is what matters |
| **A large unqualified number in or near the hero** | Conversion ("$2 billion"), DigiMark ("3x leads", "100+ clients", "11+ years") | Universal, unfalsifiable, and unverifiable at a glance |
| **The free diagnostic / free strategy as the primary CTA** | upGrowth, DigiMark; Profound's demo pair is the SaaS equivalent | The offer is category-standard, so it differentiates nothing |
| **"AI" as a headline modifier** | upGrowth, Profound; Session 02 records the same at Brainmine and SEOTonic | Present in the headline of firms doing very different work |
| **The centred hero with a pill CTA** | Profound, Kagi, Perplexity | The default output of the current front-end toolchain |
| **Motion applied to the headline** | Profound (rotating blur), Linear (blur-in) | Decorative by construction: it animates the one thing that most needs to be readable immediately |

## 3. What makes agencies look interchangeable

Six visual claims, each of which any competitor could put up tomorrow:

1. **A superlative about rank or scale.** It requires no evidence, so it conveys none.
2. **An aggregate result figure with no sample, date, method or denominator.** "$2 billion in additional revenue", "3x leads". The number's *size* is doing the work, which means the reader is being asked to be impressed rather than to check.
3. **A credibility wall of any kind.** Logos, laurels, platforms, star ratings — the reader recognises the slot before reading its contents.
4. **A metaphor of ascent.** upGrowth's staircase-arrow; iPullRank's "empire". `VOICE_AND_TONE.md` §9 already prohibits the flight/space and war/combat registers in copy; the same prohibition must bind the imagery.
5. **A service list rendered as equal-weight tiles.** A menu, not an argument. Homepage Block 5 already prohibits it for MappedSkills.
6. **"AI" as an adjective attached to an existing service.** Session 02 named this the "headline veneer" pattern; it is visible in the layout as much as in the words.

## 4. Where the whitespace is

Five positions that were **not observed on any of the six competitor entry screens**. Each is a design opportunity that exists *because* it is expensive in honesty rather than in craft.

1. **Drawn absence.** No competitor showed a gap, a limit, an unmeasured region or a system it could not test. Every visual was an assertion of presence. **A firm that draws its own gaps would be doing something none of these six does** — and doing it credibly requires having published the gap first, which is why it is not cheaply copyable.

2. **A visible evidence apparatus.** Not one of the six attached a date, a sample size, a method or a scope note to any claim on its entry screen. `Conversion`'s "$2 billion" is the clearest case: the largest number observed anywhere, carrying nothing.

3. **Per-system separation.** Rise at Seven's platform row and Profound's rotating platform name both **merge** surfaces into one undifferentiated impression of coverage. MappedSkills' approved position (`AI_SEARCH_LANGUAGE.md` §6) requires the opposite — four layers and four systems kept visually separate, with two marked as untestable. **The blending is the convention; the separation is the opening.**

4. **Light-ground technical seriousness.** Only iPullRank was observed escaping the dark convention, and it did so with a grid-and-rules editorial approach — which is evidence that light-ground technical credibility is achievable and is currently thinly occupied.

5. **The enquiry as the designed object.** Every entry screen ended in a CTA to *start a process* — a demo, a diagnosis, a strategy. None treated the enquiry itself, or what happens after it, as something worth designing. MappedSkills' entire proposition is that the enquiry is the unit.

## 5. What signals credibility to this buyer, and what signals commodity

Constrained to what was observed, plus what the approved strategy already establishes about this buyer.

**Signals credibility to a considered-purchase Indian buyer — OBSERVED:**
- **A visible, real phone number and an immediate contact affordance.** DigiMark places the number top-right under "CALL ANYTIME" and pins an "Enquire Now" pill. This is not a cliché to be designed away — `PAGE_TYPE_CRO_RULES.md` §10 already requires phone and WhatsApp to be "genuinely prominent" for the industrial segment, and homepage Block 8 requires a DIRECT CTA "always visible".
- **Third-party rating badges** (Google, Justdial, Trustpilot). **VERIFIED FACT (Session 02/04):** MappedSkills has none of these, and their absence is a documented gap, not a stylistic choice. A designer must not fill the slot with something else.
- **Specificity about place.** Locality read as real-business evidence on the Indian pages observed.

**Signals hype or commodity positioning:**
- Any superlative; any large round number without provenance; stock office-team photography; free-strategy CTAs; "AI" as a headline modifier; a growth arrow; a service tile grid; dark gradients with neon accents; motion applied to text.

## 7. MappedSkills' own current visual state — repository evidence

**VERIFIED FACT (repository inspection, 2026-09-01).** Recorded because the starting point is a constraint on the direction. **Read-only; nothing was modified.**

- `app/globals.css` and `styles/globals.css` are an **unmodified shadcn/ui default token set**: `--background: oklch(1 0 0)`, `--foreground: oklch(0.145 0 0)`, `--primary: oklch(0.205 0 0)`, `--radius: 0.625rem`, and a five-colour default chart ramp. **Every neutral has chroma 0** — the palette is achromatic-by-default rather than by decision, and there is no brand hue anywhere in the token set.
- Type is `--font-sans: 'Geist'` with `--font-mono: 'Geist Mono'`. A competent pairing, and also the default of the toolchain and the register of the SaaS category being avoided.
- A full `.dark` token block exists, so dark mode is wired but carries no strategic decision.
- The component inventory is the generic agency kit: `HeroDashboard.tsx`, `SocialProofStrip.tsx`, `StatCard.tsx`, `TestimonialCard.tsx`, `BeforeAfterMetric.tsx`, `CaseStudyCard.tsx`, `ValueCard.tsx`, `ProcessStepCard.tsx`, `IndustryCard.tsx`, `ProblemCard.tsx`, `ServiceCard.tsx`, plus a 60-file `components/ui` shadcn set.

**What this means for the direction, in one line:** the existing codebase is *built out of the exact primitives this strategy rejects* — a dashboard hero, a social-proof strip, stat cards, testimonial cards, before/after metrics and a twelve-card catalogue. **INFERENCE:** left to defaults, this stack will reproduce the category. Escaping it is a token-and-composition decision, not a colour swap.

**Components whose continued existence is a strategic risk, listed for the UX and design phases — no action taken in this session and none recommended here:** `HeroDashboard`, `SocialProofStrip`, `StatCard`, `TestimonialCard`, `BeforeAfterMetric`, `CaseStudyCard`. Each renders a proof container MappedSkills cannot currently fill.
