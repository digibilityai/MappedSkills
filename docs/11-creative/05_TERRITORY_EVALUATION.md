## 0. How to read this, and what a score is not

**Scale.** 1 = actively works against this criterion · 2 = weak · 3 = adequate · 4 = strong · 5 = a genuine strength of the territory.

**Scores are relative to each other, not absolute.** A 3 is not a failure; it means the territory is competent at that thing and another territory is better.

**Two commitments, stated before the numbers:**

1. **The scores were not tuned to produce a winner.** T4 scores 5 on three criteria where it genuinely beats the recommended territory — buyer comprehension, implementation realism and accessibility potential — and those are not trivial criteria. T2 scores 5 on buyer comprehension, conversion expression, motion potential and memorability. Both facts are recorded rather than smoothed away.
2. **The totals are a summary, not the decision.** Some criteria are load-bearing for this business and some are not; §3 states which, and the recommendation rests on those rather than on the arithmetic.
