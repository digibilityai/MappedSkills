## 1. The recommendation

> **Adopt T1 — THE RECORD — as the governing creative territory for the MappedSkills website.**

The site behaves like the working record of a firm that measures things: kept, dated, revised in the open, and deliberately left available for inspection. Two registers run throughout — a plain, commercially direct **argument** register that leads every page, and an **apparatus** register carrying provenance, status, dates and limits. Structure comes from rules, space and a measured reading column rather than from cards. Colour is nearly absent and does one job: distinguishing what is known from what is not. Explanatory weight is carried by a small number of high-quality figures rather than by decoration.

**Scores:** originally 90/100; **92/100 after the 2026-09-01 refinement** (India-first 3→4, buyer comprehension 4→5, conditional on refinements R1–R10 being binding). **30/30 on the six load-bearing criteria**, unchanged. See `05_TERRITORY_EVALUATION.md` §6.

**The refined thesis, which governs this recommendation:**

> **THE RECORD — A WORKING RECORD, NOT AN ARCHIVE.** The site is the working record of a firm that **finds what is broken, fixes it, and records what changed**. It is kept by people who intervene, not by observers. **Commercial meaning leads every screen; the evidence stands beside it, always present and never in front.** Its energy comes from consequence and contrast — a problem that costs money, an action taken, a dated change — never from colour, motion, scale or claim.

**Territory name:** retained, with a **binding implementation qualifier** — it is a working record, not an archive, a document, a publication, a periodical or a report, and must not be styled as one (`16` §7; anti-pattern B31).

### What is absorbed from the other territories, and what is not

The recommendation is for **one** territory. Two devices from the others survive inside it, in strictly bounded roles. Stating the bounds is the point — an unbounded borrowing would reintroduce the risk that caused the territory to be rejected.

| Device | Origin | Permitted role inside The Record | **Explicitly not permitted** |
|---|---|---|---|
| **The run** — the continuous traced route from demand to qualified enquiry | T2 | **A figure.** The site's single most important explanatory diagram, used in a small, defined set of places, always with a complete static state | It is **not** the site's structure, **not** ambient, **not** a navigation model, **not** a background pattern, and **not** present on pages that do not need it. The moment it becomes the site's identity, the funnel risk returns |
| **The status vocabulary** — measured · observed · inferred · owner-supplied · unmeasured · not testable · unattributed | T3 | **A component-level system inside the evidence apparatus**, applied to *evidence* | It is **not** applied to every sentence, **not** a sitewide grading system, **not** aggregated into any summary figure, coverage percentage or score, and **never** rendered as a traffic light |
| **Plainness of the first screen** | T4 | **The argument register.** Every page's first screen is plain, large, direct and commercial before any apparatus appears | Plainness does **not** govern below the first screen. That is where the evidence lives |

## 2. Why it best expresses the approved strategy

Each point ties to a frozen or approved document. None is a preference.

**1. It is the only territory whose credibility mechanism is provenance rather than assertion.**
`MESSAGING_ARCHITECTURE.md` §1.2 states the proof deficit is "the binding constraint, not the positioning", and §3 rule 3 states "the honest response to a thin evidence layer is specificity — method, dates, run counts, named people — not volume of assertion." The Record is that sentence rendered as a design system. Every other territory has to *avoid* the proof problem; this one is built out of it.

**2. It makes the accountability boundary a visible object rather than a paragraph.**
Homepage Block 4 requires the boundary to be stated "high on the page and stated as a position", and explicitly prohibits "a disclaimer typeface treatment". The Record makes that prohibition structural: limits are set at the same type size and visual quality as claims, because the apparatus register is designed to that standard everywhere.

**3. It resolves the owner's "not text-heavy" requirement without weakening honesty.**
Provenance moves out of the prose and into the apparatus. The reading column gets shorter; the honesty gets more visible. This is the mechanism described in `01_CREATIVE_DIRECTION.md` §4 and it is the single strongest practical argument for the territory.

**4. It carries the IA as approved.**
22 unconditional indexable launch pages including `/how-it-works` — "the highest-value proof asset MappedSkills can publish and the root dependency of the entire content programme" — plus `/research`, two research entries and three or four articles. The Record scores 5 on editorial scalability; T2 scores 1.

**5. It keeps AI search subordinate by construction.**
`AI_SEARCH_LANGUAGE.md` §7 permits AI search on the homepage only as "one named capability among several, in one clause" and prohibits an AI section, hero or hype block. In The Record, AI systems are named plainly in the same register as any other discovery surface, and the only place their treatment is distinctive is on `/ai-seo`, where **per-system separation** — including two systems marked *not testable* — is the visual signature. That is the approved strategy drawn, not decorated.

**6. It does not reduce the company to SEO.**
No page's identity is a technique. Pages differ by the question their figure answers, so `/seo` is visibly a segment of one system rather than the company's subject — which is the architectural test in `SEARCH_ARCHITECTURE.md` §1 and `MESSAGING_ARCHITECTURE.md` §2, restated visually.

**7. It survives without proof, and improves when proof arrives.**
Today the apparatus carries first-party measurements, the published method, named people and stated limits. When a client outcome library exists (`POSITIONING.md` §8), it drops into the same apparatus with the same provenance discipline — and will look *more* credible than a competitor's case study precisely because the discipline was already there.

## 3. Why the primary buyer is likely to trust it

The primary buyer is a founder, owner, MD or marketing lead at a considered-purchase business, who — per `ICP.md` §5 — is accountable for "the whole number", is convinced by "plain arithmetic", has "low tolerance for jargon", and, per `POSITIONING.md` §3, has been let down before.

- **They have seen the category's claims and discounted them.** Every competitor entry screen observed on 2026-09-01 asserted a superlative, a large number, or a wall of credibility marks. A buyer who has been let down reads those as noise. A dated, sourced, checkable statement is the one thing in the category they have not already been shown.
- **They are being asked to spend ₹50K–₹2L per month with no case studies.** `PAGE_TYPE_CRO_RULES.md` §4 calls `/pricing` "the largest credibility gap on the site". The only honest answer to that gap is method and provenance, and The Record is the only territory that displays them as a matter of course rather than as a special section.
- **The register can be plain, because the argument register is plain.** The apparatus does not have to be understood to be reassuring. A buyer who never opens a single evidence disclosure still registers that the disclosures are there — and that is most of the effect.
- **It does not ask them to admire anything.** No performance, no ascent metaphor, no adjective they have heard from four other agencies this quarter.

## 4. Why it is commercially stronger than the alternatives

- **Against T2 (The Run):** T2 wins the first five seconds and loses the evaluation. It cannot carry `/how-it-works`, `/research` or editorial — 8 of the 22 launch pages have no home in it — and its principal risk is becoming a funnel, which the brief prohibits. Its best idea survives as a figure inside T1 with the risk bounded.
- **Against T3 (The Standing):** T3 matches T1 on evidence and scores **1 on SaaS drift resistance**, because a universal certainty system rendered visually is a compliance dashboard waiting to happen — and a score is a product this firm has explicitly rejected on the classic SERP, inside AI answers, and methodologically. Its best idea survives as a component-level vocabulary applied to evidence only.
- **Against T4 (The Straight Answer):** T4 is cheaper, more accessible and better matched to Indian directness — and it **fails the swap test**, scoring 1 on differentiation and 2 on evidence. `Conversion` was observed executing it on 2026-09-01: immaculate, monochrome, and interchangeable. For a firm with a proof deficit, minimalism reads as emptiness rather than restraint. Its best idea — plainness — survives as the argument register governing every first screen.

## 5. What makes it distinctive in execution

Distinctiveness comes from four decisions, none of which is a style choice, and each of which a competitor would find expensive to copy for reasons of honesty rather than craft.

1. **A visible evidence apparatus, designed to the same standard as the prose.** Observed on **none** of the six competitor entry screens. Copying it requires having provenance to put in it.
2. **Drawn absence.** A measured zero, an untested system, an unattributed enquiry and an unmeasured stretch are four visually distinct states, and none of them is a blank. `Conversion`'s "$2 billion" carrying nothing is the category's opposite. **A competitor cannot copy a drawn gap without publishing a gap.**
3. **Per-system separation as a visual signature.** Rise at Seven's platform row and Profound's rotating platform name both merge surfaces into an impression of coverage. MappedSkills separates them and marks two as untestable. The separation is the strategy and it is visible at a glance.
4. **Numbers that always arrive with their provenance attached.** No counter, no number in a heading, no figure without sample, date, location and method. The absence of the category's loudest device, replaced by something more specific, is itself recognisable.

**The single sharpest expression of all four, and the thing worth building first:** the firm's own AI-visibility baseline — four systems, 28 runs, one dated day, Pune, English, signed out, **zero appearances, and two systems that could not be tested at all** — rendered as a figure in which the two untestable systems are visually distinct from the four measured zeroes. It is honest, it is first-party, it needs no permission from anyone, it is ready to publish today, and no competitor would publish its equivalent.

## 6. What must be protected during UX and design

**These are the things that will be lost first if the direction is handed to a designer without them. Each is stated as a rule.**

1. **The argument register leads every page, in plain commercial words, before any apparatus appears.** This is the mitigation for The Record's principal risk — becoming an academic paper — and for its weakest score, India-first register (3/5). If a first screen ever requires the reader to parse machinery before they learn what the firm does, the direction has been broken.

2. **The apparatus is never smaller-grey-italic.** Limits, scope notes and what cannot be attributed are set at the same type size and quality as the claims they qualify. Homepage Block 4 already prohibits a disclaimer treatment; it must be prohibited at the design-system level, not left to per-page judgement.

3. **The run stays a figure. It never becomes the brand.** No ambient route, no route in the header, no route as a background pattern, no scroll-driven route narrative, no tapering. The moment it tapers, it is a funnel.

4. **The status vocabulary never aggregates.** No coverage percentage, no evidence score, no summary badge, no traffic light. Four or fewer distinguishable states, each carrying a shape and a label as well as any colour.

5. **An untested system is never rendered as a zero.** Claude and Grok returned no testable result; they must be visually distinct from the four systems that returned a measured zero. **This is a design requirement, not only a copy one**, and it is the most specific honesty rule on this site.

6. **No proof container without proof in it.** No logo wall, no rating strip, no testimonial slot, no case-study grid, no metric tile — and no substitute placed in those slots. Six components in the existing codebase render exactly these containers (`03_COMPETITOR_VISUAL_PATTERNS.md` §7).

7. **Every explanatory figure has a complete static state and a text equivalent.** The figures are the assets most likely to be quoted, screenshotted or cited. If a figure only makes sense in motion, it fails.

8. **Colour never encodes a judgement.** No red-amber-green anywhere on the site, including in data displays. MappedSkills does not grade outcomes it does not control.

9. **Direct contact stays prominent.** Phone and WhatsApp visible per homepage Block 8, and genuinely prominent on the conditional industrial page per `PAGE_TYPE_CRO_RULES.md` §10. Editorial restraint must not quietly remove the most India-appropriate conversion affordance on the site.

10. **The reading column is protected.** A measured line length, generous leading, and a hard rule that no section runs past four paragraphs without a heading, list, table or figure (`VOICE_AND_TONE.md` §3).

**Added 2026-09-01 by the correction pass — five further protections, each closing a defect this stress test found:**

11. **Every page opens fast, and no page opens slow.** A page may have a slow middle; none may have a slow opening (`16` §4B).
12. **The argument register must be complete and confident read alone.** Hide every apparatus element — the page must still sell. The apparatus is pure addition, never a load-bearing half (`17` §1 Test 1). **This is the rule most likely to erode in execution.**
13. **Emphasis is commercial; presence is evidential.** The apparatus is everywhere and never the loudest thing. Emphasis order: buyer problem and its consequence → action → evidence → methodology (`16` §4C).
14. **No limit precedes the capability it limits, and no limits section is ever collapsible** (B28, B29).
15. **The proportion rule.** Gaps and limits must not out-weigh what the firm does and is accountable for — honesty without proportion reads as incapacity (B32).
