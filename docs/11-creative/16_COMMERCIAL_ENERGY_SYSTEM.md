# Commercial Energy System — T1 Refinement (APPROVED)

**Session:** 12 — Creative / Art Direction · **Orchestrator correction / stress-test pass**
**Date:** 2026-09-01 · **Branch:** `test_branch`
**Status:** **APPROVED — 2026-09-01 (Gate 9A, `DEC-015`).**

**Scope of this pass:** T1 (The Record) only. **The four-territory competition was not rerun. No fifth territory was created. No UX, wireframe, layout, mockup or style frame was produced. No approved page copy was modified.**

**What this document does:** interrogates T1's weakest score — **India-first appropriateness, 3/5** — finds the mechanism behind it, and specifies a binding refinement that makes The Record commercially energetic **without** weakening any evidence-led principle approved in principle by the orchestrator.

---

## 1. The verdict, stated first

**The 3/5 was correct, and the diagnosis behind it was incomplete.**

The original direction attributed the weakness to *register* — that an editorial-institutional look might read as Western and cool to an owner-led Indian buyer. That is a real risk, but it is the symptom.

**The actual defect is conceptual, and it is this:**

> **A record of a firm that measures things is a record of an observer.**
>
> MappedSkills does not observe. It **intervenes** — it finds what is broken, fixes it, and reports what changed. The territory as originally specified had a complete visual vocabulary for *measurement* and **no visual vocabulary for action at all.**

Everything that made T1 feel academic, institutional, NGO-like, passive or cold traces to that single gap. A site built entirely from findings, states, provenance and limits describes a world; it does not show anyone doing anything to it. Fixing the register without fixing the gap would have produced a warmer publication.

**This is a genuine contradiction in the original specification, not a softening for commercial taste.** Section 2 of the orchestrator brief instructs that approved principles be preserved "unless the stress test reveals a genuine contradiction". This is that contradiction, and the correction *extends* the evidence system rather than diluting it — because the strongest possible evidence a firm can publish is **a dated record of something it changed.**

## 2. The failure modes, named precisely

Twelve mechanisms by which The Record would read as a research publisher rather than a marketing partner. Each is a mechanism a designer can check for, not an adjective.

| # | Failure mode | The mechanism |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Apparatus-first reading order** | The eye lands on a date, sample or method before it lands on a commercial sentence. This alone converts a firm into a publication, regardless of styling |
| 2 | **No consequence** | Evidence answers *"is it true?"*. It never answers *"so what, and what will you do about it?"*. A site of pure findings has no stake in the reader's outcome |
| 3 | **No intervention** | The run as originally specified marks where measurement happens. It marks nothing where **work** happens. The firm appears to watch the chain rather than repair it |
| 4 | **Uniform temperature and weight** | Restraint applied evenly is flatness. If nothing is emphasised, nothing is urgent, and no reader is moved |
| 5 | **Symmetry between problem and apparatus** | If the buyer's expensive problem carries the same visual weight as a run-count note, the site has told the buyer their problem is not the point |
| 6 | **Everything at reading pace** | A record is read. A commercial site is **scanned, then read**. With no scan layer, the founder who has forty seconds receives nothing |
| 7 | **The apparatus is the most memorable thing on screen** | Then the firm is remembered as analysts. The apparatus must be the most *distinctive* feature and never the most *prominent* one |
| 8 | **People appear only as bylines** | A publication has authors. A firm has people who answer the phone. Author-scale portraits alone read as authored, not staffed |
| 9 | **Hedged states dominate** | Dashed lines, unmeasured markers and not-testable states accumulating across a screen produce a cumulative impression of a firm that mostly cannot do things. **Honesty rendered without proportion becomes apology** |
| 10 | **Price and contact given editorial restraint** | For an India-first considered purchase, price and phone number are **commercial information**. Treating them with the same quiet as a methodology note reads as evasive, not premium |
| 11 | **Limits stated before capabilities** | A page that qualifies before it claims has argued itself down before the reader knows what is on offer |
| 12 | **Archival skeuomorphism** | Cream grounds, faux print texture, newspaper rules, typewriter styling, marginalia-as-ornament. This is the name's fault and is addressed in §7 |

## 3. Refined creative thesis

> ### **THE RECORD — A WORKING RECORD, NOT AN ARCHIVE.**
>
> The site is the working record of a firm that **finds what is broken, fixes it, and records what changed**. It is kept by people who intervene, not by observers.
>
> **Commercial meaning leads every screen. The evidence stands beside it — always present, never in front.**
>
> Its energy comes from **consequence and contrast** — a problem that costs money, an action taken, a dated change — never from colour, motion, scale or claim.

The single word that changed is **fixes**. Everything in §4 follows from it.

## 4. The commercial energy system

### A. ACTION — how the system shows that MappedSkills acts

Six devices. All derive from approved material; none introduces hype, gradients, oversized metrics, aggressive CTAs, urgency, performance-agency tropes, SaaS aesthetics or stock imagery.

**A1. The intervention mark — a second mark class on the run.**
The run currently carries measurement points. It gains a distinct **intervention mark** meaning *work was done here*, tied to a date and a named owner. Where the annotation tie binds a claim to its evidence, the intervention mark binds a **state change** to when it happened and who did it.

This is the smallest possible change that converts the site's central figure from an observation diagram into a work record. It also makes the run harder to misread as a funnel, because a funnel has no repair points.

**A2. The state pair — before and after as *states*, not as metrics.**
Not a number pair. A pair of **conditions**, dated:

> *the form displayed a success screen and transmitted nothing* → *the form delivers, and delivery is verified*

**This is available today and requires nobody's permission**, because the subject is MappedSkills' own site. The approved `/research/{own-site-enquiry-diagnostic}` brief is exactly this asset — *"What it found, what was broken, what was fixed, and how long each part had been failing."* It is the firm's proof that it acts, and it was under-used in the original direction.

**Binding distinction:** a state pair is prose-and-mark, dated, on the firm's own property. It is **not** `BeforeAfterMetric`, not a percentage improvement, and never applied to a client without written permission.

**A3. Consequence carries the strongest treatment on the page.**
Where the approved copy names what the buyer's problem costs, that statement receives the page's heaviest emphasis — heavier than any figure, any number and any apparatus. This is the direct fix for failure modes 2, 4 and 5.

**A4. Verb-led emphasis.**
The approved copy already supplies verb-led headings — *"How the work produces an enquiry"*, *"Where enquiries get lost"*, *"Five checks you can run today"*. The design emphasises these over the noun-shaped ones. **No copy changes; emphasis is a design decision.**

**A5. Direction and arrival.**
The run reads with genuine direction and terminates in an **event**, not a node. The enquiry arriving is the one moment on the site permitted to feel like something happening.

**A6. Decisions rendered as the reader's, not the diagram's.**
The four causes, the five checks and the fit test are rendered as choices a reader makes, with outcomes they can record — not as labelled boxes they look at. A reader who *does* something on the page has met a firm that does things.

### B. PACE

| | Fast | Slow |
|---|---|---|
| **What it means visually** | Few elements per screen · strong type contrast · short line counts · immediate scannability · **no apparatus** · decisive CTA · quick vertical progression | Measured reading column · figures · apparatus adjacent · generous space per idea · no conversion pressure |
| **Where** | The first screen of **every** page · capability routing · `/pricing` · `/contact` · `/schedule-call` · `/thank-you` · the fit test · navigation between pages | `/how-it-works` · research entries · the problem page's diagnostic *(slow on purpose — the reader is working)* · the accountability-boundary section *(slow because precision matters)* |

> **The binding pace rule: every page opens fast. A page may have a slow middle. No page opens slow.**

That one rule resolves failure mode 6 and is the largest single contributor to the buyer-comprehension improvement in §5.

### C. EMPHASIS — the hierarchy

> **1. Buyer problem and its commercial consequence**
> **2. The action — what will be done about it**
> **3. The evidence that this is real**
> **4. The methodology behind the evidence**

This looks like it demotes evidence in an evidence-led direction. **It does not, and the distinction is the most important sentence in this document:**

> ### **Emphasis is commercial. Presence is evidential.**
>
> The apparatus is **everywhere** — that is what makes the site credible. It is **never the loudest thing** — that is what makes it commercial. Presence and emphasis are different axes, and the original direction conflated them.

A reader should be unable to find a claim without its provenance nearby, and equally unable to mistake the provenance for the point.

### D. CTA CHARACTER — how calls to action should feel

No buttons are designed. Six qualities:

1. **Definite, not loud.** Unambiguous edges, solid, high contrast against a quiet ground. **Energy comes from contrast against restraint, not from size, saturation or a coloured band.** On a page this calm, a small definite object is louder than a large one would be on a noisy page.
2. **A distinct object, never editorial.** On commercial pages a CTA is never a text link inside a paragraph. A reader must never have to *find* it.
3. **Isolated.** Its own region, with more space around it than surrounding content. **The pause before it is what makes it read as intentional rather than pushy** — this is how prominence is bought without aggression.
4. **Direct contact is a peer, not a fallback.** Phone and WhatsApp render at the same confidence as the form and booking — never as a smaller "or call us". Directly serves the India-first buyer and is required by `/` Block 8 and `PAGE_TYPE_CRO_RULES.md` §10.
5. **Consistent and unmoving.** The same treatment sitewide. Where a sticky CTA is permitted by the approved CRO rules, it is quiet, fixed and identical — never expanding, pulsing, animating or changing on scroll.
6. **Never inside an apparatus region.** No CTA sits beside a limits statement, a scope note or a provenance line. Selling next to a caveat weakens both.

**Prohibited, restated:** full-width coloured CTA bands · urgency · countdowns · "free" framing before the entry-offer decision · response-time promises · a second competing goal.

### E. COMMERCIAL PAGES — avoiding the research-institute read

The general rule: **on commercial pages the apparatus is never immediately visible above the fold.** It is adjacent-and-secondary or expandable, per `17_ARGUMENT_APPARATUS_HIERARCHY.md`.

| Page | What makes it read commercial |
|---|---|
| **`/services`** | Opens on the outcome, not the method. The run appears **after** the commercial statement, not as the page's entry. Capability routing is scannable and links out fast. Zero apparatus above the fold |
| **`/pricing`** | **The most commercial page on the site.** When the price is unblocked it is the largest typographic element on its own screen. Sparse, confident, no decoration — a decorated price page reads as defensive. Apparatus **deferred entirely** to "what moves the number". Booking emphasised, per the approved CRO rule that price-stage buyers want a person |
| **`/contact`, `/schedule-call`** | **Zero apparatus. Zero figures. Zero evidence.** The form or the booking surface *is* the page. Phone and WhatsApp at equal weight. `PAGE_TYPE_CRO_RULES.md` §9: the only job left is not to lose them |
| **`/lead-generation`** | The qualified-enquiry figure is framed as **what you are buying and what we will answer for** — a commercial promise-boundary, not a taxonomy |
| **`/google-ads`** | The fee-versus-spend boundary is an inherently practical, commercial diagram. This page should feel like a straightforward, competent supplier settling the question that causes most disputes in the category |
| **`/conversion-optimization`** | Opens on **the loss**, not the discipline. Diagnosis is the second move, not the first impression |

### F. PEOPLE — decision

> **Yes. Selective human presence is necessary, and the original direction under-specified it.**

The original recommendation — *highly selective, named people only* — is **retained unchanged in its constraints** and **strengthened in its reasoning and its placement.**

**The reasoning changes.** People are not only a trust signal (`POSITIONING.md` §5.7). They are the **staffing signal** that prevents the publication read. A publication has authors. A firm has people who will reply. Without visible people, The Record has no way to say *there is somebody here*.

**The placement changes — one recommendation, with its tension declared.**
The person who answers enquiries should be visible on the conversion surfaces, not only on `/about`. It converts *"a firm"* into *"a person who will read this"*, which is well matched to relationship-led Indian B2B buying.

> **Declared tension, for orchestrator resolution:** `PAGE_TYPE_CRO_RULES.md` §9 sets the proof requirement on `/contact` and `/schedule-call` to **"Minimal. Proof here is a distraction."** This recommendation argues a responder's portrait is a **conversion affordance rather than proof** — but that is an interpretation of an approved rule, not a right this session holds. **It requires orchestrator confirmation, and it is blocked in any case on the outstanding team/people owner input.** No other part of the People decision depends on it.

**All original prohibitions stand without exception:** no stock, ever · no smiling office teams · no handshakes · no headsets · no anonymous crowds · no "our team" without names · no image implying clients, offices, scale or headcount that do not exist · no photograph of anyone who does not work at MappedSkills.

## 5. India-first stress test

**Not** reduced to Indian colours, local motifs, stereotypical photography, skylines, flags or localisation gimmicks. Evaluated against the buyer the approved `ICP.md` §5 describes: a founder, owner, MD or marketing lead accountable for "the whole number", convinced by "plain arithmetic", with "low tolerance for jargon", who has been let down before.

| Question | Finding | Refinement |
|---|---|---|
| **Immediately legible?** | **At risk.** Only true if the argument register genuinely leads. Nothing in the original direction *enforced* it | **R1.** Every page opens fast, with zero apparatus above the fold on commercial pages *(§4B, and `17`)* |
| **Commercially confident?** | **At risk.** Restraint plus "we have no case studies" can compound into tentativeness | **R2.** Consequence carries the page's heaviest emphasis *(§4 A3)*; **R3.** limits never precede the capability they limit |
| **Requires design literacy?** | **Yes, somewhat.** The two-register system is a learned convention, and this buyer has not agreed to learn one | **R4. The argument register must be complete and confident read alone.** Test: hide every apparatus element — the page must still sell. The apparatus is pure addition, never a load-bearing half |
| **Could editorial restraint be misread as lack of capability?** | **Yes — the sharpest risk in the whole direction.** Against DigiMark's "11+ years / 100+ clients / 4.6★" and SEOTonic's $199 pricing *(both COMPETITOR CLAIM, observed 2026-09-01)*, a quiet site with no numbers can read as *new and small* rather than *rigorous* | **R5. Countable inventory facts.** Systems tested · runs published · prompts published · checks a reader can run · pages of published method. These are **checkable counts of things that exist**, not performance claims — the Our World in Data principle (R-02). They give the site quantitative presence with zero claims exposure, and are already licensed by `AI_SEARCH_LANGUAGE.md` §3. **Never styled as metric tiles, never animated, never in a heading** |
| **Could evidence language feel overly cautious?** | **Yes**, if hedged states accumulate faster than capability statements *(failure mode 9)* | **R6. Proportion rule:** on any page, the cumulative visual weight of gaps, limits and not-testable states must not exceed that of what the firm does and is accountable for. Honesty by volume reads as incapacity *(new anti-pattern B32)* |
| **Does contacting feel easy?** | **Must be, and was under-specified** | **R7.** Phone and WhatsApp persistent and at equal weight to the form, sitewide *(§4 D4)* |
| **Can price feel prominent?** | **Must be** | **R8.** `/pricing` is the most commercial page on the site; the price is the largest element on its screen; apparatus deferred *(§4E)* |
| **Does it feel like people who execute?** | **No, as originally specified.** This was the central defect | **R9.** The intervention mark, the state pair, verb-led emphasis and arrival-as-event *(§4A)*; **R10.** visible named people *(§4F)* |

## 6. What was NOT weakened

Checked explicitly against the orchestrator's approved-in-principle list. **Every item survives intact:**

credibility through visible checking · argument + apparatus as two registers · apparatus adjacent to claims · the annotation tie · dashed state meaning a genuine epistemic gap only · the run bounded as a figure · no funnel or taper · light-first · highly selective genuine photography · conventional illustration largely absent · explanatory figures as the main visual investment · no sci-fi AI identity · measured zero distinct from not-testable · no fake dashboards · limits at meaningful visual weight · the anti-pattern framework becoming binding.

**Nothing was traded for commercial energy.** The energy comes from three sources, all of them additions: **action** (a mark class the system lacked), **consequence** (an emphasis decision), and **contrast** (a pace decision). None required removing an evidence principle, and the intervention record *increases* the amount of honest evidence the site carries.

## 7. Territory name — decision

**The concern is valid.** "The Record" invites over-indexing on documents, paper, archival aesthetics, newspapers and research reports. Internal names shape implementation, and this one points at the wrong noun: a record is a *thing that has been filed*, which is the passive reading the whole of §2 warns against.

> ### **RECOMMENDATION: OPTION A — retain THE RECORD, with a binding implementation qualifier.**

**Retained** because fifteen artifacts, the handoff and `CURRENT_STATE.md` all reference it; renaming costs real churn for a name no visitor will ever see, and the strategic idea is unchanged.

**The qualifier, binding on every downstream phase:**

> **THE RECORD IS A WORKING RECORD, NOT AN ARCHIVE.**
>
> It is the record a working firm keeps while doing the work — current, dated, revised, in use. It is not a document, a publication, a periodical, a report or an archive, and it must not be styled as one.

**Banned readings of the name — these are the implementation failure modes it invites** *(now recorded as anti-pattern B31)*: aged or textured paper · faux print or letterpress effects · newspaper column rules and mastheads · typewriter or courier styling · archival or library motifs · file-folder, tab, index-card or dossier metaphors · rubber-stamp or annotation-as-ornament graphics · "issue", "volume" or "edition" framing · a serif-and-cream costume standing in for rigour.

**If the orchestrator prefers a rename**, the recommended alternative is **"THE WORKING RECORD"** — the same thesis, one word, with the corrective built into the name. It is a rename of the internal territory only, not a new territory, and would require a find-and-replace across `docs/11-creative/`. **Claude recommends Option A** as the lower-cost route to the identical outcome.
