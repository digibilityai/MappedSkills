## 2. Tier 1 — full specification

### F1 — The run
**Business question answered:** *If I am to judge you on enquiries, how does an enquiry actually get produced, and which part of it do you answer for?*

**Pages:** `/` Block 3 *(full weight, primary)* · `/services` Block 2 *(with the joins marked)* · `/conversion-optimization` *(conversion stretch emphasised)* · `/seo`, `/google-ads`, `/social-media-ads`, `/ai-seo` *(single segment emphasised, remainder recessive)* · `/how-it-works` §3.

**Why prose is insufficient:** the approved copy says so directly — *"Copy alone makes the reader hold six stages in their head."* The argument is about **the joins between stages**, and prose renders a sequence but cannot render adjacency and connection simultaneously.

**Minimum static version:** one continuous object; six approved points labelled; the accountability boundary drawn; unmeasured stretches dashed; at least one arrival with no upstream segment; the client-data segment marked as the client's. Complete and readable with no interaction, no motion, no colour dependence. A text or tabular equivalent conveying the same relationships.

**Optional enhancement:** sequential reveal on entering view, **once**, non-blocking, never scroll-scrubbed. Intervention marks resolving with their dates. Segment emphasis switching on capability pages.

**Evidence dependency:** **none.** It describes what is measured and where accountability falls — both already approved and published positions. **It does not depend on any owner input, client data or unshipped work**, which is why it is buildable first.

**What would make it misleading:**
- **Tapering** — it becomes a funnel, prohibited.
- **Rendering it as three services or a three-step process** — both explicitly prohibited in the approved Block 3 copy.
- **Implying a delivery process** — the real process is owner-blocked; a run that reads as *what MappedSkills does in what order* fabricates F17.
- **Drawing every segment as measured** — the dashed stretches are load-bearing honesty, not styling.
- **Omitting the unattached arrival** — this would state that all enquiries are attributable, which contradicts approved copy.
- **Per-stage colours** — it becomes a transit map, and a transit map of a marketing process is a funnel with better graphics.

### F2 — The four causes
**Business question answered:** *My traffic is not producing enquiries. Which of my four possible problems do I actually have?*

**Pages:** `/problems/traffic-but-no-enquiries` Move 2 *(full depth — this is the page's core value)* · `/conversion-optimization` *(deliberately shallower, visibly the same object)*.

**Why prose is insufficient:** stated in the approved copy — *"prose forces the reader to hold four branches in their head."* Branching logic is the canonical case where prose fails: a reader must simultaneously hold four alternatives and one ordering rule.

**Minimum static version:** four causes — Demand · Page · Path · Measurement — each with its distinct fix, and **measurement marked as the one to check first**, with the reason visible (without it you cannot tell which of the other three you have). Followable top-to-bottom with no interaction. Vertical form on narrow screens, never horizontally scrolled.

**Optional enhancement:** the reader records an outcome per branch and the figure indicates which cause their answers point to. **Ungated** — no email, no sign-up, no download, no score.

**Evidence dependency:** none. The four-cause structure is approved copy.

**What would make it misleading:**
- **Implying the four are mutually exclusive** — a business can have more than one.
- **Implying frequency** — no data exists on how common each cause is; any ordering that reads as "most likely" is an invented statistic.
- **Producing a diagnosis rather than a direction** — the figure narrows the question; it does not certify an answer.
- **Gating it** — converts the page into the doorway page Project Rule 10 forbids.

### F3 — The four AI layers, per system
**Business question answered:** *Do we show up when a buyer asks an assistant for a supplier like us — and how would anyone actually know?*

**Pages:** `/ai-seo` *(the page's central figure)* · `/research/{ai-visibility-baseline}` *(canonical, at full depth)*.

**Why prose is insufficient:** the approved copy names the reason — *"The whole finding would be invisible under any single score."* The finding **is** a shape: retrieval working while the three layers above it sit at zero. That relationship is the argument against a score, and a sentence cannot show a shape.

**Minimum static version:** four layers kept separate — retrieval · discovery · recommendation · citation — with MappedSkills' own result on each. **Six systems shown: four with a measured zero, two in a not-testable state that cannot be read as a value.** Frequency across runs, never rank, never order. Full provenance IMMEDIATE: 28 unbranded runs, four systems, one dated day, Pune, English, signed out. Readable in greyscale; not-testable distinguishable from zero **without colour**, in the figure and in its text equivalent.

**Optional enhancement:** switching the system under examination while the layer structure stays fixed — the variant-switch pattern from reference R-06.

**Evidence dependency:** **Session 04 first-party measurement only. No owner input, no client permission, no unshipped work.** This figure is publishable today and is the sharpest single differentiator the firm has.

**What would make it misleading:**
- **Plotting the two untestable systems as zero** — false. **Omitting them** — misleading by omission. This is the most specific honesty rule on the site.
- **Blending systems into one figure or one value** — destroys the only stable signal.
- **Any ordering, ranking or leaderboard** — order changed in every repeated prompt measured.
- **A trend line, arrow or change indicator** — there is one measurement.
- **Aggregating the four layers** — that is the score the strategy rejects, arrived at by the back door.
- **Any implication about Claude or Grok beyond "not testable"** — no claim of any kind is permitted about either.

### F4 — What cannot be attributed
**Business question answered:** *When you report where my enquiries came from, what will you actually not know — and what happens to those enquiries?*

**Pages:** `/how-it-works` §4 *(full)* · `/` Block 4 *(as a state on F1, not a separate figure)*.

**Why prose is insufficient:** Session 11's own assessment — **"the single item on the site most improved by being visual: it turns a list of caveats into a legible position."** A list of five limitations reads as five apologies; the same five shown as a bounded region of a known system reads as command of the subject.

**Minimum static version:** what is measured, what is not measured, and what cannot be measured, shown together at **true proportion**, with the boundary drawn as an edge rather than implied by a blank. Unattributed enquiries visible as a real category with real weight. Static, complete, greyscale-safe, with a text equivalent.

**Optional enhancement:** the boundary appearing as a state change on the run the reader already knows, rather than as a new object.

**Evidence dependency:** none for the categories, which are approved copy. **Any actual proportion shown for a client must come from that client's own data and be marked as theirs.** Where no real proportion exists, the figure shows categories and relationships, **not invented quantities** — see below.

**What would make it misleading:**
- **Illustrative proportions presented as real.** A figure showing "unattributed" at a plausible-looking size is a fabricated statistic in graphic form. If no measured proportion exists, the figure must not imply one.
- **Rendering the unattributed portion as a thin residual, "other", or omitting it** — it would state the opposite of the approved position.
- **Framing gaps as empty states, warnings, errors or spinner-like absences** — they are findings *(anti-pattern B22)*.
- **Placing it below a CTA, collapsing it, or setting it in a disclaimer treatment** — all prohibited.

## 5. BLOCKED — must not be designed

### F17 — The `/how-it-works` stage sequence
**Status: BLOCKED. No sketch, no prototype, no placeholder, no exploratory version.**

The real delivery process is an owner input that no Claude session can supply, and the page is BLOCKED for that reason. **A speculative process diagram would fabricate the exact content the page exists to prove** — and a diagram is more likely than a paragraph to be screenshotted, quoted and cited, so the fabrication would travel further.

The approved stage outline (*diagnose · instrument · capture demand · convert demand · measure and report · extend*) **is the architecture, not a description of what happens.** It must not be drawn.

**Unblocks when:** the owner supplies the real process, stage by stage, including what cannot be concluded from each stage.
