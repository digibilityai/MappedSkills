## 1. What was approved at Gate 9A

`17` §3 grants the homepage first screen a **single sitewide exception** to *no apparatus above the fold*:

> **ONE dated, checkable item, ADJACENT-SECONDARY**, clearly subordinate to the H1. **Not a badge, not a metric tile, not a counter.**

**Its stated purpose is specific:** the approved copy identifies the H1's own risk — *"judge us"* reads as bravado unless something checkable is immediately present.

**The exception exists to answer a rhetorical risk. It does not exist to put evidence on the first screen.** That distinction decides everything below.

---

## 2. Qualification criteria — what the one item must be

**All seven must hold. Failing any one disqualifies a candidate.**

| # | Criterion | Test |
|---|---|---|
| **Q1** | **It carries a date** | A reader can see when it was true |
| **Q2** | **It is checkable** | It names something a reader can go and verify — not something they must take on trust |
| **Q3** | **It is a record entry, not a value** | **It must not be renderable as a number set large.** If its content is essentially a figure, it is a metric tile with a date attached |
| **Q4** | **It survives the first-fixation test** | On the first screen, the eye lands on the H1. **If the item is the most interesting thing there, it has failed** |
| **Q5** | **It requires no owner input and no unshipped work** | Otherwise it does not exist at launch, and a reserved slot for it is a visible empty state |
| **Q6** | **It is one item, not a row** | A row of anything on a first screen is the credibility-wall convention the direction rejects |
| **Q7** | **It states something the firm did, not something the firm claims** | The item's whole job is to be checkable. A claim is not checkable; **a dated action is** |

**Explicitly disqualified by these criteria, and named so they cannot return under another label:** a stat card · a metric counter · a claim badge · a research promotion · a logo · a client result · a mini dashboard · a rating · a review count · a trust seal · an award · a "trusted by" strip · a countdown · a "last updated" timestamp on the page itself *(which is checkable and dated but says nothing the firm did)*.

## 4. The determination

> ### **LAUNCH DEFAULT: ZERO APPARATUS ABOVE THE FOLD ON THE HOMEPAGE.**
>
> The homepage first screen carries **four elements and no fifth**: the H1, its supporting line, and the two primary actions.

**Reasoning, stated plainly.**

1. **No candidate available at launch satisfies all seven criteria.** The only candidate that satisfies six is **blocked on a technical prerequisite**, and the only unblocked candidate fails on the two criteria that matter most — it is a value, and it reports an observation rather than an act.
2. **The orchestrator's instruction is explicit:** *if the item cannot satisfy the first-fixation test, the homepage should launch with zero apparatus above the fold.* The strongest unblocked candidate fails exactly that test.
3. **Launching the exception with a weak occupant is worse than launching it empty.** A metric tile with a date attached is still a metric tile, and it would put the site's most prohibited pattern on its most important screen — while the H1's rhetorical risk would remain unanswered, because a number does not answer *"judge us"*. **A dated repair does.**

**The Gate 9A exception is preserved and unspent.** It is not withdrawn, not reallocated, and not available to any other page.

---

## 5. What unlocks it

> **The exception unlocks when the own-site diagnostic publishes** — that is, when the technical prerequisite ships and the intervention record becomes real.

At that point the candidate satisfies all seven criteria, and it is the **strongest possible occupant**: a dated record of something the firm found broken on its own property and repaired. It answers *"judge us"* with an act rather than a claim, and it requires nobody's permission.

**Nothing else unlocks it.** Specifically, it is **not** unlocked by: a case study becoming available · a client result · a second research entry · a rating or review appearing anywhere · the passage of time.

**And when it is unlocked, all seven criteria are re-applied.** The unlock is a candidate becoming eligible, not a slot being filled.

---

## 6. Where the H1's rhetorical risk is answered instead, at launch

The risk the exception exists to address is real and does not disappear. **At launch it is answered below the fold, and quickly.**

| Where | What answers it |
|---|---|
| **Block 3 — the mechanism** | The reader is shown how an enquiry is produced, which is the substantive answer to *"judge us on the enquiries"* |
| **Block 4 — the boundary** | The firm states what it will and will not answer for, in the open, near the top of the page |
| **Block 6 — what can be checked** | It opens on the admission that there are no case studies, then lists only what genuinely exists |

**The cost, recorded honestly:** the first screen is now purely rhetorical, and a reader who bounces from it has been given a standard and no evidence. **That is a real cost, and it is smaller than the cost of putting a prohibited pattern on the first screen.** The forty-second test still passes, because it was never dependent on the first screen alone.
