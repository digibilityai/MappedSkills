## 0. What this master is, and what it is not

| It is | It is not |
|---|---|
| **One commercial page at one buyer intent** — `/seo`: high sophistication, long horizon, scepticism about outcomes rather than about measurement | A shape every commercial page is poured into |
| **A demonstration that the invariant spine (§1) survives a specific intent-driven arrangement (§2)** | A demonstration that the arrangement is correct for any other intent |
| **The inheritance point for `/seo` and `/lead-generation`** | The inheritance point for `/ai-seo`, `/conversion-optimization`, `/social-media-ads` or `/pricing`, each of which is a stated, reasoned variant (`37`–`40`) |

**`/lead-generation` inherits this master and departs from it in two stated ways** (`05` §4.5): **move 6 is the differentiator** — the published qualified-enquiry definition — and it **appears before the boundary**, because on that page the definition *is* the capability. Its F1 class is **C — STATE** at the chain's terminus, not B — PARTIAL. **Those differences are not defects in this master and must not be normalised away.**

## 2. INTENT-DRIVEN VARIATION — why *this* page is arranged *this* way

**The only permitted drivers are the four in `05` §3.** They are stated here in full rather than cited by code, because a code is not a reason.

| Driver | `/seo`'s value | **The structural consequence visible in the diagram** |
|---|---|---|
| **Sophistication** — how much the buyer already knows | **High.** Provider-seeking on `b2b seo` / `enterprise seo`. Knows the discipline | **The educational sequence is short.** MOVE 4 is **scope, month to month — not a primer.** Explaining SEO to this buyer insults them and delays the boundary |
| **Scepticism** — how much the buyer distrusts the category | **High, but about outcomes — not about whether measurement is real** | **Evidence is compressed, not early.** MOVE 7 stays in context as [EVD: EXPANDABLE] beside MOVE 6's claims. It is **not** promoted to a named section, which is what `/ai-seo` does for a different reason (`37`) |
| **Misinformation** — how much the buyer believes that is wrong | **Low.** The category vocabulary is stable | **MOVE 8 is a compact section, not the page.** Contrast `/ai-seo`, where objections *are* the page (`37`), and `/google-ads`, where one sentence suffices |
| **Dispute risk** — what argument this category usually produces | **Time.** The buyer's risk is twelve months of spend before anything moves | **MOVE 5 is elevated and carries the 12-month-plus horizon at full strength**, stated as a **horizon, not a schedule** — no month is promised. `05` §4.1: this is the single most important thing on the page for qualification, and the thing every competitor softens |

**Not permitted as drivers, and absent here:** visual novelty · a wish for the pages to look different · section-count parity · a designer's sense that the page "needs something" · commercial preference between capabilities.
