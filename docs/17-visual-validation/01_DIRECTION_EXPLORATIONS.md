## 0. What is shared, and therefore is not a difference

All three directions are **the same territory**. Each of the following is fixed by approved
direction and is identical in all three; none of them is a distinguishing feature.

Light-first, warm off-white ground, never pure white, never dark-as-brand · warm neutrals chosen
rather than defaulted · two registers, argument and apparatus, semantically consistent sitewide ·
the apparatus present everywhere and never the loudest thing · hierarchy from **type size ·
register · rule**, never from tint, elevation, shadow or radius · structure from rules and space,
not from cards · **the apparatus margin at WIDE, re-parenting inline at MEDIUM and NARROW** (this
is approved UX, `48` §1, and is not a design variable) · one accent that means *mark* · colour
never grades · a dashed line always means an epistemic gap · shape + label on every status ·
tabular lining numerals everywhere · explanatory figures as the principal visual asset class ·
photography highly selective, named people and real places only · icons functional only, never
per capability · motion only where it resolves an uncertainty.

**What differs is: typographic character · density · rule vocabulary · how evidence is
contained · how annotation is drawn · how figures are drawn · whitespace rhythm · how the
intervention is expressed.**

## 1. DIRECTION 1 — "THE PLAIN SHEET"

### 1.1 Core visual idea
**Rules are almost entirely removed. Hierarchy is carried by scale and whitespace alone.** One
type family does both registers, separated by optical size, tracking and colour value rather
than by family. Evidence has **no container of any kind** — it is a block of text at a shorter
measure, set below its claim, with a single short hairline as the tie. The page is very airy:
one idea per screen, very large jumps between the display size and everything else.

Figures are drawn thin and open, with generous internal space and few labels.

### 1.2 Why it supports commercial emphasis
Scale contrast does all the work. With no rules, no labels and no containers competing, the
commercial statement is the only loud object on any screen. It produces the fastest possible
first read, which is the fast-open rule (`16` §4B) taken to its limit.

### 1.3 How evidence appears
As plain prose in a second optical size, directly beneath the claim, at a shorter measure. The
apparatus is recognised by *position and measure*, not by any drawn device.

### 1.4 How it avoids archive / research drift
By having no marginalia, no rules, no columns, no serif and no cream. There is nothing in the
vocabulary that resembles a printed document.

### 1.5 How it avoids SaaS drift
Nothing is filled, rounded, elevated or tiled. There are no surfaces at all — only a ground and
type on it.

### 1.6 How it avoids generic-agency drift
Only by the presence of the apparatus. **This is the weakness**: strip the provenance text and
what remains is a well-set minimal page that any competitor could ship.

### 1.7 Primary risk
**The apparatus loses its identity and becomes small print.** With no drawn device, the only
things marking provenance as a different kind of content are size and measure — and size is
precisely the channel the direction forbids using for subordination (`08` §8 rule 1). The
direction therefore pushes, structurally, toward the disclaimer treatment B5 prohibits, arrived
at from the opposite side. It also fails the swap test most easily, which is the specific reason
territory T4 was rejected at Gate 9A.

## 2. DIRECTION 2 — "THE WORKING SHEET"

### 2.1 Core visual idea
**The page is a sheet a working firm fills in.** Structure comes from a **rationed rule
vocabulary** — a hairline that opens an ordinary section, a heavier rule that opens a set-piece —
and every rule carries a **lowercase register label in the apparatus register** immediately
beneath it, naming what the region is (*the boundary* · *how it is measured* · *what cannot be
attributed*). Those labels are the scan layer, bought with no card, no icon and no colour.

Evidence sits in a **rule-bounded band with no fill, no radius and no shadow**, whose left edge
*is* the annotation tie. Two type families: a sturdy grotesque for the argument, a compact
monospace **at small optical sizes only** for the apparatus. Density is medium-high and machined.
Figures are drawn like instrument scales — constant strokes, identical marks, labels hung off the
object — rather than like diagrams of a process.

### 2.2 Why it supports commercial emphasis
Three mechanisms:
1. **The register label gives the reader a scan layer** without adding a single persuasive
   element, which resolves failure mode 6 (*everything at reading pace*) directly.
2. **The heavier rule is rationed to set-pieces**, so emphasis is a scarce resource that the
   commercial consequence can take.
3. **The evidence band is visually closed and finite.** A reader can see where the apparatus
   starts and stops in one glance, which is what stops it competing with the argument.

### 2.3 How evidence appears
As a band beneath or beside its claim, opened by a mark-coloured vertical tie, set in the
monospace register at the **body minimum size** — smaller in hierarchy, never smaller in type
quality. Status is a drawn geometric mark plus a word. Provenance is a single dot-separated line.

### 2.4 How it avoids archive / research drift
The rules are **rationed and horizontal-only**. There are no column rules, no mastheads, no
running heads, no folios, no paper texture, no letterpress, no typewriter face, no file or dossier
metaphor and no serif. The register labels are lowercase and plain, not small-caps section
headers. The mono is confined to metadata at small sizes and never appears in a headline or in
body copy.

### 2.5 How it avoids SaaS drift
No fills, no rounded corners beyond 2px, no elevation, no tiles, no KPI panels, no chart
furniture, no gauges and no colour that reports a state. The evidence band is a **bounded region,
not a card**: it has one edge, not four, and that edge carries meaning.

### 2.6 How it avoids generic-agency drift
The register labels, the drawn status marks, the honest dashed vocabulary and the instrument-scale
figures are all specific to what this firm publishes. A competitor could copy the styling and
would then have visible, labelled, empty regions where the provenance should be — which is the
empty-container test working as designed.

### 2.7 Primary risk
**Rule overuse and register-label proliferation.** The vocabulary is generative, which is its
strength and its danger: a designer under pressure will add a rule and a label to every block,
and a page of rules with small labels is a spec sheet, or at the far end a newspaper. The
mitigation is that both are **countable** — rules per screen and label count per page are
checkable at review in a way that "does this feel academic?" is not. The secondary risk is the
monospace drifting up in size or out of metadata and into a credibility signal, which A14
prohibits.

## 3. DIRECTION 3 — "THE MARKED PAGE"

### 3.1 Core visual idea
**The annotation tie is promoted from a small motif to the page's primary structural device.**
Instead of rules and bands, every piece of evidence is bound to its claim by a drawn **bracket**
sized to the exact height of the block it holds — in the margin at WIDE, to the left of the block
at MEDIUM and NARROW. One type family, with the two registers separated by a very large optical
size difference and by leading rather than by family. Sections are separated by space alone.

Figures are drawn plainly and then **marked up** — the object first, the brackets and annotations
added over it, so a figure looks like something that has been examined.

### 3.2 Why it supports commercial emphasis
The bracket is a **mark, not a container**, so it is structurally incapable of out-shouting the
statement it points at. Nothing in the vocabulary can grow into a panel. The argument therefore
holds the page by default rather than by discipline.

### 3.3 How evidence appears
Inside a bracket. The bracket is the whole vocabulary: it identifies provenance, marks a limit,
and doubles as the focus indicator, which makes accessibility part of the identity exactly as the
approved direction recommends.

### 3.4 How it avoids archive / research drift
It does not, comfortably. Brackets in a margin beside running prose **are** marginalia, and
marginalia is the single most recognisable signal of a scholarly document. This is the direction's
core tension.

### 3.5 How it avoids SaaS drift
Completely and easily. There is no surface, no fill and no panel anywhere in the vocabulary.

### 3.6 How it avoids generic-agency drift
Strongly, on distinctiveness: no competitor in this category draws brackets. The mark is
unmistakable and would be recognised across pages.

### 3.7 Primary risk
**The bracket becomes the brand.** A single mark that structures every screen and every figure
stops explaining and starts signing — which is precisely the failure mode `22` §5 exists to
prevent for the run, and the direction spends its one permitted recurring signature on the run,
not on a bracket. A secondary risk follows from the same feature: because the bracket is the only
device, it must be redrawn for every context and every width, so its consistency depends on
per-instance judgement rather than on a rule.

## 4. Side-by-side

| | **1 — The Plain Sheet** | **2 — The Working Sheet** | **3 — The Marked Page** |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Type** | One family, optical sizes | Grotesque + mono, mono small only | One family, large optical contrast |
| **Rules** | Almost none | Rationed: hairline + set-piece rule | None; space only |
| **Evidence container** | None | Rule-bounded band, tie as its edge | A drawn bracket |
| **Annotation** | Position and measure | Register label + tie | The bracket, everywhere |
| **Density** | Low | Medium-high | Medium |
| **Figures** | Thin, open, few labels | Instrument scales, constant strokes | Plain objects, marked up |
| **Whitespace** | The main structural tool | Rhythm between rules | Between blocks only |
| **Intervention vocabulary** | **None available** | Native: a dated mark on a sheet | Weak: a bracket cites, it does not record a change |
| **Scan layer** | None | The register labels | None |
| **Behaviour at NARROW** | Unchanged, and undifferentiated | Band is a band at every width | Bracket must be redrawn per width |
| **Archive-drift risk** | Low | **Medium — the named risk** | **High** |
| **SaaS-drift risk** | Very low | Low | Very low |
| **Generic-agency-drift risk** | **High** | Low | Low |
| **Signature-drift risk** | Very low | Low | **High** |
| **Failure is** | Perceptual, hard to check | **Countable, easy to check** | Perceptual, hard to check |
