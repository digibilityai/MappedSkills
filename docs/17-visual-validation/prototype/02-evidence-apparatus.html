Evidence apparatus

The question this slice answers: can evidence feel like part of a commercially persuasive website without turning the website into a research paper or a dashboard?

The load-bearing decision: the apparatus is set at 16px — the body minimum — in a different register, not at a smaller size. Subordination is bought with register, measure and position. Never smaller-grey-italic (08 §8 rule 1, B5). Computed contrast of the apparatus ink on the ground is 8.77:1 (AAA).

Colour carries no meaning here. Every status is shape + word. The accent appears only as the annotation tie and on interactive controls.

Scope: this is the state treatment. It is not F3 (the four AI layers per system), which is a Tier-1 figure outside Session 14's four slices and is not designed here.

All content below is approved copy or first-party measurement. Nothing is invented.

specimen b — the boundary. always open, never collapsible

What we are accountable for, and where that stops

We are accountable for the qualified enquiry — one that is real, reachable, from a business context, with a stated need. What counts as one is published, and it is the number we report against a starting point we capture before anything changes.

Some enquiries arrive with no usable trail at all. Someone types your name into a browser, or calls, or was told about you by a customer. We record those as unattributed rather than assigning them to a channel — and a gap in the measurement never takes an enquiry out of the count.

We do not promise rankings, a position in AI answers, a number of enquiries, revenue, or a return on spend. None of that is ours to control, and a guarantee would be a claim about a business we do not run.

This region has no disclosure control. There is no state in which it is closed, at any width, in print, or with script disabled.
