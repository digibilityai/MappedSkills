# Why Session 15 Failed Perceptually — Session 15B — RECORDED

**Session:** 15B — Digital creative reset · **Date:** 2026-09-02 · **Branch:** `test_branch`
**Status:** **Owner verdict recorded. Not disputed, not defended, not re-measured.**

---

## 1. The verdict, carried as given

The owner viewed the rendered Session 15 homepage and said it **looks like a document, a journal,
a textbook**; that the design concepts feel lost; that there is effectively no animation; that the
experience is not vibrant; that it does not demonstrate the creative capability expected of a
modern marketing company; and that it feels **typeset rather than digitally designed**.

> **This overrides Session 15's own "no material drift" finding. The Session 15 visual design is
> NOT approved.**

**It is not defended here** — not with F2, not with F3, not with device budgets, not with
accessibility, not with the visual-weight rubric, and not with compliance to Direction 2. Those
remain useful constraints. **They did not produce the required visual experience, and a page that
passes every internal test and fails the owner on sight has failed.**

---

## 2. What actually went wrong, stated as a design diagnosis

The failure is not colour, not animation and not typography taken singly. It is one structural
mistake:

> ### **THE RECORD — and specifically Direction 2, "the working sheet" — was promoted from a credibility system into the visual GENRE of the whole website.**

Once the genre is "a working sheet", every downstream decision is forced, and each one was
individually correct:

| Session 15 decision | Individually defensible | Cumulative effect |
|---|---|---|
| One continuous ground, no surfaces | Avoids card/SaaS drift | Nothing on the page is an *object*; the eye has nothing to land on |
| Rules rationed to four hairlines | Avoids archive drift | No visual events across nine screens |
| Zero motion, proved sufficient | The static state must carry everything | Nothing responds to the reader; the page cannot be *experienced*, only read |
| Zero icons, zero imagery, zero cards | Every one is a real prohibition | Nothing but type remains, so type carries 100% of the impression |
| Apparatus register in monospace | Semantic, honest, AAA contrast | Monospace metadata beside a reading column **is** the visual signature of a report |
| Reading column protected at 66ch | Correct for readability | The page's dominant geometry is a text measure — which is the geometry of a printed page |
| Emphasis measured by type scale | Made F2/F3 decidable | **Optimising a page on a type-scale metric produces a well-set document** |

**The last row is the mechanism.** The measurement framework the programme built rewards
typographic hierarchy and penalises visual devices. A page tuned to score well on it will
necessarily converge on beautifully typeset prose. **The tests were passed. The thing they measure
was the wrong thing to optimise.**

## 2.1 The test that should have been run and was not

> **Would this design survive being printed and handed over as a PDF, essentially unchanged?**

For the Session 15 homepage the answer is **yes, almost entirely** — only two native `<details>`
would be lost. That is the definition of a page that is not digital. It is now a standing test in
this session, applied to all three new directions.

---

## 3. What is reset, and what is not

**RESET — the visual expression:**
Direction 2, "THE WORKING SHEET", is **no longer binding as the sitewide visual treatment.** The
Session 15 foundation is provisional and may be superseded in whole. No further page is designed
as a working sheet.

**NOT RESET — and still binding on every direction in this session:**
business strategy · positioning · IA · approved messaging · approved copy · UX architecture · CRO
architecture · CTA architecture · **evidence discipline** · measurement discipline ·
blocked-content rules · the F1 taxonomy.

---

## 4. THE RECORD, redefined

> **THE RECORD is a CREDIBILITY SYSTEM, not a visual genre.**

| It governs | It does not govern |
|---|---|
| Evidence and provenance | Every surface |
| Measurement and its limits | Every section |
| Accountability and boundaries | Visual composition |
| What can and cannot be claimed | Typography everywhere |
| — | Page geometry |
| — | Animation language |
| — | Visual atmosphere |

**"Emphasis is commercial. Presence is evidential" is read more aggressively from here on:**

> The visitor experiences **a highly capable modern marketing company** first.
> They then discover that this company is **unusually rigorous about evidence and measurement.**

Evidence is what a reader finds when they push on a claim — not the thing they meet on arrival, and
not the thing that sets the typeface for the whole site.

`docs/18-design-system/` is **unchanged and not deleted.** The rendered homepage stays available at
