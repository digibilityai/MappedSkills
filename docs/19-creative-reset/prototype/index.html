Session 15B — three digital creative directions

MappedSkills. Creative-direction prototypes, not homepages, not a design system. Each one preserves the approved H1, the approved copy meaning, the UX architecture, the CTA architecture and the evidence discipline. None of them is approved.

A — The Signal Field

Demand is a live field of signals. The site is the instrument that catches, routes and tags them — and you can close a gate and watch the enquiries stop.

Open A New tab

B — The Switch

The page has two states — the traffic view and the enquiries view — and the whole surface switches between them. The H1 becomes the mechanic of the site.

Open B New tab

C — The Trace

The site instruments this visit and shows you the record it kept — including, explicitly, what it could not see. The product is demonstrated on you.

Open C New tab
