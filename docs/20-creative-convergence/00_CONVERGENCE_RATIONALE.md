## 1. The owner decision, interpreted

> **“B feels like the right foundation, but A has the energy I want.”**

Read as: **the commercial sophistication of B, with the digital energy of A.** Direction B remains
the foundation and must still be unmistakably what you are looking at. Direction A is **felt as
energy**, never recognised as a second visual system.

**The convergence is not a collage.** Nothing from A's *appearance* was imported. What was taken
from A is **behaviour**.

## 3. What was extracted from A — as principles

| A's principle | How it enters B |
|---|---|
| **Signals behave — demand is active, not a picture of demand** | The traffic state is a real population of ~190–440 marks (capped). It is the same population that becomes the enquiries. Nothing is a picture of something else |
| **Kinetic hierarchy — type changes state** | The H1's emphasis is not a colour fade: a rule is **drawn** under whichever word currently governs, and it moves when the state changes |
| **Visible progression** | A spine persists across the three acts and fills 33 → 66 → 100 as the reader travels |
| **Reactive feedback** | The reader can run the transformation themselves, repeatedly, and the whole surface answers |
| **Stronger section handoff** | The hero's stage drifts up and fades as the reader leaves it, and a tangerine bar **passes across** the handoff band before the next line lands |
| **Loss is drawn, not stated** | Marks that are not reachable, that stall before completing, and that arrive unattributed all stay visible — they are never deleted from the record |

sticky elements, 0 continuously running animations.**

## 5. The convergence idea, in one sentence

> ### **Traffic is a field of identical, anonymous marks. Enquiries are a few distinct, named objects. The site's one move is RESOLUTION — the same population, resolved.**

This is why it is B and not a compromise: **the switch stops being a swap between two pictures and
becomes something that happens to a population.** B contributed the two states; A contributed the
insight that the objects should *behave*. The result is a single gesture, reused:

| Where | What resolves |
|---|---|
| **Hero** | An undifferentiated lattice → four named enquiry objects, one of them unattributed |
| **Capture** | Undifferentiated marks → what we can reach, and what is not measured |
| **Convert** | Undifferentiated marks → what completed, and what stalled short of the threshold |
| **Measure** | Undifferentiated marks → named sources, and one that stays unattributed |
| **The argument** | Five promises are struck through in turn; one commitment resolves in beside them |
| **The evidence** | A claim lifts; what it stands on resolves underneath it |
| **The CTA** | The surviving marks arrive on the tangerine panel — *this is where they land* |

**Nothing moves that does not represent something.** Every mark answers *“what does this
represent?”*: demand, an arrival, a source, or a gap in the measurement.

## 6. Hero behaviour

Approved H1 verbatim. Approved first-screen content only: H1, supporting copy, two primary
actions. **No evidence, no proof, no third CTA above the fold.**

- **On load the page opens on TRAFFIC.** Dark ink ground, the H1's rule under *traffic*, and a
  dense lattice of identical marks — activity, with nothing distinguishable in it. One brief
  ripple crosses the lattice, then it is still.
- **At 1.45s the same population resolves.** Every mark travels toward the object it becomes and
  goes; the ground turns to ivory; the rule moves from *traffic* to *enquiries*; four named objects
  arrive, one of them an open dashed mark labelled *unattributed*.
- **Then the surface is still**, and the reader has the control if they want to run it again.
- **The visitor understands the idea without interacting** — the transformation happens to them.

**On NARROW the timer is replaced by an on-view trigger**, because the stage sits below the fold
and a moment played to an empty screen is not a moment.

## 7. CAPTURE → CONVERT → MEASURE behaviour

**Not three cards, columns, icons, boxes, a funnel, a pipeline or a dashboard.** Three acts, each
of which resolves *once* as it is reached, sharing one mark vocabulary and one progress spine.

- **01 capture** — *"Most demand never reaches you."* Two marks resolve into reachable demand; three
  resolve into dashed, open marks labelled **demand we cannot see — not measured**. They stay on
  screen. Nothing is deleted to make the picture tidier.
- **02 convert** — *"Arriving is not the same as enquiring."* A threshold line is drawn — **the
  enquiry completes**. Two bars reach it. Two stall visibly short and become dashed.
- **03 measure** — *"Each one records where it came from."* Three marks gain named sources. One
  stays open and dashed: **unattributed — and never assigned to a channel.**

**No quantity is drawn.** Every mark field carries the standing line: *the marks are kinds, not
counts — no quantity, proportion or rate is drawn or claimed.* The vocabulary supports **measured
zero** (a measured result that is nothing) and **not testable / unattributed** (an honest gap)
because both are drawn as *shapes*, never as an absence.

## 8. Content-heavy section — the accountability boundary

The most content-heavy approved block on the homepage: four dense paragraphs about what the firm
answers for and what it will not promise.

**Transformed, not shortened.** *"Five things fall away. One does not."*
- The five refusals are listed at display weight and **a rule is drawn through each one in turn**.
  The rule is a **shape**, not a colour or an opacity fade, so every word stays fully readable —
  that was a defect found by measuring, and fixed.
- The one commitment resolves in beside them at display scale with the tangerine spine.
- **All three approved paragraphs remain in full, in a two-column reading layout below.** No
  meaning was removed and nothing load-bearing is behind an interaction.

The reader does not experience *"here comes another block of copy"* — they experience an argument
being made, and then the argument's full text.

---

## 9. Evidence behaviour — THE RECORD as a credibility system

B's relationship is kept: **the claim, and what it stands on.**

The claim is a solid ink block. *"Lift this claim — see what it stands on"* raises it and opens the
substrate beneath, where the six provenance rows resolve in sequence, the limitation is set in the
accent so it is not skipped, and both status marks appear — **measured** (filled square) and
**not testable** (hatched square), each with its word.

**No document margin, no constant metadata, no journal apparatus, no research-paper presentation.**
Evidence appears because a commercial claim needed support, and only then. The limitation —
*"No confidence interval is claimed and none is available"* — is the most prominent line in the
reveal.

---

## 10. CTA behaviour

Not a centred heading, paragraph and button. **A full-bleed tangerine panel that is the end of the
system:** the surviving marks arrive first — four solid, one dashed — then *"This is where they
land."*, then the two actions.

Primary remains `/contact` (*Tell us what you're trying to fix*); booking is the secondary peer.
Both are reachable immediately; nothing waits for an animation.
