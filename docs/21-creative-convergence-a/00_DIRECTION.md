## 1. The owner verdict, carried as binding

> **“Option A was much better than option B.”**

Session 15C therefore started from the wrong foundation. **It is superseded and is not defended.**
It is kept, unchanged, only so the owner can compare.

**The approach is reversed:**

| | |
|---|---|
| **Direction A — THE SIGNAL FIELD** | **the visual foundation** |
| **Direction B — THE SWITCH** | contributes **principles only** — sophistication, restraint, commercial clarity, state transformation, compositional confidence, static quality, claim/evidence |

**The formula is not "B + A energy". It is A's visual world + B's commercial sophistication.**
A already has the energy; none was added for its own sake. The task was to make A feel more
premium, more intentional, more art-directed and less like a technology demo — **without
neutralising what made the owner prefer it.**

---

## 2. What was preserved from A

Every one of these was treated as valuable and kept:

- **The dark, high-contrast world.** Still dark-first, still the ground of the whole page.
- **The signal metaphor**, and the sense that buyer demand is *active* rather than depicted.
- **Kinetic hierarchy** — the H1 carries the state, not a label beside it.
- **Movement through the commercial system** — a continuous field the reader travels along.
- **Visual progression** — three beats with a persistent step rail.
- **Strong visual events**, and the feeling that the site is alive.
- **Density without text-heaviness.**
- **An experience that cannot be a PDF.**
- **A memorable interaction** — the catch, and the ability to re-run it.

## 3. What was extracted from B — principles, not appearance

| B principle | How it enters A's world |
|---|---|
| **State transformation** | A signal does not get *replaced* by an illustration — the same object crosses a line and **becomes** a named arrival: colour, mark, label, ledger row |
| **Commercial clarity** | Every visual event carries the argument. The catch *is* "judge us on the enquiries"; the stalls *are* "arriving is not the same as enquiring" |
| **Static quality** | Every state resolves to a composed still. With motion off the page is a finished layout, not a waiting animation |
| **Restraint** | Two ambient regions on the page, not one per section. Long stretches deliberately do not move |
| **Claim / evidence** | The claim stands above; the surface **beneath it** is illuminated on demand. Evidence never sets the page's genre |
| **Compositional confidence** | Large decisive structures — one capture line, one horizon, one threshold rail, one full-colour close — instead of many small decorative parts |

## 4. What was rejected from B

**Not imported, deliberately:** B's ivory editorial surface as the site's ground · B's tangerine
CTA world as the page's identity · the Traffic/Enquiries toggle as the central device · reducing
motion to a minimum · turning signal behaviour into static diagrams · making the page primarily
typographic · flattening the visual experience.

**If the result looked more like B than A it would have failed.** It does not: the page is
espresso-black end to end apart from one deliberate full-colour close.

## 5. The refined creative idea

> ### **THE ARRIVAL — demand is light travelling across a dark plane. Almost all of it passes through. A few are CAUGHT — and the moment one is caught it gains a colour, a name and a place on a ledger. The website is the instrument that catches and names.**

This is A's signal field with a commercial reason for every state. **Nothing is a particle.** Each
mark is one of exactly six things, and each is drawn differently:

| State | Drawn as | Means |
|---|---|---|
| travelling | a bone streak with a gradient tail | buyer demand, in motion |
| passed the line | the same streak, dimmed to ~16% and fading | demand that never reached you |
| stalled | the streak stops and an **open square** is drawn where it stopped | it arrived and did not complete |
| caught | decelerates to the line, turns **amber**, travels to a ledger slot | an enquiry, recorded |
| tagged | an amber streak with a solid amber head | it recorded where it came from |
| untagged | an **open square** with a bone streak, never amber | arrived with no usable trail — unattributed |

**Every moving element answers "what does this represent?"** — and the two honest states
(*not measured*, *unattributed*) are drawn as **shapes**, so they survive colour removal and can
never be mistaken for a value.

### 5.1 How the AI/tech-startup drift was actually solved

Not by removing the field. By art direction, in five specific decisions:

1. **Temperature.** A's blue-black `#0B0D12` became **espresso `#0B0908`**, and A's cool light
   became **warm bone `#F4EFE7`**. This single change does more than anything else to move the
   world from "technology product" to "premium brand".
2. **One accent, and it means something.** A's mint-green + orange pair is gone. There is **one**
   accent — **amber `#FF8A1E`** — and it appears *only* where demand becomes commercially yours.
   Mint/AI-green, neon pairs and cyber palettes are absent by rule.
3. **No monospace anywhere.** A used a technical face for its labels. Monospace is the register
   that made Session 15 read as a document and makes dark sites read as developer tools. **Counted:
   0 elements using a monospace family.**
4. **Streaks in a masked band with a horizon**, not dots in a void. The hero field is masked so it
   *emerges from* the sentence describing demand; the sequence field is banded top and bottom. A
   starfield has no horizon; this does.
5. **Density down, presence up.** Fewer signals, longer and brighter, moving with intent. A tech
   demo signals effort by quantity; art direction signals it by control.

## 6. Hero experience

Approved H1 verbatim. Approved first-screen content only — H1, supporting copy, two primary
actions. **No evidence above the fold, no unsupported proof, no splash, no blocked interaction.**

**The field is not a background.** It occupies the band between the supporting copy and the capture
apparatus, so demand **travels out of the sentence that describes it** and into the instrument that
catches it. The capture line is a composition element — a 2px amber rule that draws itself down the
right column — and the ledger is the right-hand column of the hero grid.

**The first 5–10 seconds, by design:**
1. The H1 lands. "enquiries" is amber; "traffic" is recessive.
2. The capture line draws down.
3. Signals stream from behind the copy toward the line.
4. Four are caught, one at a time — each decelerates, turns amber, travels to its slot, and a named
   ledger row arrives beside it: *organic search · paid search · a page that answered the visit ·
   arrived with no usable trail — unattributed.*
5. **The field calms to ~28% and the label changes to "four kinds recorded."** Anticipation,
   release, stillness.
6. **"Run it again"** hands the interaction to the reader.

**The visitor understands the idea without interacting**, and the composition is complete when it
stops.

## 7. CAPTURE → CONVERT → MEASURE

**One continuous field the reader travels through** — A's strongest quality — with B's clarity of
transformation. **Not three cards, columns, icons, a funnel, boxes-and-arrows, a pipeline or a
dashboard.**

| Beat | What the field does | Copy |
|---|---|---|
| **01 capture** | The field runs across an open plane with a horizon and nothing catching it | *"Most of it never reaches you."* |
| **02 convert** | **Two thresholds appear.** Signals crossing them visibly **stall** — the streak stops and an open square marks where it stopped | *"Arriving is not the same as enquiring."* |
| **03 measure** | **A third threshold appears.** Survivors crossing it turn **amber with a solid head** — they recorded their source. Some cross and stay **open and bone** — unattributed. The field calms to 55% | *"Each one records where it came from."* |

**No quantity is fabricated.** No count, rate or proportion is stated anywhere; the hero's ledger
carries the standing line *"the ledger names kinds, not counts — no quantity, proportion or rate is
drawn or claimed."* **Measured zero, unattributed and not-testable are all supported**, because the
honest states are shapes rather than absences.

## 8. Content-heavy section — the accountability boundary

The most content-heavy approved block: four dense paragraphs on what the firm answers for.

**Transformed with the page's own metaphor, not with a new device.**
*"Five things travel straight past us. One does not."*

- The five refusals each carry a signal mark. On entry **the marks travel right and leave the
  frame** — they pass through, unclaimed. **The words themselves stay at full contrast**; only
  their signal departs. (An earlier version faded the words and was rejected in review: approved
  copy must never be pushed below readability to make a point.)
- Beside them, in the right column, the one commitment **resolves in** against an amber rule:
  *We are accountable for the qualified enquiry.*
- **All three approved paragraphs remain in full below**, in a three-column reading composition.

The reader perceives an argument being made, then its full text — not a wall of paragraphs.

## 9. Evidence — THE RECORD as a credibility system

B's strongest principle, executed in A's world: **the claim, and what it stands on.**

The claim sits in display type. Pressing **"Light it from beneath"** illuminates the surface below
it — a warm amber wash falling off into the lifted ground — and the six provenance rows resolve in
sequence, with the limitation set in amber so it cannot be skipped, and both status marks drawn
(**measured**, filled square · **not testable**, hatched square) each with its word.

**No document margin, no constant metadata, no journal apparatus, no audit-report or dashboard
presentation.** Evidence appears because a commercial claim invited inspection, and only then.
Limitations are explicit: *"No confidence interval is claimed and none is available."*

## 10. CTA

The one full-colour event on the page, and the end of the story the field has been telling: a
full-bleed amber panel where **the arrivals land** — three solid marks and one dashed — then
*"This is where they land."*, then the two actions.

Primary remains `/contact` (*Tell us what you're trying to fix*); booking is the secondary peer.
Both are reachable at any moment; nothing waits for an animation.

## 11. Colour, typography and composition

**Colour.** Dark-first, warm. `--void #0B0908` · `--void-2 #151110` lifted · `--bone #F4EFE7` ·
`--bone-2 #B3AAA1` · `--bone-4 #9C938A` for small recessive text · `--bone-3 #7C736B` for **shapes
and display type only** · **one accent, `--amber #FF8A1E`**. One controlled inversion: the CTA is
amber with dark type. No cyber neon, no gaming or crypto palette, no AI green, no gradient mesh.

**Typography.** **Familjen Grotesk** for display — contemporary, confident, and not a tech default —
and **Instrument Sans** for everything else. **No monospace at all**, which is both the
anti-document rule and the anti-developer-tool rule. Display type is large and integrated with the
field: the H1 sits upstream of the capture line, so the copy and the visual behaviour are one
composition.

**Composition.** Large decisive structures: one capture line, one horizon, one threshold rail, one
two-column argument, one illuminated substrate, one full-colour close. The type is never
decoration on a background, and the field is never wallpaper behind type.
