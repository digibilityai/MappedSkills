what we answer for

Five things travel straight past us. One does not.

  • rankings
  • a position in AI answers
  • a number of enquiries
  • revenue
  • a return on spend

We are accountable for the qualified enquiry.

Real, reachable, from a business context, with a stated need. What counts as one is published, and it is the number we report against a starting point we capture before anything changes.

Where you give us your close data, we report through to the deals and the revenue your own system recorded. Those are your numbers, from your system, and we state what we did and did not control alongside them.

Some enquiries arrive with no usable trail at all. Someone types your name into a browser, or calls, or was told about you by a customer. We record those as unattributed rather than assigning them to a channel — and a gap in the measurement never takes an enquiry out of the count.

We do not promise rankings, a position in AI answers, a number of enquiries, revenue, or a return on spend. None of that is ours to control, and a guarantee would be a claim about a business we do not run.
