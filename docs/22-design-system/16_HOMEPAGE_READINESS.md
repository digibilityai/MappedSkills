## 2. The five gaps

### G1 — F1 has never been drawn in THE ARRIVAL's materials · **SIGNIFICANT**
The homepage is the only route carrying **F1 FULL plus a STATE on the same instance**. `12_FIGURES.md`
specifies the materials, the prohibitions and the narrow recomposition, but **no F1 exists in this
direction** — the version that exists is in the retired light-ground system.

**Why it matters:** the field and the figure are different things (`12` §3) and they have never
been on the same page. Drawing F1 in these materials could reveal that they compete for the same
visual register.

**Why it is not a blocker:** every material F1 needs already exists — stroke weights, dashed = gap,
the amber intervention mark, the amber boundary, the recessive client-data stretch, the shape
vocabulary and the narrow-recomposition rule. **This is a drawing task, not a language task.**

**Do it first in the homepage session, before any other block.**

### G2 — The WIDE navigation dropdown is undrawn on the dark ground
The two-control rule and every behaviour are specified; the panel itself has only been rendered on
the light-ground directions. **Low risk** — it is a raised surface with hairline edges and the
established link treatment.

### G3 — MEDIUM has not been rendered for this direction
Specified in `13`, verified at WIDE and NARROW only. **The transition where the split regions
collapse but the horizontal field is still viable is the untested zone.**

### G4 — Three treatments specified but undrawn
The **symptom list**, the **capability routing list** and the **footer**. All three are plain
compositions of type, space and the existing surfaces; none needs a new primitive. **Low risk, but
they are three of eight blocks and should not be waved through.**

### G5 — P5, the always-open limits block, has no distinct drawn treatment
The 15D boundary section renders the argument (five depart, one stays) plus the approved paragraphs.
The homepage additionally needs P5 as its own always-open, never-collapsible block. **Its rules are
inherited unchanged; only its appearance on the dark ground is undecided.**

## 3. What is NOT missing

Recorded so the homepage session does not go looking for work that is done:

- **The visual language itself.** Palette, type, surfaces, primitives, signal semantics and motion
  are complete and internally consistent.
- **The hero.** Composition, apparatus, catch, pacing, ledger, narrow recomposition — all rendered.
- **CAPTURE → CONVERT → MEASURE.** Rendered as a pinned three-beat sequence with thresholds,
  stalls, tagging and the unattributed state.
- **The content-heavy transformation.** The departing list is proven, including the rule that
  approved copy is never dimmed.
- **The evidence moment.** Proven end to end.
- **The conversion moment.** Proven.
- **The motion budget**, which was the outstanding governance gap from three prior sessions.
- **Accessibility floors and the anti-pattern register.**

---

## 4. What the homepage session must NOT do

- **Invent a new visual language, primitive, colour, type role or motion behaviour.** If one seems
  necessary, that is a finding to report, not a licence.
- **Exceed the motion budget.** 2 ambient · 5 major events · 2 signature interactions · 1 pinned
  sequence · 1 inversion. It is already at maximum.
- **Reopen approved strategy, copy, IA, UX or the F1 taxonomy.**
- **Draw anything for a blocked slot.** The people element, the own-site diagnostic, the "what
  happens next" sentence, the phone/WhatsApp peer and the footer entity block all render **nothing**.
- **Fabricate a quantity.** `15_ANTI_PATTERNS.md` §Q.

## 5. Recommended order for the homepage session

1. **Draw F1 in THE ARRIVAL's materials** — FULL and STATE, WIDE and NARROW. Confirm the figure and
   the field do not compete. **If they do, that is a direction-level finding and must be reported,
   not designed around.**
2. Draw the three undrawn treatments (symptom list, routing, footer) and the P5 limits block.
3. Draw the WIDE navigation dropdown.
4. Assemble the complete page in the approved block order.
5. Count the budgets. Run the five standing tests. Render MEDIUM.
6. Re-run **F2 (first fixation)** and **F3 (visual weight)** — the mandatory Gate 10 re-tests
   carried forward from Gate 9B — against the rendered homepage.

## 6. Verdict

**The design system is sufficient to design the complete homepage.** Four of the five gaps are
low-risk drawing work. **G1 is the one that could return a real finding**, and it is first on the
list for exactly that reason.
