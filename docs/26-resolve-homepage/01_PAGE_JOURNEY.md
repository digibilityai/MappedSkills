## 2. Chapter by chapter — what happens, and why it is allowed to

### 1 — HERO. The population.
Opens **dark, in the TRAFFIC state**: an undifferentiated lattice of up to 440 identical marks.
After ~1.45s the same population **resolves** into four named objects and the surface **inverts to
light**. The keyword mark wipes from *traffic* to *enquiries* on the H1.

Four first-screen elements and no fifth: the H1, the supporting line, the two primary actions, the
PROOF offer. **No dated item renders** — the Gate 9A exception is unspent.

*Commercial reason:* the proposition, made visible. Traffic and enquiries are **the same demand**,
and the difference is whether anything is named. **This is the page's only T1 and its only
signature interaction** — the reader can switch back and put the report an agency usually shows
them beside the one we report.

### 2 — THE DEPARTURE. One mark carries on.
The resolved stage drifts up and fades as the reader leaves (scroll-linked, one rAF-throttled
handler writing two properties). A dark band passes a tangerine wipe left-to-right, a **single
tangerine square arrives**, and the transitional line follows it.

*Commercial reason:* continuity. Everything after this is **that mark**, at a different distance.
Without it, chapter 3 would read as a new section rather than the same demand moving on.

### 3 — RECOGNITION. The friction, and the alignment.
Three routes are drawn as runs of marks that get visibly different distances: three marks, six,
nine. Each ends in an **open dashed square** — nothing arrived. Then, after ~1.1s of stillness,
**the three outcomes travel into one column** and a rule is drawn through them, labelled *from the
outside, all three look identical*.

*Commercial reason:* this **is** the approved sentence — *"with no measurement in place, all three
failures look identical from the outside."* Three different causes, one indistinguishable result.
The run lengths encode **how far a route gets**, never how much demand exists, and the standing
note says so.

### 4 — THE RE-READ. Capture → convert → measure.
Not three cards, not three columns, not a funnel. **Six rows, read three times.** On entering view
the same six rows **travel to new positions and change state** as the question above them changes:

> `01 Can we reach the demand at all?` → `02 Can the enquiry actually complete and arrive?` →
> `03 Does each enquiry record where it came from?`

Each reading relabels every row. A row that was *"a page that answers what they searched for"*
becomes *"stopped before it completed"* becomes *"recorded — the page that answered the visit."*
The three approved steps stay visible as text throughout and light in turn.

*Commercial reason:* the meaning changed, so the population reorganised. This is the brief's own
example of experience-level motion, and it is the one place the visitor **feels** the difference
between the three states rather than reading three descriptions of them.

### 5 — THE DESCENT. F1, the run.
The figure begins at **0.44 scale and 0.42 opacity** — small, distant, unlabelled. On entering
view it **scales to full** over 900ms while the chain draws and the labels arrive. The reader does
not meet a new diagram; **the page gets closer to what it has been looking at.**

*Commercial reason:* the approved bridge sentence — *"What decides the result is usually the joins
between them"* — is a claim about **structure**, and structure is what appears when you get close
enough to see it. F1 answers the question the previous chapter opens.

### 6 — THE BOUNDARY. The deepest stillness.
The page's quietest surface, and it arrives **immediately after** the descent, **on the object the
reader has just learned**. The five unpromised things have a rule drawn through each in turn; the
one owned thing sits behind a tangerine edge. On the F1 instance above, the pre-boundary chain
**recedes to 34% opacity**, the boundary rule thickens, and two limit annotations appear.

*Commercial reason:* this is the **STATE** the F1 taxonomy assigns to `/` Block 4 — the same
object in a changed condition, **not a second figure**. Nothing else on this screen moves.

### 7 — CAPABILITIES. Routing at speed.
Three groups by contribution, one line per capability, a hairline per row, no icons, no cards, no
equal-weight grid. The only motion is the micro response on hover.

*Commercial reason:* Block 5 routes; it does not persuade. It is also the scanning mechanism for
the mid-funnel reader who came for a specific page.

### 8 — WHAT CAN BE CHECKED. Reader-driven.
Opens on the admission — *"We have no case studies to show you."* — at full display weight, not
shrunk and not tucked beneath the items. Then the measured claim, and a control that **lifts the
claim to show what it stands on**: what, when, where, how many, by whom, and the limits, including
that no confidence interval is claimed.

*Commercial reason:* the one place the machinery is allowed to be the point. It is left visibly
not-full — two checkable items, because two is what exists.

### 9 — FIT. Still.
Two positions at equal type quality. No revenue, turnover or headcount threshold is invented.

### 10 — THE CLOSE. Full-bleed tangerine.
The second and last surface inversion. Five marks arrive — **and one of them is the open, dashed
one**, because an enquiry with no usable trail still counts. Both surfaces at equal confidence.

*Commercial reason:* the culmination, not a CTA banner. The page finishes fast, in its own region,
with more space around it than anything preceding.

## What changed structurally

**The re-read, F1 and the entire capabilities chapter merged into one system view.** Those three
chapters were 3,518px and 454 words explaining one system three times; they are now 1,313px and 155
words showing it once, with three stages the reader can drive.

**The accountability boundary is no longer a second arrival on the figure.** The chain is complete
and static at all times, so Block 4 became a purely typographic chapter rather than a state change —
which is also what removed one T3.
