## 2. The two routes Phase J cannot honestly fix — owner decision required

`/google-ads` (run of 6) and `/social-media-ads` (5 text sections, no figure) are the worst
text-run scores among the capability routes, and **both are in that state because of a recorded
decision, not an oversight.**

### `/google-ads`

Its assigned figure **F9** — fee versus spend versus measurement — is blocked at the source. The
route file records it directly:

> *"Two owner inputs are outstanding and BOTH are respected here rather than filled … **F9 RENDERS
> NOTHING** and no substitute figure is invented for it."*

**Phase J will not invent that diagram.** The commercial fact it would draw does not exist in this
repository, and drawing a plausible one would be exactly the fabrication the programme's
non-negotiable rules prohibit.

> **OWNER INPUT REQUIRED:** how the management fee is separated from ad spend, and whether spend is
> billed through MappedSkills or paid directly to Google. **With that fact, F9 becomes buildable as
> a J7 STATE COMPARISON and the route's run of six breaks. Without it, the route keeps J9 only.**

### `/social-media-ads`

`22_F1_USAGE_TAXONOMY.md` §3 classifies it **D — NONE**, with the reasoning that it is *"the
shortest page in the set, with no page-specific figure at all"* and that adding the run would be
*"exactly the parity-driven padding the differentiation system forbids."*

> **OWNER DECISION:** leave it text-led with composition variation only *(recommended)*, or reopen
> the taxonomy. Phase J recommends **leaving it**. A short honest page is not a defect, and the
> audit finding it triggers is a **composition** finding, which J9 fixes without a figure.
