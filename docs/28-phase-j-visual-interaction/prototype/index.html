Phase J · design gate

Three prototypes, and the measured problem they answer.

Measured on the running application at 1425 px: 110 of 112 commercial sections (98%) begin at exactly the same left x-coordinate, 95 of 112 are text-only, and 16 of 18 substantive routes contain no interactive section at all. Each prototype below takes one real section, keeps its approved copy word for word, and changes what the reader has to do to understand it.
