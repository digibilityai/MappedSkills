AI search

When buyers ask AI, is your business in the answer?

AI tools like ChatGPT build their answers mostly from other websites: directories, review sites and lists. We work on those places and on your own site, then check what each AI tool says about you.

What’s changing

Buyers used to get a list of links. Now they get a shortlist.

How big is this? Our view, not a measurement: still much smaller than ordinary Google search. Worth getting right. Not worth betting everything on.

For your web team: the AI crawlers we check

Retrieval crawlers read your pages while an answer is being written: OAI-SearchBot, Claude-SearchBot, PerplexityBot. Needed, but not enough on their own.

Training crawlers decide whether your pages can train a model: GPTBot, ClaudeBot, Google-Extended, CCBot. Blocking one does not remove you from answers.

Which to allow is your decision. We set out the trade-offs. Names as listed in each vendor’s documentation, retrieved 31 August 2026.

Not tested: Claude (needed a login) and Grok (needed a sign-up; no answer). No data, so no claim, and not a zero.

Four results, kept separate, never one number. Can AI read the site? Yes, for the two crawlers we checked. Named when buyers didn’t ask for us? 0 of 28. Recommended? 0 of 28. Our site quoted as a source? 0 of 28 (Gemini showed no sources).

The directory finding: directories were cited in at least 10 of the 28 answers. One ChatGPT answer credited the agencies it named to two directories. When we measured, we had a profile on neither.

Asked about us by name: 7 of 8 answers described us substantially accurately.

What this does not show: a trend (it is one round, on one day); anything about Claude or Grok; signed-in answers, other places or languages; why we were absent (consistent with missing directory profiles, not proof of it); whether AI answers bring enquiries.

How we measured: one run is one question that does not name us, asked once in one AI tool, on 31 August 2026, from one location, in English, signed out. On a fourth Google query, no AI Overview appeared, so three are counted. Perplexity and Gemini were used signed out, which gives shorter answers. Crawler check: we requested our homepage identifying as OAI-SearchBot and as ClaudeBot (the names, not the companies’ own crawlers); both were served the full page, and our robots.txt blocked no AI crawler. The other crawlers were not checked. Counts, not rates. No confidence interval is claimed.

Questions

What people ask us

  • Everyone sells this now. What makes you different?

    We start by finding out which sites your buyers’ AI answers are built from, and whether you’re on them. Then we work on those places, not only your website.

  • Ahrefs and Semrush give me a checker for free.

    They’re worth running. A checker shows whether you appear, and some paid versions also show which sites the answers cite. Different tools answer different questions. A report tells you where you stand. Getting listed on those sites, and keeping your details right, is the work we do.

  • Can you get us into ChatGPT?

    Nobody can honestly promise that. We fix your business facts, work on the sites answers are built from, check whether anything changes, and tell you when it hasn’t.
