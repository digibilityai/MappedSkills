4. The redesigned sections

A. Industry story: replaces the self-test as the evidence section

Customer should understand: people like my customers already ask AI for suppliers, brands and projects, and AI answers with a few names. Visual: four beats per industry (question, answer, a few names, "And you?"). The last tile is the only ink tile and carries the orange dashed slot. Why clearer: the visitor sees their own kind of question, not our test. Leads to: "Will your business be considered?" in section 4.

Behaviour: plays once for Manufacturing (2 s per beat). Choosing an industry stops it and shows that industry complete. Without JavaScript all four industries are shown.

D. Scenario: replaces "Worth doing now, or later?"

Customer should understand: AI builds its answer from what it can find, so clear information about a business matters. 8. Governance and factual checks

DEC-015 (Creative direction, "The Record")

  • "Evidence is never removed to make the site feel commercial": the full dated record stays on the page in a native disclosure, with the table, the not-tested note, the four separate results, the directory finding and the limits. The text is unchanged in substance. Nothing is deleted or reworded to soften it.
  • "Apparatus may be subordinate in prominence but never downgraded in typographic quality": the record uses the page's own type and disclosure styling, with a normal H2. It is quieter only by position and by being closed.
  • "Measured zero is visually distinct from not-testable" is preserved inside the record (0 appeared; Claude and Grok shown as not tested).
  • "Commercial meaning leads every screen": R3 moves toward this.
  • "No fake dashboards" and "no proof container without proof": the example tracking table and the Business A/B cards are labelled illustrations, in words, with no numbers or scores. Interpretation needing approval (AX-R3-D12).
  • "Dashed always means a genuine epistemic gap": dashed is used for "You?" (unknown) and for Business B's missing information. The second is a fictional gap, so this is an interpretation (AX-R3-D12).
  • Approval needed: the relocation of the self-test from a headline section to a closed disclosure is a change of prominence the owner must approve (AX-R3-D03).

Disclosures that must remain

  • All "illustration, not real data" labels (hero, industry, scenario, reporting).
  • The full record and its limits ("What this does not show", method, crawler check, untested tools).
  • "AI answers can vary" beside the reporting story, and FAQ Q3 ("Nobody can honestly promise that").
