G. Audit against the eight rules (whole page)

1 Follow Brand DNATHE RESOLVE tokens only (ground, paper, ink, tangerine), Bricolage Grotesque and Manrope, light-first with one dark stage and one orange CTA band. CTAs go to /contact, with booking second. No invented proof or decorative numbers.
2 Sections not loaded with textNo paragraph is over 25 words (the live page has 7). Longest block: 24 words. Readable words: 525 (live 568), although the page now also carries picture labels and a "What happens next" row.
3 No jargonThe page never uses funnel, CTA, UX, A/B test, heatmap or friction. "Conversion optimisation" appears once, explained in the next words.
4 Keep text shortEvery journey step is one line. Paragraphs replaced: hero art caption, brief, tab paragraphs, threshold paragraph and band, proof paragraph.
5 No double or long dashesChecked by script on the rendered page: long dashes 0, double hyphens 0.
6 Every section tells a storyEach section has its own arc (see Architecture); no two sections repeat the drop-off story.
7 Journeys auto-progress, 2 sSix sequences. Step 1 is visible at once; then steps follow every 2 s once the block is in view. Each plays once and stops. Measured (below).
8 Dual-colour tilesEvery tile is cream (ink frame) or ink. Tangerine marks only the event inside a tile. Dashed frame = a genuine gap. (CO-D10)
