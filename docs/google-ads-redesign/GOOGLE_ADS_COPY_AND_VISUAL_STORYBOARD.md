### Block 2 · What we look at before suggesting Google Ads

**Heading:** **What we look at before suggesting Google Ads.**
**Lead-in:** Google Ads isn't right for every business. These are the questions we go through with you.

| Question | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| **Are people already searching for what you sell?** | Google Ads reaches people who are searching. If few are, it may not be the right way to reach them. |
| **What is a new customer worth to you?** | This shapes how much it makes sense to spend to win one. |
| **Can people easily enquire on your website?** | Ads send people to a page. If they can't enquire there, the budget goes to waste. |
| **Can your business answer enquiries when they arrive?** | An enquiry only helps if someone responds to it. |
| **Can your enquiries be tracked?** | Tracking lets us learn which searches bring genuine enquiries. How far it can go depends on your website and how people contact you. |
| **What else could reach the same people?** | Sometimes SEO or another channel fits better. See [SEO](/seo) and [social media ads](/social-media-ads). |

**Closing line (OD-09):** **There's no score. We talk these through with you before we recommend Google Ads, including when something else may suit you better.**

### Block 3 · From budget to enquiry (Path Figure)

**Heading:** **From your budget to an enquiry — and where it can go wrong.**
**Lead-in:** Google Ads reports what it's set up to record. That isn't always the same as a genuine enquiry.

**Five steps:** 1 **Your budget** · 2 **Someone searches and clicks** · 3 **They visit your website** · 4 **They enquire** · 5 **We learn what worked, and decide what to change**

**Two rails:**
- **What Google Ads can record:** ad shown · click · a recorded action *(such as a form being sent or a button pressed)*. Marks run to step 4 **only where tracking is set up**.
- **What your business needs to know:** whether each recorded action was a **genuine enquiry** — a real person, reachable, asking about your work.

**The one use of the word "conversion" (OD-11):** *Google Ads calls a recorded action a "conversion".* Everywhere else the page says **recorded action**, including in screen-reader text equivalents.

**"What if…" selectors:**

| Selector | Result shown | Who acts | Marker |
|---|---|---|---|
| …the wrong people click | Clicks are recorded, but few are people you'd want to hear from. We review the searches and change your campaigns. | **We manage this** (core service) | Ticked box |
| …the page doesn't answer them | People arrive and leave. We review the page and tell you what we find and what we'd change. | **We find and recommend** (oversight) → **changing the page is agreed separately** | Ticked box, then dashed box |
| …a recorded action isn't a genuine enquiry | Google Ads records an action that, on inspection, was spam, a mistake or someone with no real interest. We tell you what we find and what needs fixing. | **We find and recommend** (oversight) → **tracking repairs are agreed separately** | Ticked box, then dashed box |

**Closing line:**
> So we look past the report to the enquiries your business receives, as far as your tracking allows. Not every enquiry can be traced to an ad.

### Block 4 · What the work covers

**Heading:** **What the work covers.**
**Sub-line:** Management and oversight are covered by our management fee. Anything we'd build or fix on your website or tracking is agreed separately.

**1 · Core service — we run your Google Ads** *(ticked)*
- Set up and manage your Google Ads
- Write and improve your ads
- Manage your advertising budget
- Review search performance and improve your campaigns

**2 · Oversight — we look beyond the click** *(ticked)*
- Review the page people land on
- Check whether visitors can make an enquiry
- Spot problems with your website, enquiry route or tracking
- Explain what we find and recommend improvements

**3 · Agreed separately** *(dashed; empty boxes)*
- Creating or changing landing pages
- Fixing your website or enquiry forms
- Setting up new tracking, or repairing it

> If we find a problem, we'll explain what needs fixing. Any additional work is agreed with you separately.

### Block 5 · What we answer for

**Heading:** **What we answer for.**
**Lead-in:** You pay Google for the clicks. You pay us to run the work well. These are ours to get right.

1. **Your budget.** We manage it, and adjust your campaigns when the results call for it. *(OD-06)*
2. **A starting point.** Before we make significant changes, we record your starting performance, where it's available. *(OD-07)*
3. **Your website and enquiry route.** We review them and recommend improvements. Making the changes is agreed separately. *(F-2)*
4. **Clear reasons.** We explain significant changes and why we made them. *(OD-05)*
5. **Straight answers.** If results disappoint, we tell you and recommend what to change. *(OD-05)*
6. **A monthly review.** A performance report and a review call every month. We use what they show to decide what to do next. *(OD-03, OD-04)*

**One qualification (no contractions):**
> We cannot promise a number of enquiries or what each will cost. Your market, prices, competitors and how quickly you answer all play a part.

**Fee line:** Our management fee is separate from your ad spend. What it depends on is on the [pricing page](/pricing). *(OD-02)*

### Block 6 · What you learn each month

**Heading:** **What you learn each month, and what we do with it.**
**Lead-in:** Each month you get a report and a review call. Where the data allows, it covers:
1. **Which campaigns brought enquiries**
2. **Which searches brought them**
3. **How that compares with the starting point we recorded**

**Findings → decisions** *(labelled "For example")*:

| What we find | What we do |
|---|---|
| Some searches bring enquiries | We look at why, and decide whether they deserve more of the budget. |
| Some searches bring clicks but few enquiries | We check whether they're the right searches, and decide whether to change or stop them. |
| A page gets visits but no enquiries | We look at what it asks people to do, and recommend changes. Making them is agreed separately. |

**Honest measurement (no contractions):**
> **Not every enquiry can be traced to an ad.** A phone call after someone saw your ad is a common example. Where we cannot tell, we say so. We do not claim those enquiries, and we do not hide them.

**Link:** How we measure, including what can't be traced → `/how-it-works` · **Related (retained):** if the clicks arrive and the enquiries do not · the whole system · the enquiries themselves.

### Block 7 · Next step

**Heading:** **Already running Google Ads, or thinking about it?**
- **Already advertising?** Tell us what you spend, what you're getting, and whether you can tell which enquiries came from it.
- **Thinking about it?** Tell us what you sell and what one new customer is worth to you.

**Six compact steps** *(wording reuses the live How It Works and FAQ copy)*:

| # | Step | One line |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Get in touch** | You send a message. We reply by email or WhatsApp. |
| 2 | **Discovery call** | We learn what you sell, who buys it and what you want more of. |
| 3 | **Audit** | We review what we can learn about your business before recommending next steps. |
| 4 | **Proposal** | The work we propose, described so you can judge it. |
| 5 | **Your approval** | Agreed work starts after you approve. |
| 6 | **Work begins** | We start the agreed work. |

**One line beneath:** You see our findings, a recommended plan and a proposal before any work starts. *(OD-08)*

**CTAs:** **Tell us what you're trying to fix** → `/contact` · **Book a call** → `/schedule-call` (see §8).
