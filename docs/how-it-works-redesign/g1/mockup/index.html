No mystery about what happens next.

From the first conversation to the monthly review, here is how working with us runs. You see the plan before any work starts.

Finding priorities

Not everything needs fixing.

An audit usually finds more than is worth doing. Problems that cost you enquiries come first. Things that only look dated can wait.

What the audit found, in the order we found it.

    Fix first

    Costing you enquiries now.

    • The contact form fails on some phonesSome people who try to reach you never do.
    • Nobody can see which enquiries came from whereWithout it, no later change can be judged.

    Later

    Worth doing once the basics work.

    • The page for your main town is hard to find on GoogleMore of the right visitors, once the form works.
    • The services page doesn’t say which areas you coverFewer enquiries from outside your area.

    Not worth doing

    Costs money, changes little.

    • The homepage looks a few years oldTrue, but it isn’t what is losing enquiries.
    • There is no social media accountNot where people look for a renovation company.

    Example findings. Real findings, and how we weigh them, depend on your business. Illustration.

    If we don’t think something suits your business, we’ll say so.

    Doing the work

    We do the work. You stay in the loop.

    Once you approve the plan, we handle most of the agreed work, with your input where needed. What’s included depends on your service. Some work, like website or tracking changes, may be agreed separately.

    What we’ll need from you

    • AccessTo your website, accounts and wherever enquiries arrive.

    • What you knowWho your customers are and what they ask before they buy.

    • Quick decisionsWork that waits weeks for a yes loses momentum.

    • Replies to enquiriesThat part is yours, and it matters most.

    Keeping you informed

    Every month, you see what changed.

    Before we change anything, we note where things stand. Each month we compare against that starting point, where the data allows.

    Every month

    A monthly performance report, and a review call where your service includes one.

    What the report covers depends on your service and on the data available.

    What the numbers can’t always show

    Tracking helps, but it is never complete. We point out the gaps instead of hiding them.

    • Phone, then laptopSomeone who browses on a phone and enquires on a laptop can look like two different people.

    • Tracking declinedIf a visitor says no to tracking, we respect it. Their enquiry arrives without a source.

    • Calls, emails and shared linksThese often arrive with no record of how the person found you.

    • Sales and revenueWe only report these when you share figures from your own system.

    • Small numbersWhen the numbers are too small to tell, we won’t claim a change caused a result.

    A missing source doesn’t make an enquiry any less real. We report the gap, not a tidier number.

    Questions

    What people ask us

    • Do I have to commit before I see a plan?

      No. You’ll see our findings, a plan and a proposal first. Nothing starts until you say yes.

    • How soon will we see results?

      It depends on your service and your market, so we won’t promise a date. We note where you’re starting from, so you can see what changes each month.

    • Can you guarantee more enquiries or sales?

      No. We can’t control your prices, your sales team or your market. We do the work we agreed and show you what changed.

    • Who replies to the enquiries?

      Your team does. Replying quickly is the one part only you can do.

    • Do you do all the work, or do we?

      We do most of it. From you, we’ll need access to your accounts, a little of your time and quick decisions. Some work, like website changes, may be agreed separately.
