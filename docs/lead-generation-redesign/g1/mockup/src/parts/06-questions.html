Questions

What people ask us

  • Lead gen agencies sell rubbish leads.

    It happens, and the mechanism is simple: when the contract is written on volume, volume is what arrives. Our alternative is to reach the people you want and make it easy to enquire, so enquiries go straight to your team.

  • How many enquiries will we get?

    We don’t know, and neither does anyone quoting you a number. We capture a starting point first, so whatever happens next is measurable rather than arguable.

  • Will you call our leads to qualify them?

    No. We don’t make qualification calls. Enquiries go straight to your team, and your sales team handles the sales conversation.
