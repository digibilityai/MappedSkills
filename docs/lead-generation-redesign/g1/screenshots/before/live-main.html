What counts as a qualified enquiry

The standard we are introducing: confirmed by the prospect and checked by us before it reaches your sales team.

A verification standard we are introducing for client campaigns:

  1. 1

    Captured and screened

    A working contact. Spam and repeat enquiries are kept out.

  2. 2

    Eligible for you

    Checked against criteria agreed with you for this campaign.

  3. 3

    Confirmed by the prospect

    They confirm their requirement by email reply, or in a WhatsApp conversation they start.

  4. 4

    Reviewed by us

    A MappedSkills operator checks the evidence. No calls.

  5. 5

    Handed to your sales team

    Only qualified enquiries go to your sales process.

No confirmation, no qualified status.

Where each enquiry stands

  • Qualified

    Eligible, confirmed and reviewed. Handed to your sales process.

  • Pending verification

    Eligible, waiting for the prospect to confirm.

  • Expired — unverified

    Not confirmed within the window — 48 hours in the pilot. Kept separate; not sent to sales.

  • Unqualified

    Does not meet the criteria agreed with you.

  • Duplicate

    Repeats an enquiry already open.

What we answer for

We are accountable up to the qualified enquiry.

  • We don’t score enquiries.

    A score needs past outcomes to calibrate against. Without them, the weights would be invented.

  • Every enquiry should be accounted for.

    Even when its source is unknown.

We do not promise

  • a number of enquiries per month

  • a conversion rate

Questions

What people ask us

  • Lead gen agencies sell rubbish leads.

    It happens, and the mechanism is simple: when the contract is written on volume, volume is what arrives. The standard we are introducing is the alternative: an enquiry confirmed by the prospect and checked against criteria agreed with you before it reaches your sales team.

  • How many enquiries will we get?

    We don’t know, and neither does anyone quoting you a number. We capture a starting point first, so whatever happens next is measurable rather than arguable.

  • Will you call our leads to qualify them?

    No. We don’t make qualification calls. Under the standard we are introducing, the prospect would confirm digitally, and your sales team would handle the sales conversation.
